## 幻灯片 2：词卡：perilous

> 原图：`docs/ref/ppt/54e96fa342c79306197e56ef0430c197.png`

Perilous signals real danger

## perilous

/'per.əl.əs/ adjective

extremely dangerous or full of serious risk

Strong meaning: more serious than difficult

∇ Avoid using perilous for a late bus, a difficult test, or a minor inconvenience.

![](images/04ab4bfc20888b24391ba815d4e6964860810247445938c233c6417c1f2bea58.jpg)

![](images/54fda8f2140114f1371891797ecb3b3bc1799f7da35fdfa1a6ecc22789b3c1a5.jpg)

COMMON COMBINATIONS a perilous journey a perilous situation périlous weather conditions a perilous mountain path we were rafting in the river strong swell/current slap onto our raft, then we were flipped upside down, so we were in a perilous situation.

## BEST SITUATION

A storm, an accident, getting lost, extreme weather, or a journey with genuine danger.

## EXAMPLE

We found ourselves in a perilous situation when the storm cut off the only path back.

## 幻灯片 5：词卡：troubled

## Troubled describes a period or person with serious problems

## troubled

/'trab.əld/ adjective

having serious problems, worries or difficulties

For a temporary event, difficult or stressful often sounds more natural.

COMMON COMBINATIONS

a troubled period

a troubled relationship

a troubled teenager

look deeply troubled

BEST SITUATION

Family conflict, emotional stress, a difficult period, or someone who appears worried.

EXAMPLE

Ithappened during a troubled period when

was under a lot of pressure at work.

## 幻灯片 6：词卡：promising

> 原图：`docs/ref/ppt/9cdd5fc60b18f323a19f52fbab8d2770.png`

## Promising suggests a good chance of success

## promising 前程似锦的

/'prpm.I.sIn/ adjective

showing signs that something may succeed

Promising does not mean certain. It means the early signs look positive.

COMMON COMBINATIONS

a promising start

a promising opportunity

a promising sign

a promising solution

## BEST SITUATION

An interview, a new plan, early progress, an opportunity, or a solution that may succeed.

## EXAMPLE

The interview had a promising start because the manager responded positively to my ideas.

## favourable

/'fei.vər.ə.bəl/ adjective

helpful, positive or likely to bring a good result

BrE: favourable AmE: favorable

Use it for circumstances or results, not simply for a happy person.

tell sb。sth。

COMMON COMBINATIONS favourable conditions a favourable response a favourable outcome a favourable impression

## BEST SITUATION

Good weather, helpful timing, positive feedback, or a result that benefits you.

## EXAMPLE

The weather conditions were favourable, so we decided to continue the outdoor event.

• how you dealt with it
