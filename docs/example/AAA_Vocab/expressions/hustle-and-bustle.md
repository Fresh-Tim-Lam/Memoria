# hustle and bustle
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌhʌsl ən ˈbʌsl/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌhʌsl ən ˈbʌsl/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 两个实词各自首音节重读 — **HUS**tle and **BUS**tle；连词 and 弱读为 /ən/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the conjunction "and" is reduced to /ən/ and links the two words together, sounding like "hustle‑n‑bustle"; the shared /ʌsl/ ending makes the pair easy to blur into a single word.
  (中文说明：连词 and 弱读为 /ən/，与前词连读，听感近似 “hustle-n-bustle”；两词结尾同为 /ʌsl/，容易糊成一团。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, uncountable]** busy, noisy activity, especially of a large crowd or a city（繁忙嘈杂的活动；（城市的）喧嚣热闹）
- **Speaking example** – I love the hustle and bustle of the night market near my home.
  （我很喜欢我家附近夜市的喧闹热闹。）
- **Writing example** – Many young people move to big cities for the hustle and bustle of urban life, even though housing there is expensive.
  （许多年轻人为了都市生活的热闹繁华而搬到大城市，尽管那里的住房很贵。）

> 💡 用法提示：固定搭配，**必须带 the**，且**不可数**（不说 a hustle and bustle / hustles and bustles）。

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- `hustle` = to push and move quickly（匆忙推挤地行动）；`bustle` = busy, noisy movement（忙乱的活动）
- 两词押韵叠用（rhyming pair），语气比单用 hustle 或 bustle 更强，专指“人声鼎沸、熙熙攘攘”的整体氛围
- **Memory hook**: Picture a crowded market where everyone is **hustling** to move forward and every stall is **bustling** with customers.
  （想象一个拥挤的市集：每个人都在匆忙挤动，每个摊位都热闹非凡。）
