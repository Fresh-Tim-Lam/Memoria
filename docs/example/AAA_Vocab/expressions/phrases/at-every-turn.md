# at every turn
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ət ˈevri tɜːn/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ət ˈevri tɝːn/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音落在 **EV**ery 与 **TURN** 上 —— **EV**ery **TURN**；介词 at 弱读成 /ət/，几乎听不见
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: "at" reduces to /ət/ and links into the next word, so the whole phrase comes out as one breath group — "a‑TEV‑ry‑TURN" /ətˈevritɜːn/; do not confuse it with **in turn** /ɪn tɜːn/, which is a different phrase and far more common in writing.
  (中文说明：介词 at 弱读成 /ət/ 并连读进后面，整条听上去像一个词 a‑TEV‑ry‑TURN；千万别和 in turn（依次／相应地）混——那是另一条短语，写作里更常见。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary / Cambridge Dictionary / Merriam‑Webster

- **[prepositional phrase, Sense 1]** every time you try to do something; on every occasion（每次；动辄；每每）
- **Speaking example** – I tried to get through the list I had made, but something came up at every turn.
  （我想把清单上的事做完，可每次都冒出别的事。）
- **Writing example** – Attempts to reform the system were blocked at every turn.
  （改革该体制的努力处处受阻。）

- **[prepositional phrase, Sense 2]** in every place; wherever you go（处处；无论走到哪里）
- **Speaking example** – As a new student, I met kindness at every turn.
  （作为新生，我处处遇到善意。）
- **Writing example** – Visitors are reminded of the city’s history at every turn.
  （游客处处都能感受到这座城市的过往。）

> 💡 用法提示：`at every turn` 几乎总跟在动词后面收尾，**尤其被动**（`be thwarted / blocked / interrupted / helped / reminded … at every turn`）；它比 `every time` 重——`every time` 只报次数，`at every turn` 额外给出「密集、重复、躲不开」的累积感。

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: **计划／复习被一件件杂事打断**（Speaking Part 2「计划被迫改变」的 CHANGE 段）｜**改革、提案处处受阻**（Task 2）｜**初到陌生环境处处有人帮忙**｜叙事里「处处被提醒、被看见」
- **∇ Avoid**: 表**轮流**用 `take turns` / `in turn`；表**转向、反转**用 `turn out` / `make a U-turn`；只想强调「次数多」用 `time and again`。
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral‑strong**（中性偏强）——自带「密集、重复、躲不开」的累积感；要更重可加 `single`：`at every single turn`。
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `every time` → 中性有力 `at every turn` → 正式 `at every stage` / `at every juncture`

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- `turn` = 转、拐弯 → `at every turn` = 「每转一个弯都……」，像走在一条不断拐弯的走廊里，每个拐角都有一件事等着
- 易混形：`in turn`（依次／相应地）、`by turns`（轮流地；时而……时而）、`out of turn`（不合时宜／抢先）、`a U-turn`（掉头；政策急转）
- **Memory hook**: Picture a corridor that turns every few metres, and at every corner something blocks your way — that is *being obstructed at every turn*.
  （想象一条每隔几米就拐弯的走廊，每个拐角都有东西挡路——这就是 at every turn 的画面。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `at all turns` ／ ✗ `in every turn` ／ ✗ `at every turns` —— 固定形就是 **at every turn**：介词用 `at`，`turn` 用**单数**。
- ✗ `We clean the classroom at every turn.`（想说「轮流打扫」）→ `We take turns to clean the classroom.` / `We clean it in turn.`
- ✗ `I called him at every turn.`（想说「我给他打了好几次电话」）→ `time and again` / `several times`。
- ⚠️ 位置：更自然的是**跟在动词后面**（`… was blocked at every turn`）；放句首（`At every turn, I was interrupted.`）语法没错，但读着像翻译腔。
- ⚠️ 别与 `by turns`（轮流地；时而……时而）混：`She was by turns excited and nervous.` 说的是「一会儿兴奋一会儿紧张」。
