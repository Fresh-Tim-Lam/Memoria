# bump along
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌbʌmp əˈlɒŋ/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌbʌmp əˈlɔːŋ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 两个实词各自重读 — **BUMP** a**LONG**；连读后近似 /ˌbʌmpəˈlɒŋ/，听上去像一个词
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: "bump along" links into what sounds like a single word — "bumpa‑long" /bʌmpəˈlɒŋ/; do not confuse it with **bump into** (meet by chance / hit) or **bump up** (increase). The -ing form is **bumping** /ˈbʌmpɪŋ/, so "bumping along behind us" can blur into one long chunk.
  (中文说明：bump along 连读后像 “bumpa-long”；别与 bump into（偶遇／撞上）、bump up（抬高）混；-ing 形式 bumping /ˈbʌmpɪŋ/，整串 bumping along behind us 会糊成一块。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary / Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English

- **[verb phrase]** (of a vehicle or a carried object) to move forward with repeated heavy bumps or jolts（（车、被拖着的行李等）颠簸着前行；一路磕碰着挪动）
- **Speaking example** – We were sprinting the whole way through the crowd, with a suitcase bumping along behind us.
  （我们一路狂奔穿过人群，身后还拖着一个行李箱颠簸跟着。）
- **Writing example** – The old bus bumped along the mountain road for two hours.
  （那辆旧巴士在山路上颠簸了两个小时。）

- **[verb phrase, figurative]** to keep going with difficulty and without any clear plan（勉强维持着过下去；磕磕绊绊往前走）
- **Speaking example** – We just bumped along for a few months until things settled down.
  （我们就那样磕磕绊绊地过了几个月，直到情况稳定下来。）
- **Writing example** – Without a long‑term strategy, the project simply bumped along from one deadline to the next.
  （由于缺乏长期战略，这个项目只是从一个截止日期磕碰着撑到下一个。）

> 💡 用法提示：本词条重点收录**从口语素材里挑中的那半句** —— `with + 名词 + bumping along behind sb`。
> 这是**伴随状语结构**：用 `with` 引出“某物一路颠簸跟在身后”，一句话同时给出**画面**（笨重、磕碰）与**处境**（只能往前冲）。
> 结构槽位：**with + 名词 + V-ing（+ 方位状语）** → *with a suitcase bumping along behind us*。

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: **a suitcase or trolley trailing behind you**（行李箱、推车跟在身后）｜**an old bus or train on a rough road**（旧车走烂路）｜**a cart being dragged along**（拖着的小车）
- **∇ Avoid**: 描述**平稳**移动用 `roll along` / `glide along`；强调**声音**则用 `rattle along`（哐当作响）。
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral‑vivid**（中性但画面感强）—— 口语叙事很好用；正式写作改用 `lurch along` / `move unsteadily`。
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `bump along` → 正式 `lurch along` / `jolt along` → 文学 `rattle and judder along`。
- **来源 Source**: 库内口语素材 [`speaking/part2/1.md`](speaking/part2/1.md) 第 58 行的原句；释义据 Oxford / Longman。

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- `bump` = 碰撞、磕碰（拟声词，读音本身就“咚”一下）；`along` = 向前、沿路 → 合起来就是“一路磕碰着往前”
- 易混短语动词（意思都不同）：`bump into sb`（偶遇）／`bump into sth`（撞上）／`bump up`（抬高、增加）／`bump off`（俚：干掉）
- **Memory hook**: Picture a cheap suitcase with a broken wheel, **bumping along** behind a running traveller.
  （想象一只轮子坏了的便宜行李箱，跟在狂奔的旅客身后一路磕碰。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `with a suitcase bump along behind us` —— `with + 名词` 后面必须接 **V-ing**（bumping）或过去分词，不能用动词原形。
- ✗ `bump behind` —— 少了 along，就丢掉“一路颠簸前行”的过程感。
- ⚠️ 别与 `bump into` 混：`I bumped into my old teacher at the airport.` 是“偶遇”，与颠簸无关。
