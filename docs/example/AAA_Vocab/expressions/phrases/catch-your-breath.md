# catch your breath
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌkætʃ jɔː ˈbreθ/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌkætʃ jʊr ˈbreθ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音落在 **BREATH** 上；`catch` 与物主代词连读，`your` 在口语里弱读成 /jə/，整串听起来近似 /ˌkætʃəˈbreθ/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the final /tʃ/ of "catch" runs straight into "your", so the whole phrase blurs into "catch‑yer‑breath"; the possessive is unstressed and can sound as if it were missing.
  (中文说明：catch 的 /tʃ/ 与 your 连读，your 弱读，整条像 "catch‑yer‑breath"；物主代词几乎被吞掉，别误听成 `catch a breath` 或 `catch the breath`。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford / Cambridge（库内 `data/**`、`docs/ref/**` 暂无该搭配条目）

- **[idiom] catch one's breath**（also **get one's breath back**）– to breathe normally again after physical effort（（剧烈运动、狂奔、爬楼之后）把气喘匀；缓过气来）
  (中文释义：从「喘得说不了话」回到「能正常喘气」——**运动之后**的恢复动作；`Let me catch my breath.`)
- **Speaking example** – I sprinted all the way to the gate, and it took me a minute to catch my breath.
  （我一路冲到登机口，花了一分钟才把气喘匀。）
- **Writing example** – Runners were told to slow down and catch their breath before the next lap.
  （选手们被要求放慢速度，喘匀气再跑下一圈。）

- **[idiom] catch one's breath** – to stop breathing for a moment because of surprise, fear or shock（（因惊讶、害怕而）一时屏住呼吸；倒吸一口气）
  (中文释义：这一义的 `catch` 是「**突然被抓住**」——气一下子停住，不是「把气喘匀」。)
- **Speaking example** – I caught my breath when I saw how much the tickets cost.
  （看到票价的那一刻我倒吸了一口气。）
- **Writing example** – She caught her breath at the sight of the ruined house.
  （看到那栋破败的房子，她一时屏住了呼吸。）

- **[idiom, figurative] catch one's breath** – to pause and rest for a moment before continuing（（引申）停下来歇口气、缓一缓再继续）
  (中文释义：不一定真在喘，多用于忙完一段之后的短暂停顿。)
- **Speaking example** – Hold on — let me catch my breath and I'll tell you the rest.
  （等等——让我缓口气，我再把剩下的讲给你听。）
- **Writing example** – After three back‑to‑back deadlines, the team finally had a chance to catch its breath.
  （连赶三个截止日期之后，团队终于有机会喘口气。）

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
> **闸门作用**：这条习语的闸门不在难易，而在**「喘」是恢复性的还是主动的**——`catch` 只接**运动／受惊之后**的恢复，主动深吸一口气是另一条。
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 跑步、爬楼、狂奔赶路之后的恢复：`I stopped at the top of the stairs to catch my breath.` ② 讲受惊的瞬间：`He caught his breath in surprise.` ③ 引申「忙里歇口气」：`a chance to catch our breath` ④ 口语里请对方等一下：`Give me a moment to catch my breath.`
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「**主动深吸一口气**」（示范发音、稳住情绪）→ 用 `take a breath` / `take a deep breath`：`Take a deep breath and read it again.`
  - ✗ 想描述「**喘不上气的状态**」→ 用 `be out of breath` / `be short of breath`：`By the time I got there I was out of breath.`
  - ✗ 想纯说「呼吸」这个生理动作 → 用动词 `breathe`：`Breathe in slowly through your nose.`
  - ✗ 写成 `catch a breath` → 词典立条形式用物主代词：`catch my / your breath`
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral**——中性习语，口语与写作都可用；词典立条形式是 `catch one's breath`，实际最常说出来的形式是 `catch your breath`
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `get your breath back`（英式口语）→ 中性 `catch your breath` → 正式书面 `recover one's breath`
- **来源 Source**: 用户 2026‑10‑07 口述（课堂纠错）；物主代词形式与各义项释义据 Oxford / Cambridge，库内无词条可交叉核（`vocab/breathtaking.md` 只收 `breath` 词族）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- `catch` = 抓住、赶上——这里带「**追回来**」的意味：气跑在前头，你得把它追回来；`one's breath` = 自己的呼吸
- 同构「catch + 名词」结构：`catch a cold`（感冒）、`catch a glimpse of sb`（瞥见）、`catch sb's eye`（引起某人注意）
- 一字之差另一条：`hold one's breath`（屏住呼吸，主动憋住）；词族另见 [[breathtaking]]（`breath` 的名词化形容词用法）
- **Memory hook**: You **catch** your breath the way you **catch** a train — it has run on ahead, and you have to get it back.
  （把「气」当成一班跑在前头的车，你得追上它——这正是 catch 的意味。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `catch a breath` —— 把 `take a breath` 的冠词类推了过来；「跑步后把气喘匀」这条习语词典立条为**物主代词**形式 `catch one's / your breath`。（出处：用户 2026‑10‑07 口述的课堂纠错）
- ✗ 用 `catch my breath` 表示「主动深吸一口气」—— `I caught my breath and kept running.` 会被听成「我停下来把气缓过来了」；想说吸气用 `took a deep breath`。（出处：同上——用户本人的习惯说法在「吸气」义上是对的，只是不能拿去讲运动后的恢复）
