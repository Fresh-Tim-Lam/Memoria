# queue for ages
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /kjuː fə ˈeɪdʒɪz/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /kjuː fər ˈeɪdʒɪz/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 实词重读 — **QUEUE** /kjuː/、**A**ges /ˈeɪdʒɪz/；介词 for 弱读为 /fə/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: "queue" /kjuː/ is a homophone of "cue" and the letter "Q" — only the context tells them apart; the -ing form is **queuing** /ˈkjuːɪŋ/, also spelled **queueing**. American English usually says "stand in line / wait in line" instead of "queue".
  (中文说明：queue 与 cue、字母 Q 同音，只能靠上下文分辨；变 -ing 时拼作 queuing 或 queueing，两种都被词典收录，queuing 更常见。美式英语里“排队”常说 stand in line / wait in line，几乎不用 queue。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary / Longman Dictionary of Contemporary English

- **[verb phrase + adverbial]** to wait in a queue for a very long time（排很久的队；排队等上老半天）
- **[fixed adverbial]** for ages = for a very long time（很久）——英式口语，语气比 for a long time 更随意
- **Speaking example** – We queued for ages outside the ticket office, but it was worth it in the end.
  （我们在售票处外排了好久的队，但最后觉得值得。）
- **Writing example** –[[\c:blue| Because passengers had to queue for ages at the check-in desk, many of them missed the chance to shop in the departure lounge.]]
  （由于旅客在值机柜台排了很久的队，许多人错过了在候机厅购物的机会。）

> 💡 用法提示：这个表达＝**动作**（queue）＋**时长强化**（for ages），三处常见落点：
> ① 句尾状语 —— We queued for ages.（我们在队伍里排了很久。）
> ② after + V-ing，表先后 —— after queuing for ages
> ③ spend ages + V-ing —— I spent ages queuing for tickets.（我花了好久排队买票。）
> ⚠️ 介词与 such as 后面必须接 **-ing**：✗ after queue for ages ／ ✗ such as queue for ages
> ⚠️ for ages 属英式口语；正式书面（Task 1/2）改用 for a long time ／ for hours。

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- queue 源自拉丁语 **cauda**（尾巴），经古法语进入英语：一列人像尾巴一样蜿蜒排开 → “队列”
- 名词与动词同形：a queue（队列）／ to queue（排队）；英式日常还有动词短语 **queue up**
- **Memory hook**: Picture a line of people wriggling like a long tail, all checking their watches — that is a queue, and you have been in it for ages.
  （想象一列人像长尾巴一样蜿蜒排开、人人都在看表——这就是 queue，而你已经在里面待了很久。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `after queue for ages` —— 介词 after 后必须接 -ing：**after queuing for ages**。
- ✗ `such as queue for ages` —— such as 后只接名词短语：**such as **[[\c:blue|***queuing for ages****。*]]
- ✗ `queing` —— 少了一个 u，正确拼写为 **queuing** ／ **queueing**。
- ✗ `I queued for ages waiting in the queue` —— 语义重复，二者留一：I queued for ages. ／ I spent ages waiting.
- ⚠️ 语域偏差：`for ages` 很口语，正式书面请用 **for a long time / for hours**。
- ⚠️ 地区偏差：美式英语几乎不用 queue，说 **stand in line / wait in line** 更自然。
