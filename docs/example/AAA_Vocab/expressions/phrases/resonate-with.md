# resonate with sb/sth

## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˈrezəneɪt wɪð/   (based on Oxford/Cambridge)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˈrezəneɪt wɪð/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 主重音落在 **RES-**（第一音节），词尾 `-ate` 读足 /eɪt/ 不弱化；`with` 在语流里弱读，常整块黏进后面的代词
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the verb keeps its full /eɪt/ ending even at speed, and *with* usually fuses into the pronoun that follows, so *resonates with me* can sound like one long word — /ˈrezəneɪtswɪðmi/; the opening is also easy to mistake for *resort* or *respond*.
  (中文说明：resonate 的词尾 -ate 在语流里仍读足 /eɪt/，不弱化；with 又常与后面的代词黏成一个词，听感上像一整串，别在中间找停顿；开头也容易听成 resort / respond。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford / Cambridge（库内 `docs/ref/**` 暂无该词条，下列措辞待核）

- **[verb, intransitive] resonate (with sb/sth)** – if an idea, a statement or a work of art resonates with you, you feel that it matches your own situation, beliefs or feelings（引起共鸣；让你觉得说的就是自己）
  (中文释义：宾语只能是**人**或**人的经历**——`with me` / `with voters` / `with my own experience`；动词不及物，人称代词不能直接放在动词后面。)
- **Speaking example** – That line about being the first in the family to go to university really resonated with me.
  （那句「家里第一个上大学的人」真说到我心里去了。）
- **Writing example** – Such arguments resonate with voters who feel left behind by globalisation.
  （这类论点能在被全球化抛下的选民那里引起共鸣。）

- **[verb, intransitive] (of a sound) resonate** – to produce a deep, full sound that keeps going in a space, or (of an event) to keep having an effect long after it happens（（声音）回荡、共振；（事件）影响久远）
  (中文释义：物理义是声音在空间里回响；写作里由它引申出「余响至今」的用法——`The decision still resonates today.`，用来收束历史或政策类话题很好用。)
- **Speaking example** – The hall is so large that every note resonates for a second or two.
  （大厅太大，每个音都要回荡一两秒。）
- **Writing example** – The reforms of 1978 still resonate across the whole economy.
  （1978 年的改革至今仍在整个经济领域留有回响。）

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
> **为何放在这个位置**：先判断「共鸣」发生在**听者**身上，还是来自**说话人的意图**——`resonate with sb` 讲的是听者的真实感受，不含「刻意讨好」的意思；这一层看不清，就会把「迎合」误写成「共鸣」。
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 描述书／电影／演讲打动自己（Part 2）：`The film resonated with me because …`；② Part 3 解释媒体或口号为什么有力量：`It resonates with people who …`；③ 写作里谈观点、政策的接受度：`The message resonates with younger voters.`；④ 讲事件影响久远（物理义引申）：`The speech still resonates today.`
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「迎合观众、专挑他们爱听的说」→ 用 `pander to sb` / `play to the audience` / `tell viewers what they want to hear`：`He was pandering to his viewers.`（`resonate` 描述的是自然发生的共鸣，没有「刻意讨好」这层批评意味，写在这里就用错了词）
  - ✗ 人不能作宾语：`It resonates me` 不成立 → `It resonates with me.`（动词不及物，`with` 不能省）
  - ✗ 写作里不写个人感受：Task 2 不用 `This resonates with me`，改让第三方作主语——`This argument resonates with voters.`
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral** —— 它比 `I liked it` 更精确地说明「为什么被打动」，但并不表示程度更强；情绪强度靠 `really` / `deeply` 调
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 口语 `I could really relate to it` → 中性 `It resonates with me` → 正式 `It strikes a chord with voters`

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `son-` = sound（拉丁语 *sonare*「发声」）+ `re-` = back / again —— `resonate` 字面就是「声音再回来」：先指**声音在空间里回荡**，再指**一句话在心里回荡**
- 同族词：`sonic`（声音的）、`sonorous`（声音洪亮的）、`dissonance`（不和谐音 → 观点分歧）、`supersonic`（超音速的）
- 结构提示：`-ate` 结尾的动词重音落在倒数第三音节——`RES-onate`、`CON-stitute`、`AN-ticipate`；记住这条，读音就不会往后跑
- **Memory hook**: A bell keeps ringing after the hand has gone; an idea that **resonates** keeps ringing inside you after the speaker has stopped.
  （钟敲过了、手拿开了，声音还在；一句 resonate 的话，说话人停下了，它还在你心里响。）
