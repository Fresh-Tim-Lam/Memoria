# step back in time
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌstep bæk ɪn ˈtaɪm/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌstep bæk ɪn ˈtaɪm/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 实词重读 — **STEP** **BACK** in **TIME**；介词 in 弱读为 /ɪn/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: "back in" links across words and sounds like "back-in" /bækɪn/; be careful not to hear it as "backing". Also note that "step back" alone means to move backwards or to withdraw — the "in time" is what creates the time‑travel meaning.
  (中文说明：back 与 in 连读，听感接近 “back-in” /bækɪn/，勿听成 backing；另注意 step back 单独使用只表“退后一步／抽身”，须带 in time 才有“穿越回过去”之义。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary / Collins COBUILD

- **[idiom / noun phrase]** if a place is **a step back in time**, it makes you feel as if you have travelled into the past（（地方）让人仿佛回到过去；有穿越时空之感）
- **Speaking example** – Walking through the old town feels like a step back in time.
  （漫步在这座老城里，仿佛回到了过去。）
- **Writing example** – The restored market square, with its cobbled lanes and gas lamps, is a step back in time.
  （修复后的集市广场有鹅卵石小巷和煤气灯，让人仿佛穿越回过去。）

- **[verb phrase]** to **step back in time** – to imagine or experience what the past was like（回到过去；追想往昔）
- **Speaking example** – The open‑air museum lets visitors step back in time and try traditional crafts.
  （这座露天博物馆让游客回到过去，体验传统手工艺。）
- **Writing example** – Preserving such districts allows future generations to step back in time and understand how their ancestors lived.
  （保护这类街区能让后代回溯往昔，理解先人的生活方式。）

> 💡 用法提示：这是**形容历史街区氛围**的高频地道表达，比 `very old` / `historical` 生动得多。
> 谈论历史场所时，语义重心在**主观体验**（“我仿佛穿越了”），而非客观年代。

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 三个部分：`step back`（向后迈一步）+ `in time`（在时间中）→ 比喻义：**在时间轴上向后迈一步**
- 无派生词；但同一喻体可换动词：`go / travel / be transported / take a step back in time`（见下表）
- **Memory hook**: Imagine literally taking **one step backwards**, and landing 300 years ago.
  （想象你真的向后迈一步，就落在三百年前。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `Stepping back several centuries to describe ...` —— 分词短语**不能单独作谓语**，须依附主句：
  ✓ `It feels like stepping back several centuries.` / ✓ `You feel as if you are stepping back several centuries.`
- ✗ `a step back of time` / `step back to time` —— 固定用 **in** time。
- ✗ `step back in the time` —— 不加冠词，永远是 `in time`。
- ⚠️ 语义偏差：只说 `step back` 时意思是“退后一步”或“抽身重新思考”，**不带穿越义**，必须补 `in time`。
