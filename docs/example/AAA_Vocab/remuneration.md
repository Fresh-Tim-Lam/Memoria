## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary
For each sense:
- **[noun, countable] Definition 1** – something that is part of your past, left by an ancestor, predecessor or history (遗产；遗留问题；历史积淀)
  (中文释义：遗产；遗留影响；历史遗留产物)
- **Speaking example** – The city’s cultural legacy draws millions of tourists every year.
  (中文翻译：这座城市的文化遗产每年吸引数百万游客。)
- **Writing example** – Colonialism left a complex legacy of social inequality across many nations.
  (中文翻译：殖民主义在诸多国家留下了社会不平等这一复杂遗留问题。)

- **[noun, countable] Definition 2** – money or property left to somebody in a will (遗赠的钱财、财物)
  (中文释义：遗嘱留下的财产，遗赠)
- **Speaking example** – He received a substantial legacy from his late grandfather.
  (中文翻译：他从已故祖父那里得到一笔可观的遗赠。)
- **Writing example** – A large legacy was donated to fund scientific research.
  (中文翻译：一笔数额巨大的遗赠被捐赠用以资助科学研究。)

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `leg‑` = to send, bequeath (拉丁语legare，遗赠、托付) (中文：拉丁语词根，含义遗赠、托付)
- Affixes: `-acy` (名词后缀，表状态、产物)
- Memory hook: What the past **bequeaths** becomes your **legacy**, good or bad. (中文记忆提示：过去所托付留存下来的，无论好坏，就是legacy)
