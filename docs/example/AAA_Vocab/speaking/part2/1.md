# Describe a time you received some good news

> **来源**：课件 PPT「PRACTICE TOPIC 01 · Direction 1: a positive situation」——题干、4 条提示、`SETTING / CHANGE / FEELING` 规划框架与关键词（take off / landed / baggage claim / flight）。
> **素材**：用户本人口述（2026-09-23）——坐飞机回家转机，时间极紧、取完行李还要再转机、机场极大、从一端跑到另一端，最后得知转机航班延误，侥幸赶上。
> **题型**：Speaking Part 2 经历题（positive situation）｜目标时长 1′40″–2′00″（约 245 词 @ 120–130 wpm）｜示范作答约 245 词。
> **说明**：情节按用户口述逐条保留；英文为示范转写（非用户原文）；示范作答中的升级词块按库内词条组织，对应关系见「词块与升级」。

---

## 📋 题目 / Task

**Describe a time when you received some good news.**

You should say:
- what the news was
- when and where you received it
- who told you
- and how you felt about it

> 课件底部给的三个备选素材：`winning a competition` ｜ `buying train tickets successfully` ｜ `transferring a plane in time`——本条用的是**第三个**。

---

## 🧭 三槽位规划 / Planning Frame（SETTING → CHANGE → FEELING）

| 槽位 | 本故事对应内容 | 关键英文词块 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **SETTING** | 上学期末坐飞机回家；没有直飞，要在超大机场转机；第一程就起飞晚了，只剩大约五十分钟 | flying home / a connecting flight / a stopover / took off late / only about fifty minutes |
| **CHANGE** | 行李没直挂，得先去 baggage claim 取回再重新办登机；两个登机口在航站楼两端，一路狂奔、精疲力竭——结果**转机航班延误了** | my bag hadn't been checked through / baggage claim / at opposite ends of the terminal / I was completely out of breath / the flight had been delayed |
| **FEELING** | 先是如释重负的狂喜，接着是腿软般的虚脱；像被给了第二次机会 | a huge wave of relief / burst out laughing / my legs were still shaking / a second chance |

**四问覆盖检查**：what＝转机航班延后一小时（不是取消）｜when & where＝上学期末，机场转机柜台／登机口｜who＝登机口的地勤人员｜how＝狂喜＋虚脱。四条全部落在上面三槽位里，不必另编内容。

## 🗣️ 固定开场模板 / Fixed Opening（必须套用）

> 老师给的固定开头，**答题第一句就得说它**；`...` 处按自己的素材填空：

```text
Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there are/were ... , such as ... . However, if I had to choose one / pick one, I would say that is/was ...  /  I would definitely go for ...
```

**本故事的填空版**：

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there **have been quite a few things that really lifted my spirits**, such as **getting my train tickets sorted out after [[queuing for ages]]**. However, **if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the time I just managed to catch my connecting flight on my way home.**

> 💡 模板的语法开关：素材讲**一般情况／多个例子** → `there are` + `that is`；素材讲**一件过去的事** → `there were` + `that was`（本故事用后者）。`such as` 后面只接**名词短语**，不要接完整句子。
> 🔁 **开场句备用版**（`if I had to pick one` 说腻了就换一个）：
> - `However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be ...`——「挑一个最出挑的」；
> - `However, the most memorable one was definitely ...`——直接点出「最……的那一个」。
> ⚠️ **题干原词别照搬**：题目写的是 `good news`，答案里就不要再出现 `good news`——照抄题干原词**很扣分**（考官一听就知道在背题）。同一个意思换成 `wonderful news`、`a piece of great news`、`a few things that really lifted my spirits`、`something that made my day`。

## ✍️ 示范作答 / Model Answer（约 2 分钟）

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been quite a few things that really lifted my spirits, such as getting my train tickets sorted out after [[queuing for ages]]. However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the time I just managed to catch my connecting flight on the way home.

It happened at the end of last term, on my way home from university. My first flight took off late, and to make matters worse, my suitcase hadn't been checked through, so I had to collect it at the baggage claim and check in again — by then I had only fifty minutes left.

Our two gates were at opposite ends of that huge airport, so we were [[sprinting]] through the crowd with a suitcase [[bumping along behind]] us. By the time we reached the counter I was completely out of breath — I was sure we had missed it.

And then the woman at the gate told us our connecting flight had been delayed by an hour — the best news all week, because the plane was not going anywhere without us.

So how did I feel? A huge wave of relief — it was like a weight coming off my shoulders. My legs were still shaking, but I burst out laughing. [[Perilous]] though that half an hour was, it ended far better than I had any right to expect, and a delayed flight can still feel like wonderful news.

## 🔁 平实版对照 / Say It in Plain English

> **为什么加这一节**：示范作答里有不少「专有词 / 文雅词」，说不出来不等于讲不了这个故事。下面**逐句**配一个**平实版**——只用常见词与常见句型，意思和原句**完全一样**；临场卡住就说平实版，内容一点不打折（考官评的是「讲清楚」，不是「用大词」）。
> **怎么读**：每对里，上面一行是示范作答的原句（原句里的词条跳转已省略，便于对照），下面 `↩︎ 平实版` 是你可以直接说的版本；括号里标出「哪个词被换掉了」。

### 1. 逐句对照（共 11 句）

**①** Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been quite a few things that really lifted my spirits, such as getting my train tickets sorted out after queuing for ages.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样照说即可——这是老师给的固定模板，本身就是常见词；`lifted my spirits` 想不起来就说 `made me really happy`。

**②** However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the time I just managed to catch my connecting flight on the way home.

↩︎ **平实版**：However, if I had to choose one, it would be the time I caught my next flight just in time on my way home.（`pick one that stands out` → `choose one`；`connecting flight` → `next flight`；`just managed to catch` → `caught … just in time`）

**③** It happened at the end of last term, on my way home from university.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样说（`last term` 想不起来就说 `last semester`／`at the end of the school year`）。

**④** My first flight took off late, and to make matters worse, my suitcase hadn't been checked through, so I had to collect it at the baggage claim and check in again — by then I had only fifty minutes left.

↩︎ **平实版**：My first flight left late, and worse still, my suitcase had not been sent all the way to my last stop, so I had to pick it up and check in again — by then I had only fifty minutes left.（`took off late` → `left late`；`to make matters worse` → `and worse still`；`hadn't been checked through` → `had not been sent all the way to my last stop`；`baggage claim` 想不起来就说 `the place where you pick up your bags`）

**⑤** Our two gates were at opposite ends of that huge airport, so we were sprinting through the crowd with a suitcase bumping along behind us.

↩︎ **平实版**：Our two gates were at the two ends of that very big airport, so we ran through the crowd, pulling a suitcase behind us.（`at opposite ends of` → `at the two ends of`；`were sprinting` → `ran`；`bumping along behind us` → `pulling a suitcase behind us`）

**⑥** By the time we reached the counter I was completely out of breath — I was sure we had missed it.

↩︎ **平实版**：When we reached the counter I could hardly breathe — I thought we had missed the flight.（`by the time we reached` → `when we reached`；`completely out of breath` → `could hardly breathe`；`was sure` → `thought`）

**⑦** And then the woman at the gate told us our connecting flight had been delayed by an hour — the best news all week, because the plane was not going anywhere without us.

↩︎ **平实版**：And then the woman at the gate told us our next flight was an hour late — the best news all week, because the plane would wait for us.（`had been delayed by an hour` → `was an hour late`；`the plane was not going anywhere without us` → `the plane would wait for us`）

**⑧** So how did I feel?

↩︎ **平实版**：原样说。

**⑨** A huge wave of relief — it was like a weight coming off my shoulders.

↩︎ **平实版**：I felt very relieved — it was like a heavy weight came off my shoulders.（`a huge wave of relief` → `I felt very relieved`；`a weight coming off my shoulders` → `a heavy weight came off my shoulders`）

**⑩** My legs were still shaking, but I burst out laughing.

↩︎ **平实版**：My legs were still shaking, but I laughed out loud.（`burst out laughing` → `laughed out loud`／`started laughing`）

**⑪** Perilous though that half an hour was, it ended far better than I had any right to expect, and a delayed flight can still feel like wonderful news.

↩︎ **平实版**：That half an hour was very dangerous, but it ended much better than I could have hoped — and a delayed flight can still feel like wonderful news.（让步倒装 `Perilous though that half an hour was` → 平铺 `That half an hour was …, but …`）

> 💡 平实版是**兜底**，不是目标：示范作答里的升级词记得住就照样用（那些是评分里的加分点）；平实版只保证你想不起难词时**不卡住、不中断**。整段讲顺了，再回来把词一个个升上去。

## 🔍 高分句（背下来就能用）

1. [[\h|to make matters worse, my suitcase hadn't been checked through]] —— 递进式加重困境，`to make matters worse` 是口语叙事里最省力的转折词块。
2. [[\h|we were sprinting through the crowd with a suitcase bumping along behind us]] —— `sprint`（冲刺）比 `run` 更快更形象，配 `through the crowd` 直接把画面拉出来。
3. [[\h|the connecting flight had been delayed by an hour]] —— **点题句**：好消息的具体内容；`had been delayed by + 时长`（被动＋过去完成）说明「在我们到达之前就已经发生」。
4. [[\h|Perilous though that half an hour was, it ended far better than I had any right to expect]] —— 让步倒装收尾（句式说明见 [[concessive-inversion]]），把前面的波折一句话收成感想，是 Part 2 最划算的加分句型。

## 🧰 词块与升级 / Chunks & Upgrades

### 1. 课件关键词（必须会说）

| 课件写的 | 读法要点 | 在答案里怎么用 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **take off** | /teɪk ɒf/，重音落 off | My first flight **took off** late.（起飞晚）——反义为 `land` / `touch down` |
| **landed** | /ˈlændɪd/ | By the time we **landed**, I had fifty minutes left.（落地时） |
| **baggage claim** | /ˈbæɡɪdʒ kleɪm/，重音落 bag | I had to go to the **baggage claim** first.（行李提取处） |
| **flight** | /flaɪt/ | a **connecting flight** / a **delayed flight**（转机航班／被延误的航班） |

⚠️ 课件上还写了一个 **`walked out the board`** ——这不是通行英语，疑为课件笔误或识别误差；想说「下飞机」用 `get off the plane` / `disembark`，想说「登机」用 `board the plane` / `go through the boarding gate`。

### 2. 库内词条升级（可直接替换平实说法）

| 想说的意思 | 平实说法 | 升级（库内词条） | 例句 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 那半小时很凶险 | it was dangerous | **perilous**（[[perilous]]） | Perilous though that half an hour was, we made it. |
| 消息让人放心 | it made me feel better | **reassuring**（[[reassuring]]） | The announcement was deeply reassuring. |
| 情况看起来不错 | it looked good | **promising**（[[promising]]） | For a while, our chances didn't look promising at all. |
| 结果不错 | a good result | **favourable**（[[favourable]]） | The delay turned out to be favourable for us. |
| 机场里人声鼎沸 | the busy airport | **the hustle and bustle of ...**（[[hustle-and-bustle]]） | We were running through the hustle and bustle of the terminal. |
| 让步倒装收尾 | even though it was hard | **hard though ... may be**（[[concessive-inversion]]） | Perilous though that half an hour was, it ended well. |

> 课件底部三句素材的**小修**（原句语法有误，说的时候别照念）：`a time that I transfer a plane in time` → **`a time when I made a tight transfer just in time`**；`a time that I successfully buying train tickets` → **`a time when I managed to buy the train tickets`**。

## ⚠️ 本题专属坑 / Traps

- **news 不可数**：✅ a piece of good news / a few bits of good news；❌ a good news、two good news。
- **题干原词别照搬**：题目写的是 **good news**，答案里就不要再出现 `good news`——照抄题干原词**很扣分**（考官一听就知道在背题）。同一个意思换成 **wonderful news** / a piece of great news / something that really made my day；开场铺陈用 **a few things that really lifted my spirits**，收尾呼应写 **felt like wonderful news to me**。
- **delay ≠ cancel**：延误是 `be delayed by an hour`，取消是 `be cancelled`。本故事的好消息正是「**延误**」——顺口说成 cancelled 就变成坏消息了。
- **make it / catch 的搭配**：✅ managed to catch the connecting flight / made it with minutes to spare / made it by the skin of my teeth（侥幸赶上）；❌ catch up the flight。
- **transfer 的用法**：讲经历用 `a connecting flight` / `change planes at ...` 最稳；`transit` 多指「过境」（a transit visa），不用于「我转了机」。
- **luggage / baggage**：航空场景与 `baggage claim` 固定用 baggage；两者都不可数，❌ luggages。
- **过去完成时用对位置**：航班延误发生在「我们到柜台之前」，所以 the flight **had been delayed**；写成 was delayed 会听起来像当场才延误。

## 🎤 交付提示 / Delivery Notes

- **句重音**：压在 `late`、`fifty minutes`、`baggage claim`、`opposite ends`、`out of breath`、`delayed`、`relief` 上——考官靠这些词抓故事线。
- **停顿**：在转向好消息处停半拍（And then … the woman at the gate told us …），制造「反转」效果。
- **语速**：SETTING 段可略快、一口气说完；说到 I was sure we had missed it 时放慢，再报出好消息。
- **填充词**：卡顿时用 honestly / to be honest / the thing is 拖时间。
- **降调收尾**：最后一句用降调落下来，给出「故事讲完了」的信号，方便考官接话。

## 🔁 追问演练 / Part 3 Follow-ups

1. **Why do people like sharing good news with others?** → 开场：I suppose it comes down to ...（多半是因为……）；可用词块：spread the joy、a sense of relief。
2. **Do you think the media focuses too much on bad news?** → 开场：There's no denying that ... , but ...；负面用 [[troubled]]、[[perilous]]，正面用 [[reassuring]]、[[promising]]。
3. **Is it better to receive good news by phone or in person?** → 开场：On balance, I'd say ...（总的来说）；可用词块：hearing it face to face、seeing someone's face light up。
