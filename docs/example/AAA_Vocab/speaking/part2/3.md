# Describe a time when you had to change your plans unexpectedly

> **来源**：课件 PPT「PRACTICE TOPIC 03 · Direction 3: an unexpected change of plan」——题干、4 条提示、`SETTING / CHANGE / ACTION / RESULT` 四槽位规划框架与 `Target: use two situation words naturally and include one clear turning point.`；OCR 全文见 `docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md`（幻灯片 1），原图 `docs/ref/ppt/2dc24721f1ed401a0e507b5a218f3fa9.png`；本题可用的语言表见同课件「Useful language for different situations」（幻灯片 3）。
> **素材**：用户本人口述（2026-09-23）——本人自述是爱做计划的人，但事情太多忙不过来，对生活琐碎不太关心；以前很在意计划被改变，如今连细节都不太记得，只能讲个大概。这一件：一次回家前把假期排得很满（看论文、补上上周落下的课、把论文里的算法真正做出来），回家后不断被家务、家人聚餐、出门玩打断，精力被切碎；想靠刷手机把状态捡回来时，发现假期已快结束，而任务需要长时间专注，于是不得不把计划改成补上周落下的内容；这件事很让人郁闷——一是计划没完成、被迫改了，二是那份计划本身就远超自己的能力。
> **题型**：Speaking Part 2 经历题（unexpected change of plan）｜目标时长 1′40″–2′00″（约 260 词 @ 120–130 wpm）｜示范作答约 260 词。
> **说明**：情节按用户口述逐条保留；用户自述「只能描述一个大概」，故示范作答**不编造**具体日期、课程名、人物与数字；英文为示范转写（非用户原文）；示范作答中的升级词块按库内词条组织，对应关系见「词块与升级」。

## 📋 题目 / Task

**Describe a time when you had to change your plans unexpectedly.**

You should say:
- what you planned to do
- what caused the change
- what you did instead
- how the situation ended

**课件的语言表（同课件「Useful language for different situations」，按情境分两组）**

| 情境 | 课件写的 | 中文 | 课件例句 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **POSITIVE OR IMPROVING** | `rewarding` / `worthwhile` | 值得的、有收获的 | The experience was challenging but rewarding. |
| | `reassuring` / `comforting` | 令人安心的 | Her calm response was extremely reassuring. |
| | `work out well` / `end successfully` | 结果顺利 | Fortunately, everything worked out well. |
| | `turning point` / `key moment` | 转折点 | That phone call became a turning point. |
| **DIFFICULT OR UNCERTAIN** | `tense` | 气氛紧张 | The atmosphere became tense. |
| | `awkward` | 别扭、尴尬 | I found myself in an awkward situation. |
| | `under pressure` | 在压力之下 | I had to decide while under pressure. |
| | `take a turn for the worse` | 变得更糟 | The weather took a turn for the worse. |

> 🎯 **课件 Target：用上两个「情境词」，并给一个清楚的转折点**。同一套课件有 [[perilous]]、[[promising]]、[[troubled]]、[[favourable]] 四张情境词卡（词卡 OCR 见 `docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md` 幻灯片 2 / 5 / 6 / 7）；课件**没有**指定本题配哪两张（Direction 2 的题卡明写 `perilous situation`，本题题卡没有这一行），所以本条按语义选取 [[promising]]（计划开场看着有戏）与 [[favourable]]（到后面条件已经不利）当两个情境词。
> ⚠️ [[troubled]] 本题**慎用**：课件明写 `For a temporary event, difficult or stressful often sounds more natural.`——一次假期被打乱属**一次性事件**，说 `a troubled holiday` 是夸张；要铺背景可用 `a difficult couple of weeks`。

## 🧭 四槽位规划 / Planning Frame（SETTING → CHANGE → ACTION → RESULT）

| 槽位 | 本故事对应内容 | 关键英文词块 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **SETTING** | 用户是爱做计划的人；回家前把假期排得很满——看论文、补上上周落下的课、把论文里的算法真正做出来；「人」是家里人（后面的聚餐、出门都跟他们在一起） | a planner / write out a full list / read papers I had been putting off / catch up on the lectures I had missed / get my paper's algorithm working |
| **CHANGE** | 一回到家就被一件件事占满：家务、家人聚餐、出门玩，全都推不掉；计划只能被一件件打断着往前挪；精力被切成碎片，而几项任务都要长时间专注才做得动；**转折点**——某个晚上想靠刷手机把状态捡回来，两个小时就没了，翻日历时只剩几天 | housework / meals with my family / days out / the plan bumped along from one interruption to the next / my energy was cut into small pieces / needed long, focused hours / the turning point came when … / only a few days were left |
| **ACTION** | 把清单砍到只剩一件：补上上周落下的课（唯一能拆成小段做的）；论文算法那块只能先放下 | cut the list down to one item / go over what I had missed in class / do it in short bursts / the algorithm work had to wait |
| **RESULT** | 郁闷——两层：一是没做完、被迫做了别的事；二是终于看清那份清单远超自己的能力；唯一让人安心的是至少有一件事做完了；最后落在「按真实的时间与精力做计划」这个认识上 | felt quite low about it / had been pushed into something completely different / far bigger than my actual ability / the only reassuring part / plan around the time I really have |

**四问覆盖检查**：what you planned to do＝看论文＋补上周落下的课＋把论文算法做出来｜what caused the change＝家务、聚餐、出门玩把时间与精力切碎，而任务需要长时段专注｜what you did instead＝把清单砍到只剩「补上周落下的课」｜how it ended＝很郁闷（没完成＋计划远超能力），但认清了要按真实的时间与精力做计划。四条全部落在上面四槽位里，不必另编内容。

## 🗣️ 固定开场模板 / Fixed Opening（必须套用）

> 老师给的固定开头，**答题第一句就得说它**；`...` 处按自己的素材填空：

```text
Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there are/were ... , such as ... . However, if I had to choose one / pick one, I would say that is/was ...  /  I would definitely go for ...
```

**本故事的填空版**：

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there **have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked**, such as **missing a train after a last-minute phone call, or losing a weekend trip to bad weather**. However, **if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the holiday I spent at home last term.**

<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：`such as` 后两个备选经历（错过火车／周末旅行因天气中断）是为套模板举的一般性例子，非用户亲历；实际作答请换成自己的经历 -->

> 💡 模板的语法开关：素材讲**一般情况／多个例子** → `there are` + `that is`；素材讲**一件过去的事** → `there were` + `that was`。本故事前半句铺「不止一次」（到现在为止的经历）用 `there have been`，真正要讲的那一件是过去的事，所以落到 `it would be`／`that was`。`such as` 后面只接**名词短语**，不要接完整句子。
> 🔁 **开场句备用版**（`if I had to pick one` 说腻了就换一个）：
> - `However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be ...`——「挑一个最出挑的」；
> - `However, the most memorable one was definitely ...`——直接点出「最……的那一个」。
> ⚠️ **题干原词别照搬**：题目写的是 `change your plans` 与 `unexpectedly`，答案里就**不要**再说 `change my plans` / `unexpectedly`——照抄题干原词**很扣分**（考官一听就知道在背题）。换成：`my plan had to be reworked`、`my plan fell apart`、`I had to rethink what I could realistically get done`、`out of the blue`。

## ✍️ 示范作答 / Model Answer（约 2 分钟）

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked, such as missing a train after a last-minute phone call, or losing a weekend trip to bad weather. However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the holiday I spent at home last term.

I have always been a planner, and this time the list had three big items: reading papers, catching up on lectures, and getting an algorithm working. The plan looked [[promising]], and the first day or two went well.

But then everything at home piled up: housework, meals with my family, and days out, so the plan simply [[bumped along]] from one interruption to the next, and I [[indulged]] myself with one family dinner after another. The turning point came one evening, when I picked up my phone and ended up [[flailing]] on it for two hours. When I opened my calendar, only a few days were left, and the time was clearly not [[favourable]].

So I cut the list down to one item: going over the lectures I had missed. The algorithm work had to wait.

Honestly, I felt quite low about it. I had not finished my list, and underneath that, I saw that it had been far bigger than my ability. The only [[reassuring]] part was that one thing got done.

Frustrating though it was, it taught me to plan around the time I really have, not the time I wish I had.

## 🔁 平实版对照 / Say It in Plain English

> **为什么加这一节**：示范作答里有不少「专有词 / 文雅词」，说不出来不等于讲不了这个故事。下面**逐句**配一个**平实版**——只用常见词与常见句型，意思和原句**完全一样**；临场卡住就说平实版，内容一点不打折（考官评的是「讲清楚」，不是「用大词」）。
> **怎么读**：每对里，上面一行是示范作答的原句（原句里的词条跳转已省略，便于对照），下面 `↩︎ 平实版` 是你可以直接说的版本；括号里标出「哪个词被换掉了」。

### 1. 逐句对照（共 13 句）

**①** Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked, such as missing a train after a last-minute phone call, or losing a weekend trip to bad weather.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样照说即可——这是老师给的固定模板，本身就是常见词；`reworked` 想不起来就说 `had to be sorted out again`（`change` 是题干原词，也尽量别照念）。

**②** However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be the holiday I spent at home last term.

↩︎ **平实版**：However, if I had to choose one, it would be the holiday I spent at home.（`pick one that stands out` → `choose one`；`last term` 想不起来就说 `a few months ago`）

**③** I have always been a planner, and this time the list had three big items: reading papers, catching up on lectures, and getting an algorithm working.

↩︎ **平实版**：I have always liked making plans, and this time my list had three big things on it: reading papers, going over the classes I had missed, and getting the method in my paper to work.（`a planner` → `liked making plans`；`catch up on lectures` → `go over the classes`；`algorithm` 想不起来就说 `the method in my paper`）

**④** The plan looked promising, and the first day or two went well.

↩︎ **平实版**：The plan looked good, and the first day or two went well.（`looked promising` → `looked good`／`looked like it could work`）

**⑤** But then everything at home piled up: housework, meals with my family, and days out, so the plan simply bumped along from one interruption to the next, and I indulged myself with one family dinner after another.

↩︎ **平实版**：But then everything at home kept coming: housework, meals with my family, and days out, so the plan just kept getting interrupted, and I had one family dinner after another.（`piled up` → `kept coming`；`bumped along from one interruption to the next` → `kept getting interrupted`；`indulged myself with` → `had`）

**⑥** The turning point came one evening, when I picked up my phone and ended up flailing on it for two hours.

↩︎ **平实版**：The key moment came one evening, when I picked up my phone and ended up doing nothing on it for two hours.（`the turning point came` → `the key moment came`；`flailing on it` → `doing nothing on it`；`when I picked up … and ended up …` 这个架子留着）

**⑦** When I opened my calendar, only a few days were left, and the time was clearly not favourable.

↩︎ **平实版**：When I opened my calendar, only a few days were left, and things were clearly not in my favour.（`not favourable` → `not in my favour`／`not good for me`）

**⑧** So I cut the list down to one item: going over the lectures I had missed.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样说（`cut the list down to one item` → `made the list much shorter` 更简单）。

**⑨** The algorithm work had to wait.

↩︎ **平实版**：The method work had to wait.（`the algorithm work` → `the method work`；`had to wait` → `had to be put off`）

**⑩** Honestly, I felt quite low about it.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样说（`felt quite low about it` → `felt pretty bad about it`／`was in a bad mood`）。

**⑪** I had not finished my list, and underneath that, I saw that it had been far bigger than my ability.

↩︎ **平实版**：I had not finished my list, and deeper down I saw that it was much more than I could really do.（`underneath that` → `deeper down`；`far bigger than my ability` → `much more than I could really do`）

**⑫** The only reassuring part was that one thing got done.

↩︎ **平实版**：The one thing that made me feel better was that one thing got done.（`the only reassuring part` → `the one thing that made me feel better`）

**⑬** Frustrating though it was, it taught me to plan around the time I really have, not the time I wish I had.

↩︎ **平实版**：It was frustrating, but it taught me to make a plan with the time I really have, not the time I wish I had.（让步倒装 `Frustrating though it was` → 平铺 `It was frustrating, but …`；`plan around` → `make a plan with`；`not the time I wish I had` 这个对比留着，很好用）

> 💡 平实版是**兜底**，不是目标：示范作答里的升级词记得住就照样用（那些是评分里的加分点）；平实版只保证你想不起难词时**不卡住、不中断**。整段讲顺了，再回来把词一个个升上去。

## 🔍 高分句（背下来就能用）

1. [[\h|the turning point came one evening, when I picked up my phone and ended up flailing on it for two hours]] —— 课件 Target 点名要「一个清楚的转折点」，这句就是：`the turning point came + 时间` 把转折钉在一个**具体画面上**；`pick up my phone` 与后面 `end up flailing on it` 形成自嘲式反差。
2. [[\h|when I opened my calendar, only a few days were left, and the time was clearly not favourable]] —— 把「来不及了」拆成**一个动作画面**（翻日历）＋一句判断（`not favourable`）；`only a few days were left` 比 `I had no time` 具体得多。
3. [[\h|the plan simply bumped along from one interruption to the next]] —— `bump along from one … to the next`（勉强从一个……撑到下一个）比 `I kept getting interrupted` 具体得多；`simply` 加重「什么都做不成」的无力感。
4. [[\h|Frustrating though it was, it taught me to plan around the time I really have, not the time I wish I had]] —— 让步倒装收尾（句式说明见 [[concessive-inversion]]）；`the time I really have, not the time I wish I had` 是自我纠正式对比，正好把「计划远超能力」这个反省收进一句话。

| 我一件件都享受了 | I had one dinner after another | **indulge**（[[indulge]]） | I **indulged** myself with one family dinner after another. |

## ⚠️ 本题专属坑 / Traps

- **这题的「变化」是被打断，不是灾难**：[[perilous]] 不能用（课件明写不用于 `a late bus`、`a difficult test`、`a minor inconvenience`）；一次假期被打乱也够不上 [[troubled]]（课件：一次性事件用 `difficult` / `stressful` 更自然）。
- **题干原词别照搬**：`change my plans`、`unexpectedly` 换成 `my plan had to be reworked`、`my plan fell apart`、`out of the blue`（详见「固定开场模板」）。
- **转折点必须说清**：课件 Target 明确要 `one clear turning point`——必须有一句把「事情从这儿开始变」钉住（`the turning point came when …`），只罗列一堆打断不算；这一句也是本题最好背的高分句。
- **两个情境词要修饰「情境」**：situation words 评的是**条件、处境、迹象**（`the plan looked promising`、`the conditions were not favourable`），不能用来形容人的心情——❌ `I was favourable`、❌ `I was promising`。
- **「计划」可数**：`a plan` / `my plans` / `make a plan`；❌ `make plan`、❌ `do a plan`。
- **「落下的课」**：`catch up on the lectures I had missed` / `what I had fallen behind on`；❌ `the classes I lost`。
- **`put off` 才是「拖着不做」**：`papers I had been putting off`；❌ `putting out`（那是「扑灭」）。
- **「精力分散」的地道说法**：`my energy was cut into small pieces`（自造比喻，说得清就行）／`I was spread too thin`（被摊得太薄）／`my attention was all over the place`；❌ `my energy was separated`。
- **「需要专注」**：`it needed long, focused hours` / `it was deep work`；❌ `it needed long time focus`。
- **「没做完」的说法**：`I never got through the list` / `I did not finish what I had set out to do`；❌ `I failed my plan`。
- **「计划远超能力」**：`the list was far bigger than my ability` / `I had planned beyond my own capacity` / `I had overestimated my free time`；❌ `my plan surpassed my ability`（surpass 多用于好的超过）。
- **「郁闷」的分寸**：`felt quite low` / `felt frustrated` / `was in a bad mood`；`I was depressed` 太重（像抑郁），对一件小事别用。
- **算法落地**：`get the algorithm from my paper working` / `put the algorithm into practice` / `implement the algorithm`；❌ `land the algorithm`（中文直译）。
- **时态**：假期前的清单与落下的课是「过去的过去」——`I had written out`、`I had missed`；被打断的常态用一般过去；`by the time I opened my calendar, only a few days were left`。
- **别把模板句和话题句混在一起**：`such as` 后只接名词短语（`missing a train`、`losing a weekend trip`），不要接 `I missed …` 这样的完整句。

## 🎤 交付提示 / Delivery Notes

- **句重音**：压在 three big items、went well、piled up、one interruption after another、the turning point、only a few days、not favourable、one item、far bigger than my ability、the time I really have 上——考官靠这些词抓故事线与反思。
- **停顿**：`But then …` 之前停半拍（转折开始）；`The turning point came one evening` 之前再停一下（课件要的那个 clear turning point 要让人听出来）；说 `for two reasons` 之后停一拍，再报两条原因。
- **语速**：SETTING 段（清单三件事）可略快、一口气说完；CHANGE 段放慢，说出「一件接一件」的无力感；反省段（Honestly …）用平缓、偏低的语调。
- **情绪**：这题是「郁闷＋自省」，别讲成灾难，也别一路抱怨——最后一句要落在**学到了什么**上，把分数收在反思里。
- **填充词**：卡顿时用 `honestly`、`I have to admit`、`the thing is` 拖时间。
- **降调收尾**：最后一句用降调落下来（… not the time I wish I had），给出「故事讲完了」的信号。

## 🔁 追问演练 / Part 3 Follow-ups

1. **Why do people often make plans they cannot keep?** → 开场：I suppose it comes down to …（多半是因为……）；可用词块：aim too high、be over-optimistic about time、plan for the person we wish we were。
2. **Is it always a bad thing when plans have to be changed?** → 开场：There's no denying that …, but …（不可否认……但是……）；可用词块：a turning point、work out well、adapt to、see it as a chance to reset。
3. **How can students manage their time better?** → 开场：On balance, I'd say …（总的来说）；可用词块：break a big task into small steps、protect a few quiet hours、say no to things、plan around your real energy。
