# Describe a time when you had to change your plans unexpectedly

> **来源**：课件 PPT「PRACTICE TOPIC 03 · Direction 3: an unexpected change of plan」——题干、4 条提示、`SETTING / CHANGE / ACTION / RESULT` 四槽位规划框架与 `Target: use two situation words naturally and include one clear turning point.`；OCR 全文见 `docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md`（幻灯片 1），原图 `docs/ref/ppt/2dc24721f1ed401a0e507b5a218f3fa9.png`；本题可用的语言表见同课件「Useful language for different situations」（幻灯片 3）。
> **素材**：用户本人口述（2026-09-30）——要坐高铁回家，跟朋友约好在一家网上很火的餐厅吃饭（那家店他早就期待）；我以为时间够，提前一小时出门，结果路上每一段都比预估久：从宿舍走到地铁站花了 15 分钟、进站后还等了 6 分钟地铁、出站挤人群＋检票＋安检又花 15 分钟（原本的打算是「地铁 30 分钟 ＋ 到站留 30 分钟」），于是彻底赶不上，只能改签下一班并把晚饭往后推；朋友很失望，为了弥补他，我请他吃了这一餐。｜**素材沿革**：2026-09-23 版讲的是「假期计划被家务、聚餐、出门玩一件件打乱」，因老师认为该例太普通，2026-09-30 整体替换为本故事（旧版正文不再保留，相关词条引用已同步调整）。
> **题型**：Speaking Part 2 经历题（unexpected change of plan）｜目标时长 1′40″–2′00″（约 230–260 词 @ 120–130 wpm）｜示范作答约 262 词（⚠ 比 260 词上限多 2 词，待压缩）（全篇第一人称叙述）。
> **说明**：情节按用户口述逐条保留（15 分钟步行 / 6 分钟等车 / 30 分钟车程＋30 分钟余量 / 出站＋检票＋安检 15 分钟 / 改签 / 推迟吃饭 / 请客弥补）；示范作答没有复述这笔账，而是把它压成一句内心独白（`"Half an hour to spare — I'll be fine," I told myself`）＋路上逐段报出的 15 / 6 / 15 分钟；日期与车次不写；英文为示范转写（非用户原文）；示范作答中的升级词块按库内词条组织，对应关系见「词块与升级」。

## 📋 题目 / Task

**Describe a time when you had to change your plans unexpectedly.**

You should say:
- what you planned to do
- what caused the change
- what you did instead
- how the situation ended

**课件的语言表（同课件「Useful language for different situations」，按情境分两组）**

| 情境 | 课件写的 | 中文 | 课件例句 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **POSITIVE OR IMPROVING** | `rewarding` / `worthwhile` | 值得的、有收获的 | The experience was challenging but rewarding. |
| | `reassuring` / `comforting` | 令人安心的 | Her calm response was extremely reassuring. |
| | `work out well` / `end successfully` | 结果顺利 | Fortunately, everything worked out well. |
| | `turning point` / `key moment` | 转折点 | That phone call became a turning point. |
| **DIFFICULT OR UNCERTAIN** | `tense` | 气氛紧张 | The atmosphere became tense. |
| | `awkward` | 别扭、尴尬 | I found myself in an awkward situation. |
| | `under pressure` | 在压力之下 | I had to decide while under pressure. |
| | `take a turn for the worse` | 变得更糟 | The weather took a turn for the worse. |

> 🎯 **课件 Target：用上两个「情境词」，并给一个清楚的转折点**。同一套课件有 [[perilous]]、[[promising]]、[[troubled]]、[[favourable]] 四张情境词卡（词卡 OCR 见 `docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md` 幻灯片 2 / 5 / 6 / 7）；课件**没有**指定本题配哪两张（Direction 2 的题卡明写 `perilous situation`，本题题卡没有这一行），所以本条按语义选取 [[promising]]（出门时纸面上的时间安排看着可行）与 [[favourable]]（赶到车站时形势已经不利）当两个情境词。
> ⚠️ [[perilous]] 与 [[troubled]] 本题**都不能用**：这既不是危险情境，也够不上「困苦、多灾」——一次误车就是 `a stressful morning`（课件原话：`For a temporary event, difficult or stressful often sounds more natural.`）。

## 🧭 四槽位规划 / Planning Frame（SETTING → CHANGE → ACTION → RESULT）

| 槽位 | 本故事对应内容 | 关键英文词块 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **SETTING** | 要坐高铁回家，跟朋友约好去一家网上很火的餐厅吃饭——那家店他期待已久；高铁 6 点开，我提前一小时从宿舍出发，自己对自己说「还有半小时，来得及」 | catch my high-speed train home / a table at a restaurant that had gone viral online / he had been looking forward to it for weeks / I left an hour early / "Half an hour to spare — I'll be fine," I told myself |
| **CHANGE** | 纸面上的时间安排看着完美，实际每一段都被拖慢：走到地铁站 15 分钟、又等 6 分钟地铁、出站挤人群＋检票＋安检再 15 分钟；**我站在原地想「完了」**；**转折点**——赶到检票口，那趟车已经停止检票 | on paper the timing looked promising / things went wrong at every turn / neither of which I had counted in / the crowd and the checks had eaten another fifteen / I stood there thinking, "I'm not going to make it" / the timing was clearly not favourable / the turning point came when … / the train had stopped taking passengers |
| **ACTION** | 时间压力下只能改签下一班，并给朋友发消息把晚饭往后推（消息原文一并给出） | under pressure / rebook on the next one / texted my friend, "I'm so sorry, I missed it. Can we push dinner back?" |
| **RESULT** | 朋友很失望——那家店是他唯一期待的事；电话那头一阵沉默（我听得见的尴尬），唯一能弥补的方式就是请他吃这一餐（我买单）；教训是「纸面上的时间」不等于「真实的时间」，要给路上留缓冲 | he was really disappointed / the silence on the line was awkward / make it up to him / treat him to the meal / plan on paper vs. real time / leave a margin |

**四问覆盖检查**：what you planned to do＝提前一小时出门，按「步行 ＋ 30 分钟地铁 ＋ 到站留 30 分钟」赶 6 点的高铁｜what caused the change＝路上每一段都比预估久（宿舍到地铁站 15 分钟、等车 6 分钟、出站检票安检 15 分钟），赶不上那趟车｜what you did instead＝改签下一班，并把跟朋友的晚饭往后推｜how it ended＝朋友很失望、场面尴尬，我请他吃这一餐收场，也学会了给路上留缓冲。四条全部落在上面四槽位里，不必另编内容。

## 🗣️ 固定开场模板 / Fixed Opening（必须套用）

> 老师给的固定开头，**答题第一句就得说它**；`...` 处按自己的素材填空：

```text
Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there are/were ... , such as ... . However, if I had to / were to choose one / pick one, I would say that is/was ...  /  I would definitely go for ...
```

**本故事的填空版**：

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there **have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked**, such as **missing a bus**, or **having a flight delayed**. However, **if I had to pick one, it would be the evening I missed my train home.**

<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：`such as` 后的两个例子（missing a bus ／ having a flight delayed）是为套模板举的一般性例子，非用户亲历；实际作答请换成自己的经历 -->

> 💡 模板的语法开关：素材讲**一般情况／多个例子** → `there are` + `that is`；素材讲**一件过去的事** → `there were` + `that was`。本故事前半句铺「不止一次」（到现在为止的经历）用 `there have been`，真正要讲的那一件是过去的事，所以落到 `it would be`／`that was`。`such as` 后面只接**名词短语**，不要接完整句子；**例子举 2–3 个**——前面说的是 `plenty of times`，只举一个撑不起来。
> 🔁 **开场句备用版**（`if I had to pick one` 说腻了就换一个）：
> - `However, if I had to pick one that stands out, it would be ...`——「挑一个最出挑的」；
> - `However, the most memorable one was definitely ...`——直接点出「最……的那一个」。
> ⚠️ **题干原词别照搬**：题目写的是 `change your plans` 与 `unexpectedly`，答案里就**不要**再说 `change my plans` / `unexpectedly`——照抄题干原词**很扣分**（考官一听就知道在背题）。换成：`my plan had to be reworked`、`my plan fell apart`、`I had to rethink what I could realistically get done`、`out of the blue`。

## ✍️ 示范作答 / Model Answer（约 2 分钟）

Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked, such as missing a bus, or having a flight delayed. However, if I had to pick one, it would be the evening I missed my train home.

A friend and I had booked a table at a restaurant that had gone viral online, and he had been looking forward to it for weeks. My train left at six, so I left an hour early, telling myself, "Half an hour to spare — I'll be fine" — on paper, the timing looked [[promising]].

But things went wrong [[at-every-turn|at every turn]]. The walk ate fifteen minutes, the next train six more — neither of which I had counted in — and by the time I got to the station, the crowd and the checks had eaten another fifteen. I stood there thinking, "I'm not going to make it" — the timing was clearly not [[favourable]].

The turning point came when I got to the gate — my train had stopped taking passengers. Under pressure, I did the only thing I could: I rebooked on the next one and texted my friend, "I'm so sorry, I missed it. Can we push dinner back?"

He was really disappointed — the silence on the line was awkward — and the only way to make it up to him was to treat him to the meal.

[[concessive-inversion|Promising though the timetable looked on paper]], it had no room for anything to go wrong — so now I always leave a margin.

> 💡 **视角提示（第一人称）**：老师爱听的是「我」在经历，而不是「事情」在发生。凡是能让「我」去经历的，就别让物当主语说完整句——❌ `The walk to the metro stop took fifteen minutes.` → ✅ `The walk ate fifteen minutes … and I stood there thinking, "I'm not going to make it."`；❌ `He was really disappointed, and it was awkward.` → ✅ `He was really disappointed — the silence on the line was awkward.`（尴尬是「我」听见的）。再加一句说出口的原话（`"I'm so sorry, I missed it."`），故事立刻活了。

## 🔁 平实版对照 / Say It in Plain English

> **为什么加这一节**：示范作答里有不少「专有词 / 文雅词」，说不出来不等于讲不了这个故事。下面**逐句**配一个**平实版**——只用常见词与常见句型，意思和原句**完全一样**；临场卡住就说平实版，内容一点不打折（考官评的是「讲清楚」，不是「用大词」）。
> **怎么读**：每对里，上面一行是示范作答的原句（原句里的词条跳转已省略，便于对照），下面 `↩︎ 平实版` 是你可以直接说的版本；括号里标出「哪个词被换掉了」。

### 1. 逐句对照（共 11 句）

**①** Well, before talking about this topic, I would like to say there have been plenty of times when my plans had to be reworked, such as missing a bus, or having a flight delayed.

↩︎ **平实版**：原样照说即可——这是老师给的固定模板，本身就是常见词（`such as` 后举两三个例子：只说一个撑不起前面的 `plenty of times`，接名词短语、不接句子）；`reworked` 想不起来就说 `had to be sorted out again`（`change` 是题干原词，也尽量别照念）。

**②** However, if I had to pick one, it would be the evening I missed my train home.

↩︎ **平实版**：However, if I had to choose one, it would be the evening I missed my train home.（`pick one` → `choose one`；点题只说「错过高铁」就够——故事讲的是赶车，晚饭被推迟留在 ACTION 段再说，省下的词留给内心话）

**③** A friend and I had booked a table at a restaurant that had gone viral online, and he had been looking forward to it for weeks.

↩︎ **平实版**：A friend and I had booked a table at a restaurant that was really famous online, and he had wanted to go there for weeks.（`had gone viral online` → `was really famous online`；`had been looking forward to it` → `had wanted to go there`）

**④** My train left at six, so I left an hour early, telling myself, "Half an hour to spare — I'll be fine" — on paper, the timing looked promising.

↩︎ **平实版**：My train left at six, so I went out an hour early — I thought I would have enough time to spare — and on paper the time was enough.（`I thought I would have enough time to spare` 就是「我以为来得及」，直说就行，不必绕成直接引语；`looked promising` → `was enough`；`left an hour early` → `went out an hour early` 更像口语）

**⑤** But things went wrong at every turn.

↩︎ **平实版**：But something went wrong at every step.（`at every turn` → `at every step`／`again and again`）

**⑥** The walk ate fifteen minutes, the next train six more — neither of which I had counted in — and by the time I got to the station, the crowd and the checks had eaten another fifteen.

↩︎ **平实版**：The walk took fifteen minutes, and the next train six more — I had not counted either of them in — and when I got to the station, getting through the crowd and the checks took another fifteen minutes.（`ate` → `took`；`neither of which I had counted in` 这个定语从句架子留着，想不起来就说 `I had not counted either of them in`；`the checks` 想不起来就展开成 `the ticket check and the security check`）

**⑦** I stood there thinking, "I'm not going to make it" — the timing was clearly not favourable.

↩︎ **平实版**：I stood there thinking, "I'm not going to make it", and things were clearly not going my way.（把心里那句话直接说出来（`I stood there thinking …`）——考官要的就是这个第一人称的瞬间；`not favourable` → `not going my way`）

**⑧** The turning point came when I got to the gate — my train had stopped taking passengers.

↩︎ **平实版**：The key moment came when I got to the gate — my train had already closed.（`the turning point came` → `the key moment came`；`had stopped taking passengers` → `had already closed`；破折号后面直接给结果，比 `and saw that …` 更急——听到的就是当时的反应）

**⑨** Under pressure, I did the only thing I could: I rebooked on the next one and texted my friend, "I'm so sorry, I missed it. Can we push dinner back?"

↩︎ **平实版**：I had no time to think, so I changed to the next train and sent my friend a message: "I'm so sorry, I missed it. Can we move dinner to later?"（`under pressure` → `I had no time to think`；`rebooked` → `changed to`；`texted` → `sent … a message`；`push dinner back` → `move dinner to later`；消息原话照念就行，直接引语最省力也最生动）

**⑩** He was really disappointed — the silence on the line was awkward — and the only way to make it up to him was to treat him to the meal.

↩︎ **平实版**：He was really disappointed, and the silence on the phone felt awkward; the only way to make it up to him was to pay for the meal.（`the silence on the line was awkward` → `the silence on the phone felt awkward`——把「尴尬」放在「我听到的那阵沉默」上，比 `it was awkward` 有画面；`treat him to the meal` → `pay for the meal`）

**⑪** Promising though the timetable looked on paper, it had no room for anything to go wrong — so now I always leave a margin.

↩︎ **平实版**：The timetable looked fine on paper, but it had no room for any problem, so now I always leave extra time.（让步倒装 `Promising though the timetable looked on paper` → 平铺 `The timetable looked fine on paper, but …`；`no room for anything to go wrong` → `no room for any problem`；`leave a margin` → `leave extra time`）

> 💡 平实版是**兜底**，不是目标：示范作答里的升级词记得住就照样用（那些是评分里的加分点）；平实版只保证你想不起难词时**不卡住、不中断**。整段讲顺了，再回来把词一个个升上去。

## 🔍 高分句（背下来就能用）

1. [[\h|things went wrong at every turn]] —— 把「一路被拖慢」压成四个词：`at every turn`（每一步、每一次）比 `I kept being late` 具体得多；后面再用三个数字（15 / 6 / 15 分钟）把「每一次」逐个兑现，逻辑立刻严密起来。
2. [[\h|"Half an hour to spare — I'll be fine," I told myself]] —— 「我以为来得及」平铺直叙也行（`I thought I would have enough time to spare`）；想说得更有画面，就把这句心里的估算变成自己对自己说的话（`I told myself`），后面再跟 `on paper, the timing looked promising` 点出「纸面上的计划」——考官听见的是**你真的算过时间**，还听见了你当时的信心。
3. [[\h|the turning point came when I got to the gate — my train had stopped taking passengers]] —— 课件 Target 点名要「一个清楚的转折点」，这句就是：`the turning point came when + 一个动作画面` 把转折钉死在检票口（破折号后面直接给结果，比 `and saw that …` 更急）；`had stopped` 用过去完成，说明「我到的时候它已经停了」。
4. [[\h|Promising though the timetable looked on paper, it had no room for anything to go wrong]] —— 让步倒装收尾（句式说明见 [[concessive-inversion]]）：先承认计划本身没毛病（`promising`），再一刀切到「容不下任何意外」；`no room for anything to go wrong` 比 `I was too optimistic` 更有画面。

## 🧰 词块与升级 / Chunks & Upgrades

### 1. 课件关键词（必须会说）

| 课件写的 | 读法要点 | 在答案里怎么用 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **turning point** | /ˈtɜːnɪŋ pɔɪnt/，重音落 **TUR-** | **The turning point came** when I got to the gate — my train had stopped taking passengers.（把转折钉在一个画面上——课件 Target 指定要这一句） |
| **under pressure** | /ˌʌndə ˈpreʃə/，重音落 **PRES-** | **Under pressure**, I rebooked on the next one and texted my friend.（时间紧、只能当场决定时用；也可说 `with the clock running`） |
| **take a turn for the worse** | /teɪk ə tɜːn fə ðə wɜːs/ | That was when things **took a turn for the worse**: the walk ate fifteen minutes and the next train six more.（事情开始变坏的那一句；本故事可放在 CHANGE 段开头） |
| **awkward** | /ˈɔːkwəd/，重音落 **AW-**，结尾 -ward 轻读 | It was **awkward**, because he had been looking forward to that restaurant for weeks.（「场面别扭、不知怎么开口」时用；比 `embarrassing` 轻） |

### 2. 库内词条升级（可直接替换平实说法）

| 想说的意思 | 平实说法 | 升级 | 例句 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 出门时纸面上时间够用 | the time looked enough | **promising**（[[promising]]） | On paper, the timing looked **promising**: a short walk, a thirty-minute ride, and half an hour to spare. |
| 路上每一步都被拖慢 | something went wrong at every step | **at every turn**（[[at-every-turn]]） | Things went wrong **at every turn** — the walk, the wait, the checks. |
| 赶到时形势已经不利 | things were not going my way | **favourable**（[[favourable]]） | By the time I got to the station, the timing was clearly not **favourable**. |
| 纸面上的安排再好看也留不住意外 | the plan looked fine, but it broke | **让步倒装**（句式见 [[concessive-inversion]]） | **Promising though the timetable looked on paper**, it had no room for anything to go wrong. |
| 人群与安检「吃掉」了时间 | the crowd and the checks took another fifteen minutes | **eat another fifteen minutes**（地道动词，库内暂无词条） | The crowd and the checks **had eaten another fifteen minutes**. |
| 面对时间压力 | with no time to think | **under pressure**（课件词，库内暂无词条） | **Under pressure**, I did the only thing I could and rebooked on the next one. |
| 改签到下一班车 | change to a later train | **rebook on the next one**（地道搭配，库内暂无词条） | I **rebooked on the next one** and texted my friend. |
| 请客、赔罪 | say sorry and pay for the meal | **make it up to sb / treat sb to sth**（地道搭配，库内暂无词条） | The only way to **make it up to him** was to **treat him to the meal**. |

> ⚠️ 分寸提醒：**awkward** 说「场面别扭」刚刚好，但它不表示「内疚」——想说自己心里过意不去要说 `I felt bad about it`。**rebook** 只用在「改签、重新订」这个意思上（`rebook a ticket / a train`）；**push back** 是「往后推」，宾语可以是 `dinner / the meeting / the deadline`。

## ⚠️ 本题专属坑 / Traps

- **这题的变化不是灾难**：[[perilous]] 不能用（课件明写不用于 `a late bus`、`a difficult test`、`a minor inconvenience`）；也够不上 [[troubled]] —— 一次误车说 `a stressful morning` / `a messy start` 就够。
- **题干原词别照搬**：`change my plans`、`unexpectedly` 换成 `my plan had to be reworked`、`my plan fell apart`、`out of the blue`（详见「固定开场模板」）。
- **转折点必须说清**：课件 Target 明确要 `one clear turning point`——必须有一句把「事情从这儿定局」钉住（`the turning point came when I got to the gate …`），只说「每一段都慢了」不算；这一句也是本题最好背的高分句。
- **两个情境词要修饰「情境」**：situation words 评的是**条件、处境、时机**（`the timing looked promising`、`the timing was not favourable`），不能用来形容人的心情——❌ `I was favourable`、❌ `I was promising`。
- **`favourable` 的拼写与搭配**：英式 `favourable`（美式 `favorable`）；常配 `conditions / timing / weather + be favourable`；❌ `the time was favourable for me to catch the train`（绕口，直接说 `I still had time`）。
- **「时间够不够」的地道说法**：`I had half an hour to spare` / `I left an hour early` / `I cut it too fine`（时间掐得太紧）；❌ `I had enough free time`。
- **「赶上车／没赶上」**：`catch my train` / `miss my train` / `miss it by minutes`；❌ `fail the train`。抢时间说 `rush for the train`，别说 `run the train`。
- **`count in` 是「算进去」**：`I had not counted the walk in` / `I had not allowed for it`；❌ `I had not calculate it`（时态与搭配都错）。
- **「出站＋检票＋安检」别逐字硬译**：`getting through the crowd and the checks` / `the ticket check and the security check`；❌ `check the ticket and check the safe`。
- **「吃掉了 15 分钟」的动词**：`had eaten another fifteen minutes` / `cost me another fifteen minutes` / `took up another fifteen minutes`；❌ `eaten my 15 minutes time`。
- **改签**：`rebook on the next one` / `change to a later train` / `get the next one`；❌ `change my ticket to later train`（缺冠词、语序生硬）。
- **「把饭局往后推」**：`push dinner back` / `move dinner to later` / `put dinner off by two hours`；❌ `delay our eat`。
- **「请他吃」与「弥补他」是两个词**：`treat him to the meal`（我请客）＋`make it up to him`（弥补）——两者都要把「他」放在宾语位；❌ `pay the meal for him`。
- **时态**：出发前的打算与路上没算到的东西都是「过去的过去」——`I had not counted that in`、my train `had stopped` taking passengers；不要全用一般过去平铺。
- **别把模板句和话题句混在一起**：`such as` 后只接名词短语（`missing a bus`、`having a flight delayed`），不要接 `I missed …` 这样的完整句；例子给 **2–3 个**，只举一个太单薄。

## 🎤 交付提示 / Delivery Notes

- **句重音**：压在 an hour early、looked promising、at every turn、fifteen minutes、six minutes、another fifteen minutes、not favourable、the turning point、stopped taking passengers、rebooked、really disappointed、awkward、margin 上——考官靠这些词抓故事线与那三个数字。
- **停顿**：`But things went wrong …` 之前停半拍（转折开始）；三个数字（15 / 6 / 15）每个后面短停一下，让「一次次被拖慢」听得出来；`The turning point came when …` 之前再停一下（课件要的那个 clear turning point 要让人听出来）。
- **语速**：SETTING 段（约饭＋提前出门＋纸面账本）可略快、一口气说完；CHANGE 段的三个数字放慢、逐个说清；RESULT 段（朋友失望、请他吃饭）用平缓语调，别越说越急。
- **情绪**：这题是「慌＋歉意」，别讲成灾难，也别一路抱怨——最后一句要落在**学到的做法**上（现在一定要留缓冲），把分数收在反思里。
- **填充词**：卡顿时用 `honestly`、`I have to admit`、`the thing is` 拖时间。
- **降调收尾**：最后一句用降调落下来（… so now I always leave a margin），给出「故事讲完了」的信号。

## 🔁 追问演练 / Part 3 Follow-ups

1. **Why do people often make plans they cannot keep?** → 开场：I suppose it comes down to …（多半是因为……）；可用词块：cut it too fine、be over-optimistic about time、count only the ride and forget the walk。
2. **Is it always a bad thing when plans have to be changed?** → 开场：There's no denying that …, but …（不可否认……但是……）；可用词块：a turning point、work out well、make it up to sb、see it as a chance to reset。
3. **How can students manage their time better?** → 开场：On balance, I'd say …（总的来说）；可用词块：leave a margin、count the whole journey in、break a big task into small steps、say no to things。
