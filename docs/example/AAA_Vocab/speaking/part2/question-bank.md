# Speaking Part 2 题卡池

> **来源**：由已练过的三张题卡派生——`speaking/part2/experience_1.md`（课件 Direction 1：a positive situation）、`speaking/part2/experience_2.md`（Direction 2：a dangerous or difficult situation）、`speaking/part2/experience_3.md`（Direction 3：an unexpected change of plan）；题干框架与 Useful language 沿用课件 OCR（`docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md`）。
> **A 组**＝同一个故事换张题卡（不必新编素材，只换点题句与收尾句）；**B 组**＝同方向的新素材题（需另配经历，逐题标出素材缺口）。
> **两条纪律**：① 题干原词不在答案里重复（`good news` / `change my plans` / `unexpectedly`）——换说法见 §5；② 换题后必须重新对一遍四条提示，哪一槽位分量变了就重排（情绪题要把 FEELING 拉长、把 SETTING 压到两句）。

---

## 1. A 组 · 故事一：转机狂奔（原题＝a time you received some good news）

**A1　Describe a journey that did not go as planned.**
- where you were going / what went wrong on the way / what you did about it / how the journey ended
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be our journey home at the end of a family trip — which nearly ended at a transfer counter.
- 🎯 只换点题句与收尾：SETTING（没有直飞、第一程晚起飞）与 CHANGE（跨航站楼狂奔）原样可用；收尾落在「旅程的结局比预想的好」，把 `wonderful news` 那几句换掉。

**A2　Describe a time when you felt relieved.**
- what the situation was / why you were worried / what happened in the end / how you felt afterwards
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the moment I heard our connecting flight had been delayed — I do not think I have ever felt so relieved.
- 🎯 FEELING 要拉长（a huge wave of relief / a weight coming off my shoulders / my legs were still shaking），SETTING 压成两句；`relieved` 是题干词，答案里改说 `a weight coming off my shoulders`。

**A3　Describe a time when you had to hurry.**
- when it was / why you were in a hurry / what you did / what the result was
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the fifty minutes we spent running from one end of a huge airport to the other.
- 🎯 落点从「好消息」挪到「抢时间」：把三个时间数字（起飞晚 / 落地只剩五十分钟 / 转机延误一小时）讲清就够，`I kept telling myself they would hold the plane` 是这题的画面中心。

## 2. A 组 · 故事二：漂流翻船（原题＝a difficult or dangerous situation）

**A4　Describe a time when someone helped you.**
- who helped you / what the situation was / what they did / how you felt about their help
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the lifeguard who pulled me out of the water after our raft flipped over in a whirlpool.
- 🎯 救生员要**从一句变成一段**：poled his raft over / held out his pole / pulled me up / left me on the raft and swam to the bank himself；「我」的应对压成一句（stay calm and keep my head above the water）。

**A5　Describe an activity you found exciting but a little risky.**
- what the activity was / where you did it / why it was risky / whether you would do it again
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be that rafting trip in Guizhou — the most exciting afternoon of my life, and the closest I have ever come to real danger.
- 🎯 最后一问是「还会不会再去」——必须正面回答（`Not that stretch, no. On a gentler river with a proper guide, maybe.`），不要拿「敬畏生命」代替。

**A6　Describe a time when you followed someone's suggestion.**
- whose suggestion it was / what it was / what happened / whether you would follow it again
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the day I went along with my friend's idea and said nothing while he poled our raft onto a rock in the middle of the river.
- 🎯 这题的落点是「我当时的判断」：要点出「我本该说一句」（`I should have said something`），把责任放到自己身上，故事才有反思。

## 3. A 组 · 故事三：误高铁（原题＝you had to change your plans unexpectedly）

**A7　Describe a time when you were late for something.**
- what you were late for / why you were late / what you did / how it ended
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the evening I missed my train home.
- 🎯 三个数字（走到地铁站 15 分钟 / 等车 6 分钟 / 出站检票安检 15 分钟）原样可用；点题句从「计划被打乱」换成「我迟到了」。

**A8　Describe a time when you had to apologise to someone.**
- who you apologised to / what had happened / how you apologised / how they reacted
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the apology I had to make after I missed dinner with a friend.
- 🎯 「道歉」要有具体动作：那通电话＋原话（`I'm so sorry, I missed it. Can we push dinner back?`）＋弥补方式（`the only way to make it up to him was to treat him to the meal`）；对方的反应（`the silence on the line was awkward`）是这题的高分点。

**A9　Describe a meal you had with a friend that you still remember.**
- where you ate / who you were with / what the occasion was / why you remember it
- 📌 点题句：However, if I had to pick one, it would be the dinner I ended up paying for — the one I nearly did not make.
- ⚠️ **素材偏薄**：现有故事只写到「请他吃这一餐」，没有餐厅、菜品、氛围。要答这题得先补料（哪家店 / 点了什么 / 当时聊了什么），否则撑不满两分钟。

## 4. B 组 · 同方向的新题（9 题，需另配素材）

### Direction 1 · a positive situation

**B1　Describe a time when you were proud of yourself.**
- what you did / why it was difficult / how you felt / what others said
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：现有三个故事都够不上「为自己骄傲」（漂流是别人救的、误车是失误）。候选：一次考出好成绩 / 自己搞定的旅行安排 / 教会长辈用某个应用。
- 📌 点题句示范：... it would be the first time I handled a whole trip on my own, at nineteen.

**B2　Describe something you did that helped someone.**
- who you helped / what the problem was / what you did / how you felt afterwards
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：与 A4 互为镜像——把救生员的角色换成「我」（帮陌生人搬行李 / 给迷路的人带路 / 帮室友处理搬家琐事）。
- 📌 收尾落点：`what surprised me was how little it cost me and how much it seemed to matter to him.`

**B3　Describe a piece of good news you heard from someone else.**
- what the news was / who told you / when you heard it / how you reacted
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：与 `experience_1.md` 相反——那条是「我收到消息」，这条是「我被告知别人的好消息」（朋友考上 / 家人搬家）。**人称要换**：主角是他，我只负责反应。
- 📌 点题句示范：... it would be the evening my best friend rang me to say he had got the job.

### Direction 2 · a dangerous or difficult situation

**B4　Describe a time when you got lost.**
- where you were / how you got lost / what you did / how you found your way back
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：库内暂无成稿（`experience_2.md` 的模板里只把 `getting lost in the mountains` 当过例子）。要真答这题得有一次真实的迷路：山里 / 陌生城市 / 商场停车场。
- 📌 可用语言：`we took a wrong turning` / `the path had disappeared under the snow` / `I had no signal at all`。

**B5　Describe a time when the weather changed your plans.**
- what you had planned / what the weather did / what you did instead / how you felt about it
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：候选是「旅行遇暴雨改行程」或「台风取消航班」。**可借经验**：`experience_3.md` 的「计划被一路拖慢」的叙事骨架完全可搬，只把原因换成天气。
- 📌 点题句示范：... it would be the afternoon a thunderstorm wiped out everything we had booked.

**B6　Describe a time when you had to make a quick decision.**
- what the situation was / what you had to decide / what you chose / what the result was
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：漂流翻船那一刻（要不要跟水流硬抗）可以借，但需说清「决定」是什么；也可以讲赶车时「改签还是硬冲」。
- 📌 要有一句「分岔口」：`For a second I had two options, and neither of them was good.`

### Direction 3 · an unexpected change of plan

**B7　Describe a time when you changed your mind about something.**
- what you had decided / what made you change it / what you did instead / whether you were glad you changed
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：「change my mind」与「plan 被打乱」不同——这题要有**一个明确的反悔时刻**（本来不想参加某个活动 / 本来打算学某个专业）。
- 📌 点题句示范：... it would be the week I decided to drop the course I had fought to get into.

**B8　Describe a time when a plan with a friend fell through.**
- what you had planned / why it fell through / what you did / how your friend reacted
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：可借 `experience_3.md` 的尾段，但原因要换成「对方的缘故」（他临时有事 / 票卖完了）。
- 📌 落点：`he rang me two hours before, and there was nothing anyone could do.`

**B9　Describe a time when you had to wait for a long time.**
- where you were / who you were with / why you had to wait / how you passed the time
- ⚠️ 素材缺口：转机那段（等延误通知）可借一点点，但「等待」本身库内没有细节（等了多久、怎么打发）。
- 📌 点题句示范：... it would be the two hours we spent at a gate that kept saying "delayed".

## 5. 换题不换故事：四句改写公式

| 位置 | 保留还是改写 | 怎么做 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ① 开场模板 | 原样 | 模板句一字不动，只把 `such as` 后的例子与最后的点题句换成新题目 |
| ② 点题句（第一段最后一句） | **必改写** | 结构＝`However, if I had to pick one, it would be + 故事名`；故事名要挂住题干关键词（journey / lifeguard / apology），但不照抄题干原词 |
| ③ 中段叙事骨架 | 原样 | SETTING → CHANGE（含一个清楚的 turning point）→ ACTION 三槽位不动，只调整各段长度 |
| ④ 收尾反思句 | **必改写** | **直接回答题干最后一条提示**：问 feeling 就写 feeling，问 learned 就写教训，问 would you do it again 就正面表态 |
| ⑤ 让步倒装收尾 | 原样可用 | `Promising though the timetable looked on paper, …` / `Terrifying though it was, …` / `Perilous though that half an hour was, …` |

> ⚠️ **换题三坑**：① 照抄题干原词（`good news`／`changed my plans`／`unexpectedly`）；② 四条提示少答一条——换题后槽位分量变了，最容易被挤掉的永远是最后一问；③ 素材复用过度，同一段故事在两题里几乎逐句相同（串题训练时逼自己换开头与收尾）。

> 🧰 常用情境词与句式：[[perilous]]、[[promising]]、[[favourable]]、[[concessive-inversion]]、[[at-every-turn]]。

## 6. 怎么练（四种走法）

1. **90 秒换题**：同一个故事换三张题卡，每题只讲 90 秒——只练「点题句 + 转折点 + 收尾句」，不追求完整。
2. **抽签连答**：从 A 组随机抽两题连着说，中间不给准备时间——练临场重组与词汇复用。
3. **换人称／换时态**：把故事讲成「我朋友的故事」（he did …），或把好新闻那题改成「如果我当时没赶上会怎样」（虚拟语气 `If I had missed it, I would have …`）。
4. **录音回听**：只查三件事——点题句是否落在第一段最后、转折点有没有独立的一句、收尾有没有正面回答最后一问。
