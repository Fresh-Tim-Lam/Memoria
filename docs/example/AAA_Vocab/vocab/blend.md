# blend
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /blend/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /blend/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: ˈblend → single‑syllable, full stress
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: initial /bl‑/ cluster is often rushed in fast speech; do not confuse with "bland /blænd/" (boring, tasteless) or "blend" vs "blind /blaɪnd/".
  (中文说明：开头 /bl-/ 在快读时容易一带而过；注意区分 blend（融合）与 bland（乏味的）、blind（盲的）。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, countable, usually singular]** a mixture of different things that combine well（融合体；混合体）
- **Speaking example** – My hometown is a fascinating blend of ancient temples and glass skyscrapers.
  （我的家乡是古庙与玻璃幕墙摩天楼交融的迷人混合体。）
- **Writing example** – Modern urban design aims for a blend of functionality and visual appeal.
  （现代城市设计追求功能性与视觉美感的融合。）

- **[verb, transitive / intransitive]** to mix something with something else so that they become one（使混合；使融合）
- **Speaking example** – This district blends traditional architecture with modern street art.
  （这个街区把传统建筑与现代街头艺术融为一体。）
- **Writing example** – Immigrants gradually blend into the local community while keeping their own traditions.
  （移民在保留自身传统的同时逐渐融入当地社区。）
