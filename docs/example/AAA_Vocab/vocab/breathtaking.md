# breathtaking
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˈbreθteɪkɪŋ/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˈbreθteɪkɪŋ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第一音节 **BREATH** — **BREATH**‑ta‑king
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the "-th" is /θ/ (voiceless), never /ð/ or /t/; the compound is often written as one word, so be careful with the spelling — not "breath-taking" in British usage and never "brethtaking".
  (中文说明：-th 读清辅音 /θ/，不读 /ð/ 或 /t/；该词为合成词，注意拼写：breathtaking（易错拼为 brethtaking）。)
---
## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[adjective]** extremely beautiful or impressive, so that it takes your breath away（令人叹为观止的；美得令人窒息的）
- **Speaking example** – The view from the top of the mountain was absolutely breathtaking.
  （山顶的景色美得令人窒息。）
- **Writing example** – The region is famous for its breathtaking scenery, which draws visitors from all over the world.
  （该地区以令人叹为观止的风光闻名，吸引着世界各地的游客。）
