## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌklɪəˈkʌt/   (based on Oxford / Cambridge)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌklɪrˈkʌt/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 复合形容词**双拍**——次重音落在 **CLEAR**、主重音落在 **CUT**（ˌCLEAR‑ˈCUT）；两个成分都是实词，哪个也不弱读
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: three things happen at once in *everything's not so clear cut*. ① In the audio the phrase is **one stress unit**, not two words: British `clear` ends in /ə/ and `cut` starts with /k/, so the pair fuses into [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|/klɪə-kʌt/]] — the ear should catch **two beats**, not four syllables. ② `so` is unstressed and very short, and `not so` runs together as /nɒtsə/; miss it and you lose the **degree** — `not so clear-cut` means 没那么界限分明, which is the whole point of the sentence. ③ `everything's not …` sounds like a blanket negative but is meant as a **partial** one: hear it rewritten as [[\h:#14532d:#a7f3d0|not everything is so clear-cut]]. The tail `haven't been fully grasped` then packs /vn bɪn/ and the /spt/ cluster, so the passive ending is easy to drop.
  (中文说明：三件事同时发生——① `clear cut` 在音频里**粘成一个重音单位**，英式的 clear 收在 /ə/、下一词以 /k/ 起头，听时该抓**两拍**而不是四个音节；② `so` 弱读极短，`not so` 常连成 /nɒtsə/，一漏就丢掉**程度下调**——「not **so** clear-cut」＝「没那么界限分明」，而这正是全句要点；③ `everything's not …` 听着像全盘否定，实际是**部分否定**，要在心里改写成 `not everything is so clear-cut`；紧接的 `haven't been fully grasped` 把 /vn bɪn/ 与 /spt/ 挤在一处，被动语尾最容易丢。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: 用户提供的雅思听力材料（Q29 段落原句）＋ Oxford / Cambridge / Merriam‑Webster（库内暂无该词语料）

- **[adj] clear-cut** – definite and easy to understand; so clearly defined that there is no doubt about it（明确的、不含糊的；界限分明的）
  (中文释义：两个成分就是它的意思——`clear`（清楚）＋ `cut`（切）＝ **切得干干净净**，切口一望便知 ⇒ [[\h:#fff3cd:#5c4a00|界限分明]]。两个用法面：**判断面**（答案、结论无争议）与**界线面**（两类东西分得清）。)
  - **Listening example（用户材料原句，逐字）** – But everything's not so clear cut in a child's world, and abstract concepts such as honesty haven't been fully grasped by young children.
    (中文翻译：但在孩子的世界里，事情并不是那么**界限分明**；而像「诚实」这类抽象概念，小孩子也还没有完全弄明白。)
  - **Speaking example** – Whether a child is lying or just imagining is not always clear-cut.
    (中文翻译：孩子是在撒谎还是只是在想象，并不总是分得那么清。)
  - **Writing example** – The boundary between advice and instruction is rarely clear-cut in a classroom.
    (中文翻译：课堂上，建议与指令之间的界线很少泾渭分明。)
- **[adj] 定语 vs 表语（拼写要点）** – 作**定语**时几乎一律带连字符：`a clear-cut answer`；作**表语**时连字符常省去，材料里就写作 `everything's not so clear cut`——加不加连字符都不改变词性或意思。
  (中文说明：所以「clear cut 分开写」并不算错；但名词前一律补上连字符。)

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
> **为何放在这个位置**：它决定「这个词该不该用在这里」——`clear-cut` 讲的是**界线是否分明**，既不讲难易，也不讲对错。
- **✅ Best situation**:
  1. **听力 / 阅读里判断立场**：作者写 `not so clear-cut` 就是在说「别指望一刀切」——这类句子后面往往紧跟反例（本句就接「抽象概念孩子 grasp 不了」）。
  2. **口语 Part 3 表达分寸**：`It's not that clear-cut.` / `The line isn't clear-cut.`——比 `It depends.` 更像成年人说话，比 `It's complicated.` 更具体。
  3. **写作提「区分不易」**：`The distinction between the two is not clear-cut.` / `a clear-cut answer`——让「不能简单二分」这个论点有词可用。
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「难」 → 用 `difficult` / `tricky`；`clear-cut` 管的是**界线**，不管难度
  - ✗ 想说「含糊、没定论」 → 用 `vague`（本身说不清）或 `a grey area`（地带说不清）；`not clear-cut` 是**界线两侧都有东西**，不等于「含糊」
  - ✗ 名词前漏连字符 → ✓ `a clear-cut answer`（❌ `a clear cut answer`——读的人先看见「切」这个动作）
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 口语 `not that clear-cut` → 中性 **`clear-cut`** → 书面 `unambiguous` / `well-defined`
- **来源 Source**: 用户听力材料原句 ＋ Oxford / Cambridge（库内 data/sources/** 暂无该词语料）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 构词：`clear`（清楚的，形容词）＋ `cut`（切，过去分词）＝ **adj + p.p.** 型复合形容词；重音因此落在后件 `cut` 上<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：构词与重音说明按通用词典与常见读音整理，未核原书 -->
- 同型词：`short-lived`、`hand-written`、`half-baked`——一律**后件重读**、一律带连字符
- **Memory hook**: 一刀下去切口平平整整，是哪一半一眼就分得清——这就叫 clear-cut。
- 一句话分两层：**判断明确无争议**（a clear-cut answer）→ **界线分明**（a clear-cut division）——都从「切得干净」来。

|:---|:---|:---|:---|

| Formality (English + 中文) | neutral-formal；口语与书面通吃 | neutral；口语里常带批评（It's not that black-and-white.） | informal → neutral；书面里偏口语 |
| Typical collocates (English + 中文) | a clear-cut answer / case / distinction / victory | a black-and-white view / issue / world；in black and white（白纸黑字） | a cut-and-dried decision / answer / procedure |

> 最关键的一格：**`black-and-white` 说的是「只有两档」，`clear-cut` 说的是「界线清晰」**——孩子的世界不是「只有两档」（所以本句能用 `not so clear-cut`），但也不等于成人看得清；作者在意的是**界线**，不是**档数**。

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
In a child's world, honesty and imagination are not so __________ cut.
（中文提示：在孩子的世界里，「诚实」和「想象」并没有那么清楚的分界）
### 2. Speaking scenario
**Is it always clear-cut who is telling the truth?**（口语 Part 3 追问）
（中文说明：先给判断句 `It's not that clear-cut.`，再补一个**具体场景**把界线弄糊——`With young children, what we call lying is often just imagination.`，最后落在结论 `So the line has to be drawn case by case.`）
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 sentence arguing that a policy cannot be judged in black-and-white terms.
（中文说明：写一句大作文，说明某项政策不能用非黑即白的方式评判）
> 💡 例：However convenient a clear-cut rule may be, the lived reality of families is rarely clear-cut.（一条界限分明的规则再方便，家庭的实际处境也很少那么分明。）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using 'clear-cut', [[not-the-least-bit]] and [[take-sb-at-their-word]].
（中文提示：用 clear-cut、not the least bit、take sb at their word 合成一句，例如写「孩子被问话时是非界线一点也不分明，可大人却往往不核实就采信他们的话」）
