# cricket
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˈkrɪkɪt/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˈkrɪkɪt/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 双音节，重音在**第一音节** — **CRI**cket /ˈkrɪkɪt/；词尾 -et 弱读为 /ɪt/，不读 /eɪ/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the final **-et** is weak and the closing /t/ is often unreleased, so *a cricket* can sound like *a crickets*; and because **cricket** carries two unrelated senses (a sport / an insect), only the neighbouring words tell them apart — a match, a pitch, a bat vs chirping, in the grass.
  (中文说明：词尾 -et 弱读、收尾 /t/ 常不爆破，`a cricket` 听起来像 `a crickets`；更要紧的是 cricket 有两个毫不相干的义项——**板球**（a cricket match／a cricket bat）与**蟋蟀**（crickets chirping／crickets in the grass），只能靠旁边的词判断。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary / Cambridge Advanced Learner’s Dictionary；本库源文件 `data/sources/vocab_source1.txt` 第 556、1803 行（两个义项分别收录），`data/sources/vocab_source2.txt` 第 556、1805、1808 行

- **[noun, uncountable] Sense 1 — the sport**: a game played by two teams of eleven on a grass field, in which a player tries to hit a ball bowled towards them with a flat wooden bat and score runs（板球）
- **Speaking example** – I have just learned a new sport, cricket: it looks slow, but the moment the ball is bowled everything happens at once.
  （我刚学了一项新运动——板球：看着慢，可球一投出来，所有事都发生在同一瞬间。）
- **Writing example** – Cricket is played from village squares to international stadiums, and a single match can last five days.
  （从乡村广场到国际球场都有人打板球，一场比赛可以持续五天。）
  <!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：「一场可打五天」是 Test cricket 赛制的通用背景知识，源文件与库内材料未载 -->

- **[noun, countable] Sense 2 — the insect**: a small brown or black insect that lives in grass, jumps, and makes a loud high‑pitched sound at night by rubbing its wings together（蟋蟀；蛐蛐儿）
- **Speaking example** – We left the window open, and all we could hear was crickets in the grass.
  （我们开着窗，只听见草丛里的蟋蟀声。）
- **Writing example** – A cricket in the house is good luck.
  （屋里有蟋蟀是吉兆。出处：`data/sources/vocab_source2.txt:556`，源文件原句。）
  <!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：源文件只给例句、未给英释；「吉兆」属民间说法，非词典义项 -->

> 💡 用法提示：`cricket` **两个义项毫无关系**，靠搭配分辨，而且**可数性不同**：
> ① **运动义（不可数）**—— play cricket ／ a cricket match ／ a cricket pitch ／ a cricket bat
> ② **昆虫义（可数）**—— a cricket chirping ／ crickets in the grass ／ crickets at night
> ⚠️ 源文件例句 *The cricket match was washed out my rain*（`data/sources/vocab_source2.txt:1805`）**疑漏介词**，应为 *washed out **by** rain*（比赛因雨取消）；此处照录原文并标注，未据以改写。

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 口语 Part 2「Describe a sport you would like to learn」——cricket 是**小众但有文化厚度**的选项（学一项新爱好＝a rewarding hobby，见 [[rewarding]]）② Part 3／写作谈**体育文化、英联邦历史**：cricket 常作为殖民遗产被引用（见 [[legacy]]）③ 描写夏夜环境音：the chirping of crickets ④ 读／听英国、印度、澳洲材料时的背景词
- **∇ Avoid**: ① 泛指「运动」时别把 cricket 当 `sport` 用——它只指板球这一项 ② 对美式听者提 cricket 时补半句话（a bat‑and‑ball sport popular in the UK and South Asia），否则对方第一反应是 **baseball** ③ 想写「害虫／虫子」用 `pest`／`insect`，cricket 只指蟋蟀这一类

- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 泛指 `a sport / a ball game` → 具体 `cricket` → 环境描写档 `the chirping of crickets`（昆虫义的文学用法）

### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 词源：经古法语 **criquet** 进入英语，多认为源自拟声词根 **crik-**（模仿蟋蟀「唧唧」的鸣声）——**昆虫义在先**，板球义是后世另起的义项，两义同形属同形词。<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：词源沿革按通用词源资料整理，未核原书 -->
- 派生：`cricketer`（板球运动员）、`cricketing`（形容词，如 a cricketing nation）、`crickets`（复数；另见词族表末行）
- **Memory hook**: Same letters, two pictures — a green pitch with a bat, or a dark field at night full of chirping. Ask yourself one question: is there a **bat** in the sentence, or a **chirp**?

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `criket` —— 漏掉第二个 **c**，正确拼写是 **cricket**（c‑r‑i‑c‑k‑e‑t）。（出处：用户 2026-10-05 会话原话「我学到一个新的运动criket」；错题本条目 `err-20261005-001` 由 `.memoria/agent/scratch/log-cricket-error.py` 运行后登记进 `.memoria/agent/review/errors.json`）
- ✗ 把运动义当可数用：`I watched a cricket yesterday.` —— **a cricket 是一只蟋蟀**，比赛要说 a cricket match。（依据：Sense 1 不可数／Sense 2 可数，见上文释义）
- ⚠️ 冠词：球类运动前不加 the —— play cricket ／ play football（语法通则，非本词特例）。
