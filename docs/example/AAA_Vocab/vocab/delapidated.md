# dilapidated
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue

- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /dɪˈlæp.ɪ.deɪ.tɪd/ 
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /dɪˈlæp.ə.deɪ.t̬ɪd/ 
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第二音节 **LA** — di-**LA**-pi-day-tid 

- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: In fast speech, the final `-ted` is often reduced to a soft `/təd/` or even `/ɾəd/` (American flap T), making it sound like "day-did" rather than "day-tid". The middle syllable `pi` may also be reduced to a schwa `/pə/`.
  （在快速口语中，结尾的 `-ted` 常被弱读为 `/təd/` 甚至美式闪音 `/ɾəd/`，听起来像“day-did”而非“day-tid”。中间的 `pi` 也可能弱化为 `/pə/`。）


## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)

> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary 

- **\[adjective\]** (of furniture and buildings) old and in very bad condition
  （（指家具或建筑）破旧的，年久失修的）

- **Speaking example** – The house at the end of the street looks so dilapidated that I’m afraid it might collapse.
  （街尾那栋房子看起来破败不堪，我担心它会塌掉。）

- **Writing example** – Many historic buildings in the old town have fallen into a dilapidated state due to a lack of government funding for preservation.
  （老城区的许多历史建筑因缺乏政府修缮资金而变得破败不堪。）

### Morphology / Root (memory aid)

- Root `lapid` = stone（源自拉丁语 *lapis* = 石头）
- Prefix `di-` = apart / away（分开、散开）
- 字面含义：**把石头都打散开了** → 建筑散架了 → 破败不堪

- **Memory hook**: Imagine a building so old that even the **stones (lapid)** have fallen **apart (di-)**. That’s dilapidated.
  （想象一栋老建筑，连石头都散开了——那就是 dilapidated。）

## 🎯 Active Output Drills

### 1. Gap‑fill
After years of being left untouched, the old warehouse had become completely __________, with holes in the roof and rusted metal beams.
（经过多年无人照管，那座旧仓库已变得完全__________，屋顶有洞，金属梁也锈蚀了。）

### 2. Speaking scenario
Describe a building in your city or hometown that is in very bad condition. Explain what it looks like, why it has fallen into that state, and what should be done about it.
（描述你所在城市或家乡一栋状况很差的建筑。说明它看起来什么样、为什么会变成这样、以及应该如何处理。）
> 💡 Use **dilapidated** at least twice.

### 3. Writing scenario
**IELTS Writing Task 2**: Some people believe that governments should spend money on preserving old buildings, while others think it is better to demolish them and build new ones. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
（雅思写作任务2：有人认为政府应花钱保护老建筑，另一些人则认为拆掉建新的更好。讨论双方观点并给出你的看法。）
> 💡 Use **dilapidated** to describe buildings that are beyond repair, and **dilapidation** as a noun.

### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Create a short paragraph (4–5 sentences) using **at least three** of the following: *dilapidated*, *dilapidation*, *renovate*, *neglect*, *restore*.
（用至少三个以下词汇写一段短文：dilapidated, dilapidation, renovate, neglect, restore。）
> （中文提示：描述一个社区决定改造一片破败的旧工厂区）
