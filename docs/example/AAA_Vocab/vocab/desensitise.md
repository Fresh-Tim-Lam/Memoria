# desensitise
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌdiːˈsensətaɪz/   (based on Oxford / Cambridge)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌdiːˈsensətaɪz/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: de·**SEN**·si·tise —— 主重音在第二音节 /ˈsen/；前缀 `de‑` 弱读 /di/，`‑tise` 读 /taɪz/
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: `de‑` is a weak /di/, never /deɪ/ and never stressed; the ending /taɪz/ is **the same sound** for `‑tise` and `‑tize`, so the sound alone will not tell you which spelling the exam wants; in `desensitise us`, the /z/ links onto `us` and the three syllables collapse into two. Spelling trap: build it in order — `sensitive` → `sensitise` → `desensitise` — the middle `s` is single.
  (中文说明：`de‑` 弱读、不重读；词尾 /taɪz/ 两种拼法同音，听到声音分不出 `‑tise` / `‑tize`，英式写 `‑ise`。拼写按 `sensitive → sensitise → desensitise` 加，中间的 `s` 只有一个。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford / Cambridge 体例整理；话题语境取自本课 L19-2「坏新闻」脑暴（课件词汇，尚未落库 ⇒ 例句均标 ⚠）
- **[v., transitive] Sense 1 — to make somebody less sensitive to something, especially by exposing them to it many times**（使（对某事物）不再敏感、变麻木；尤指反复接触之后）
  (中文释义：关键在于「反复接触」——不是天生冷漠，而是**被喂出来的**反应消失。所以主语常常是媒体、游戏、暴力画面这类「反复投喂」的东西，宾语是人或公众。)
- **[v., transitive] Sense 2 — (medical) to make a person less sensitive to a substance such as an allergen**（（医学）使脱敏）
  (中文释义：医学义是中性的「脱敏治疗」，同一个词褒贬全靠语境——`desensitise a patient to bee stings` 是好事，`desensitise children to violence` 是坏事。)
> **Speaking example (Sense 1)** – I think we've all been desensitised to bad news; another disaster barely registers any more.
> （中文翻译：我觉得我们对坏新闻都麻木了；再一场灾难也几乎激不起反应。）⚠ 自撰示例，待核
>
> **Writing example (Sense 1)** – Constant coverage of war and crime can desensitise the public to human suffering.
> （中文翻译：对战争与犯罪的持续报道会使公众对人间苦难变得麻木。）⚠ 自撰示例，待核

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① **坏新闻／暴力内容的反复曝光**：`repeated exposure desensitises children to violence`（本课话题） ② **公众对灾难的反应退化**：`audiences have been desensitised to human suffering` ③ **医学脱敏（中性）**：`desensitise a patient to bee stings` ④ **批评平台算法与流量逻辑**：`an algorithm that desensitises users to shock`
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「我习惯了」→ 用 `be accustomed to` / `get used to`：中性，只讲**行为与心理的适应**
  - ✗ 想说「身体没感觉了」→ 用 `numb`：`My fingers went numb.`
  - ✗ 想说「适应新环境／新规则」→ 用 `adjust to` / `adapt to`；本词不管环境
  - ✗ 想说「减轻疼痛／钝化刺激」→ 用 `dull` / `numb the pain`；`desensitise` 的宾语是**人**，不是痛感
- **强度标记 Strength**: 社会语境 **strong（贬义）**；医学语境 **neutral**
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 口语 `get used to it` → 通用 `stop reacting to it` → 正式／评论（本词）`be desensitised to …`
- **来源 Source**: 义项边界按 Oxford / Cambridge；话题语境取自本课 L19-2 的「坏新闻」脑暴（课件词汇，尚未落库）⚠

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 词根 **sent** / **sens**（拉丁 `sentire` ＝ to feel，感觉）→ `sense`、`sensation`、`sensitive`、`consensus` 全是这一族。<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：词源按通用词源资料整理 -->
- 构词：`de‑`（去掉、取消）＋ `sensitise`（使有感觉）→「把感觉开关关掉」。
- 拼写链：`sensitive`（形容词）→ `sensitise`（动词）→ `desensitise`（再套一层 de‑）→ `desensitisation`（名词，注意 `‑tise` 变 `‑tisa‑`）。
- **Memory hook**: `de‑` 是「拆掉」，`sensitive` 是「会疼的那根神经」——拆掉神经，人还在看，心已经不在场。

## ⚔️ Confusion Matrix (with Chinese explanations)
Compare with *be accustomed to*, *numb*, *inure* — 分家的地方是「**消失的是什么**」：accustomed 消失的是**陌生感**，desensitise 消失的是**情感反应**，numb 是**当下**没感觉，inure 是同一件事的书面版。
| Dimension | This word: desensitise | Synonym 1: be accustomed to | Synonym 2: numb |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Core nuance (English + 中文) | 反复刺激后**不再震惊、不再同情**（道德评价：不好） | 对新情况**不再陌生**（中性，纯适应） | 生理或情绪上**此刻无感**（状态，不强调过程） |
| Formality (English + 中文) | 正式／评论，医学义中性 | 中性，口语书面皆可 | 口语与新闻皆可（形容词更常见） |
| Typical collocates (English + 中文) | be desensitised **to** violence / suffering；desensitising effect | be accustomed **to** doing sth；get used to | numb **with** cold / shock；go numb |
| IELTS example (English + 中文翻译) | Years of crime reporting have desensitised viewers to violent images.（多年的犯罪报道让观众对暴力画面麻木了。） | Children quickly become accustomed to a new school.（孩子很快习惯新学校。） | My hands were numb with cold after an hour outside.（在外面待了一小时，我的手冻得没感觉了。） |
（中文说明：一句话记——`accustomed` 说「我不陌生了」，`desensitised` 说「我不会再被震动了」，`numb` 说「此刻我没感觉」。媒体题要表达的正是第二句，前面加 `become / be` 用被动：`audiences have been desensitised to …`，介词只能是 **to**。）

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
After months of graphic reporting, many viewers have been __________ to images of natural disasters.
（中文提示：经过数月血淋淋的报道，许多观众对自然灾害的画面已经麻木）
### 2. Speaking scenario
**Part 3: Do you think the media reports too much bad news?**
（中文说明：正面回答「是」，再用本词说明后果（`we get desensitised, so real suffering stops moving us`），最后给解法（`more good news would rebalance it`）——三步一段，Part 3 最稳。）
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 body sentence explaining why constant bad news is harmful.
（中文说明：大作文写一句因果——主语放「反复曝光」，宾语放「公众」，介词落到 `to`。）
> 💡 例：When war, crime and disasters fill the screen every night, constant exposure **desensitises** audiences **to** suffering that would once have shocked them.（当战争、犯罪与灾难每晚占满屏幕，持续的曝光使观众对本来足以震惊他们的苦难变得麻木。）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using **desensitise**, [[cynical]] and [[mainstream]].
（中文提示：三词合成一句——主流媒体喂料，人先麻木、后愤世）
> 💡 例：**Mainstream** outlets that lead with shock value may **desensitise** their audience long before it turns **cynical** about the world.
