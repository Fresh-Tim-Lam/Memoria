# heritage
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˈherɪtɪdʒ/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˈherɪtɪdʒ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第一音节 **HER** — **HER**‑i‑tage
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the final "-tage" is /tɪdʒ/, a soft "j" sound, not /teɪdʒ/; do not confuse with "hermit /ˈhɜːmɪt/" (隐士).
  (中文说明：词尾 -tage 读 /tɪdʒ/（软 j 音），不读 /teɪdʒ/；勿与 hermit（隐士）混淆。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, uncountable, usually singular]** the traditions, buildings, art and culture that a society has inherited from the past（遗产；底蕴；文化传统）
- **Speaking example** – My city is proud of its rich cultural heritage, especially its ancient temples.
  （我所在的城市以丰富的文化遗产为傲，尤其是那些古庙。）
- **Writing example** – Preserving cultural heritage is essential for maintaining a strong sense of national identity.
  （保护文化遗产对于维持强烈的民族认同至关重要。）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `her` / `hes` = heir, inheritance（拉丁语 *heres* = 继承人）
- 后缀 `-age` = 名词后缀（集合、状态）
- 字面含义：**由继承而来的一切** → 遗产、传承
- **Memory hook**: What you **inherit** from the past becomes your **heritage**.
  （从过去继承下来的一切，就是你的 heritage。）
