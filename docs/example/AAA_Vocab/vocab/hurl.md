# hurl
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /hɜːl/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /hɜːrl/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: ˈhurl → single‑syllable, full stress
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: single syllable; in fast speech /h/ may weaken; do not confuse with “hurtle /ˈhɜːtl/”
  (中文说明：单音节词，快读时h音弱化；区分hurl投掷 和 hurtle猛冲)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary
For each sense:
- **[verb, transitive] Definition 1** – to throw something with a lot of force (猛扔；用力投掷)
  (中文释义：用力猛掷，猛扔)
- **Speaking example** – Protesters began to hurl stones towards the building.
  (中文翻译：抗议者开始朝大楼猛扔石块。)
- **Writing example** – Angry crowds hurled objects at police during the street disturbance.
  (中文翻译：骚乱期间愤怒的人群向警察投掷物品。)

- **[verb, transitive] Definition 2** – to shout insults or criticism at somebody angrily (大声呵斥；怒斥；恶语相向)
  (中文释义：愤怒地大声辱骂、抨击)
- **Speaking example** – Some online users hurl abuse at strangers for minor disagreements.
  (中文翻译：部分网民因一点分歧就对陌生人恶语相向。)
- **Writing example** – Politicians often hurl accusations against their opponents during campaigns.
  (中文翻译：竞选期间政客常常向对手大肆发难。)

- **[verb, intransitive, informal] Definition 3** – to vomit (呕吐，非正式)
  (中文释义：呕吐，口语非正式用法)
- **Speaking example** – He hurled after eating spoiled seafood.
  (中文翻译：他吃了变质海鲜后吐了。)
- **Writing example** – *Rare in formal writing, mostly spoken*
  (中文翻译：极少用于正式写作，仅限口语)

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `hurl‑` = to cast violently (古英语，暴力抛出) (中文：古英语本源，表猛烈抛出动作)
- Affixes: no productive affixes; past/past participle: hurled; present participle: hurling
- Memory hook: You hurl heavy things hard; you can also hurl harsh words. (中文记忆提示：既可以猛扔实物，也可以猛烈抛出难听话语)
