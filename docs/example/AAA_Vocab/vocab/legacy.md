# legacy
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **IPA**: /ˈleɡəsi/ | /ˈleɡəsi/
- **Stress**: first syllable (leg‑)
- **🗣️ Listening trap**: final syllable reduced to /si/, do not confuse with `legislation`. Common linking: `cultural legacy` /ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈleɡəsi/ (尾音节弱读，勿与legislation立法混淆)
---
## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Cambridge Advanced Learner’s Dictionary
- **[noun, countable]** something that is the result of events in the past (历史遗留产物；遗产，精神文化遗产)
- **Sense 1: cultural/historical heritage**
- **Speaking example** – Visiting ancient sites helps us understand our cultural legacy.
  (参观古迹帮助我们理解自身的文化遗产。)
- **Writing example** – The war left a painful legacy of social division across the region.
  (这场战争给该地区留下了社会分裂的痛苦遗留问题。)

- **[noun, countable/uncountable]** money or property left to someone in a will (遗嘱留下的金钱、实物遗产)
- **Speaking example** – He received a large legacy from his grandfather.
  (他从祖父那里继承了一大笔遗产。)
- **Writing example** – Much of the museum’s collection came from private legacies donated by wealthy families.
  (博物馆大量藏品来自富裕家族捐赠的私人遗赠。)
