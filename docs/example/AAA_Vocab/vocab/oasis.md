# oasis
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /əʊˈeɪsɪs/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /oʊˈeɪsɪs/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第二音节 **EIS** — o‑**A**‑sis
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the plural is irregular — "oases" /əʊˈeɪsiːz/, with the final sound changing from /s/ to /z/; learners often wrongly say "oasises".
  (中文说明：复数不规则，oases 读 /əʊˈeɪsiːz/，词尾由 /s/ 变 /z/；不要错说成 oasises。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, countable] Sense 1** – a small area in a desert where water and plants are found（沙漠中的绿洲）
- **Speaking example** – The travellers finally reached an oasis after three days in the desert.
  （旅行者在沙漠中走了三天后终于抵达一处绿洲。）
- **Writing example** – Ancient trade routes depended on a chain of oases across the desert.
  （古代商路依赖沙漠中一连串的绿洲。）

- **[noun, countable] Sense 2 (figurative)** – a pleasant, peaceful place or moment in the middle of something busy or unpleasant（喻指喧嚣或困境中的一片净土、慰藉之所）
- **Speaking example** – That little café is an oasis of calm in the middle of the shopping district.
  （那家小咖啡馆是购物区中的一片宁静净土。）
- **Writing example** – Urban parks act as oases of greenery in densely built cities.
  （城市公园在建筑密集的城市中充当一片片绿意盎然的绿洲。）
