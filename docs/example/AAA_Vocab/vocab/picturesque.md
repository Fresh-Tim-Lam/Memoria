# picturesque
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌpɪktʃəˈresk/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌpɪktʃəˈresk/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第三音节 **RESK** — pic‑ture‑**RESQUE**
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the middle "-tur-" is swallowed and sounds like /tʃə/, so the word comes out as "pick‑chuh‑RESK"; the final "-esque" is /esk/, not /eskjuː/.
  (中文说明：中间的 -tur- 被吞音成 /tʃə/，听感近似 “pick-chuh-RESK”；词尾 -esque 读 /esk/。)
---
## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[adjective]** (of a place, building or view) pretty and charming, like a picture（风景如画的；优美的）
- **Speaking example** – There is a picturesque fishing village on the coast where the houses are painted in bright colours.
  （海边有个风景如画的渔村，房屋都刷成鲜艳的颜色。）
- **Writing example** – The picturesque old town attracts millions of visitors, which places great pressure on its narrow streets.
  （这座风景如画的老城吸引数百万游客，给狭窄的街道带来巨大压力。）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `pict` = to paint, picture（拉丁语 *pingere* = 绘画）
- 后缀 `-esque` = in the style of（…风格的，如…一般的）
- 字面含义：**像画一样** → 风景如画的
- **Memory hook**: A **picturesque** view is one worth turning into a **picture**.
  （picturesque 的风景，就是值得画下来的一景。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `a picturesque` 后面直接接 `scenery` 并加冠词 —— `scenery` 为**不可数名词**，不说 a scenery / sceneries。
- ✓ `picturesque scenery` / `a picturesque view`

（中文提示：这个村庄风景如画，像明信片一样）
### 2. Speaking scenario
Describe a beautiful place you have visited. Describe what you saw there and how you felt.
（中文说明：口语 Part 2 地点题，描述一处你去过的美丽之地，说明所见与感受）

（中文说明：大作文写一句话，论述旅游业对风景如画老城的影响）

（中文提示：用 picturesque、lush、secluded、heritage 合成一句）
