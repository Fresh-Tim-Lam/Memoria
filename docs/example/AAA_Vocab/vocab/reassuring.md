# reassuring

- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌriː.əˈʃʊr.ɪŋ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster / Oxford US)

  (中文说明：重音在 -sur- 上，前面的 re- 与词尾 -ing 都弱读；英式 /ʃʊə/ 里的 /r/ 常被连读带出，美式卷舌更明显——别与 assuring、ensuring 听混。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: 库内语料 data/sources/vocab_source2.txt:3958（reassure|v.|使安心；使放心|When the child was afraid in the storm, his parents reassured him）＋ Oxford / Cambridge / Merriam‑Webster 词典释义

- **[adjective] Sense 1** – making you feel less worried about something（使人安心的；令人放心的）
  (中文释义：重点在**消掉一个具体的担心或疑虑**——检查结果、一句「已经处理好了」、数据好转，都能是 reassuring。)
- **Speaking example** – The doctor's calm voice was reassuring, so I finally stopped worrying about the results.
  （医生平静的语气让人安心，我终于不再担心检查结果了。）
- **Writing example** – The latest employment figures are reassuring: unemployment has fallen for the third quarter in a row.
  （最新的就业数据令人放心：失业率已连续三个季度下降。）

- **[adjective] Sense 2** – (of words or actions) intended to remove sb's doubts（（话语、举动）宽慰人的；给人定心丸的）
  (中文释义：这一支落在**人与话**上，如 a reassuring smile / tone / presence；它**不承诺结果**，只表示「你不必再担心」。)
- **Speaking example** – The coach said nothing definite, but she was a reassuring presence on the sidelines.
  （教练没给出什么定论，但她在场边就是一种让人安心的存在。）
- **Writing example** – It is reassuring to know that the money is held in a separate account.
  （得知这笔钱存在独立账户，令人放心。）

- **[verb] reassure sb (that) …** – to say or do sth to make sb feel less worried（使安心；使放心）
  (中文释义：宾语是**人**——reassure the passengers / the public / investors；库内语料原句即此用法。)
- **库内语料例句** – When the child was afraid in the storm, his parents reassured him.
  （暴风雨中孩子害怕时，父母安慰他、让他放心。）
- **Writing example** – Governments must reassure the public that their personal data is properly protected.
  （政府必须让公众放心：他们的个人数据受到妥善保护。）

- **[noun] reassurance** – words or actions that make sb feel less worried（安慰的话；宽慰、保证）
- **Writing example** – Students need constant reassurance that their progress is being tracked fairly.
  （学生需要不断得到宽慰：他们的进步被公平地记录着。）

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 口语「担心一件事、后来放下心」的经历：*The doctor's voice was reassuring.* ② 描述**数据、报告、检查结果**：*The test results were reassuring.* ③ 写作：*It is reassuring to know that …* ／ *a reassuring sign* ④ 危机沟通：*reassure the public*
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 表达**情感上的抚慰**（朋友失恋、亲人去世）→ 用 `comforting`（a comforting hug）——reassuring 消掉的是**担心／疑虑**，不是悲伤
  - ✗ 想说自己**有信心** → 用 `confident`（reassuring 是「**让别人**放心」）
  - ✗ 想说「令人鼓舞／前景看好」→ 用 `encouraging` / `promising`（[[promising]]）——reassuring 是「**不再担心**」，encouraging 是「**多了干劲**」
  - ✗ 想说「（这个人）很会安慰人」→ 用 `supportive` 或 `a reassuring presence`（reassuring 修饰**话、语气、消息**比修饰人本身更自然）
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral（中性偏温和）**——它不表示「事情变好了」，只表示「**我不再担心了**」；常被 `very / quite / deeply` 修饰
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `That makes me feel better.` / `Don't worry.` → 通用 **`reassuring` / `comforting`** → 正式／新闻 `heartening`（令人宽慰、振奋）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ⚠️ **拼写**：reassure = re + assure，**双 s**：❌ reasure / reassureing；形容词 **reassuring**，❌ reassurring。
- ⚠️ **worry 的拼写**：worry → **worrying / worried**（直接加 ‑ing，**不双写 r**）：❌ worring / worred。（这正是本次原句的笔误）
- ⚠️ **‑ing / ‑ed 的方向**：`What he said was reassuring`（他的话**让我**安心）vs `I felt reassured`（**我自己**安心了）；❌ I was reassuring by his words → ✓ I was reassured by his words.
- ⚠️ **宾语是人**：reassure **sb**（the passengers / the public / investors）；❌ reassure the problem。
- ⚠️ **不含承诺**：想说「我保证会做到」用 `promise` / `assure`；reassure 只表示**消掉对方的担心**，不承诺义务。
- ⚠️ **别与 assuring / ensuring / insuring 混**：`assure sb that …` 向人保证｜`ensure sth` 确保结果｜`insure sth` 为…投保——三者读音接近（/əˈʃʊə/ vs /ɪnˈʃʊə/），听力要靠**句法与搭配**判断。

### 5. User Output Sample（你的输出 · 订正版）
- **你的原句**: What my friend said was so 'reassuring' that I stopped worring then.
- **订正版**: What my friend said was so reassuring that I stopped worrying.
- **要点**:
  1. 拼写：**worring → worrying**（worry ＋ ‑ing 直接加，**不双写 r**）。
  2. 句尾 `then` 去掉：`so … that …` 已经包含「于是」的意思；要强调「当场」可写 `straight away` / `on the spot`。
  3. `reassuring` 的**词性与搭配是对的**——但要认清边界：它消掉的是**具体的担心**；若朋友是在安慰你**难过／失落**的情绪，英语更常说 `comforting`。
  4. 想突出「朋友**使我**安心」这个动作，可换成动词（宾语是**人**）：*My friend reassured me so much that I soon stopped worrying.*
  5. 加分写法（把「担心什么」说清）：*What my friend said was so reassuring that I stopped worrying about the interview altogether.*
