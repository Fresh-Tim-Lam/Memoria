## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford / Cambridge；库内语料 data/sources/vocab_source2.txt:3444（仅词条与一个例句）

- **[noun, countable]** a person who fights against a government, or who refuses to obey the rules of a group（反叛者；叛逆者；起义者）
  (中文释义：既指政治／军事上的造反者，也指不服管、跟主流对着干的人——`a teenage rebel` 属后者)
- **Speaking example** – He was a bit of a rebel at school and never followed the dress code.
  （中学时他有点叛逆，从不按着装规定来。）
- **Writing example** – The rebels took control of the northern provinces after months of fighting.
  （数月战斗之后，反叛者控制了北部各省。）

- **[verb, intransitive]** to fight against or refuse to obey an authority; to dislike rules and try to change them（造反；反抗；叛逆）
- **Speaking example** – Teenagers often rebel against their parents just to show that they are independent.
  （青少年常为了证明自己独立而反抗父母。）
- **Writing example** – When young people feel shut out of decision‑making, they are more likely to rebel against the system.
  （当年轻人感到被排除在决策之外时，他们更可能反抗体制。）

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 口语人物题：`He was a bit of a rebel at school` ② 社会／教育话题：`rebel against strict rules`、`rebel against authority` ③ 写作历史政治题：`the rebels seized the capital` ④ 写作教育题：`students who rebel against rigid rules`
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 当形容词表语用（❌ `teenagers are rebel`）→ 形容词形式是 `rebellious`
  - ✗ 想说「有个性、不爱跟风」→ 用 `independent‑minded` / `a free spirit`（rebel 含**对抗权威**义，偏负面）
  - ✗ 说日常小事上的不听话（不吃青菜、不肯睡觉）→ 用 `refuse to` / `won't`（rebel 语义过重）
- **强度标记 Strength**: **strong**——含公开对抗，比 `disobey` 重；一段里用一次就够
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `disobedient` → 通用 **`rebel`** → 正式／政治 `insurgent`

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 源自拉丁语 `rebellare` = 重新开战（re‑ 再 + bellum 战争）；同源词 `belligerent`（好斗的）、`bellicose`（好战的）
- 词形变化：名词复数 **rebels**；动词 **rebelled / rebelled / rebelling**
- **Memory hook**: re‑（再次）＋ bell（战争）＝ 再次拿起武器反抗 → 名词「反叛者」、动词「反抗」；形容词另起一词 **rebellious**，表语别用 rebel。

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ⚠️ **词性**：`rebel` 只作名词／动词，❌ `teenagers are rebel`；作表语要用 `teenagers are rebellious`（形容词）或 `teenagers are rebels`（名词复数）。
- ⚠️ **及物性**：动词 rebel 不及物，反抗谁必须带 `against`：`rebel against the system`；❌ `rebel the system`。
- ⚠️ **拼写**：rebellion / rebellious 都是**双 l**，❌ rebelion / rebelious。
- ⚠️ **重音**：名词 `REBel`、动词 `reBEL`；同一段里换词性就要换重音。

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
He was a bit of a __________ at school, but he follows every rule at work now.
（中文提示：他上学时有点叛逆，现在工作上却循规蹈矩）
### 2. Speaking scenario
Talk about a time you disagreed with a rule at school or at work.
（中文说明：口语 Part 2 经历题——先用 `rebel against` 点出对抗对象，再说理由与结果）
> 💡 Use "rebel against ..." and "a bit of a rebel" once each.
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 sentence on whether schools should allow students to question their rules.
（中文说明：大作文写一句话，论述学校是否应允许学生质疑校规）
> 💡 例：Schools that punish every challenge to their rules may push students to rebel against authority rather than think critically.（把每次质疑都当违纪来罚的学校，可能把学生推向反抗权威，而不是批判性思考。）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using "rebel", [[mainstream]] and [[troubled]].
（中文提示：用 rebel、mainstream、troubled 合成一句——写一个叛逆少年后来被主流社会接纳）
### 5. User Output Sample（你的输出 · 订正版）
- **你的原句**: teenagers are 'rebel'; most teenagers 'indulge in juvenile delinquency
- **订正版**: Teenagers tend to be rebellious, and a small minority of them drift into juvenile delinquency.
- **要点**:
  1. `rebel` 是**名词／动词**，不能作形容词表语：❌ `teenagers are rebel` → ✅ `teenagers are rebellious`；若要用名词，须有限定词并用复数：`teenagers are rebels`。
  2. `most teenagers` 属**过度概括**：一句把「大多数青少年」断成违法犯罪，容易失分 → 用 `a small minority of` / `some`，或加 hedge（`tend to`、`can`）。
  3. 朗读时按词性换重音：名词 **REB**·el、动词 re·**BEL**。
