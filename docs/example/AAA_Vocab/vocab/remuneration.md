# remuneration
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /rɪˌmjuːnəˈreɪʃn/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /rɪˌmjuːnəˈreɪʃn/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第四音节 **REI** — re‑mu‑ne‑**RA**‑tion
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the first three syllables are all weak (/rɪ‑mjuː‑nə/), so the word rushes toward the stressed "-RA-"; the ending "-tion" is always /ʃn/, never /tɪən/.
  (中文说明：前三个音节全部弱读（/rɪ‑mjuː‑nə/），语流急速冲向重读音节 -RA-；词尾 -tion 一律读 /ʃn/，不读 /tɪən/。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, uncountable]** (formal) an amount of money paid to somebody for the work they have done（报酬；酬金；薪酬）
- **Speaking example** – Teachers often do a great deal of unpaid work on top of their official remuneration.
  （教师常常在正式薪酬之外做大量无报酬的工作。）
- **Writing example** – Competitive remuneration is one of the main reasons why skilled workers migrate to richer countries.
  （有竞争力的薪酬是技术工人迁往富国的主要原因之一。）

> 💡 语域提示：**remuneration 属正式书面语**，日常口语说工资用 `salary` / `pay`。雅思中它主要出现在 **Task 2 与 Part 3 的工作、就业、社会公平类**话题。

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `muner` ← 拉丁语 *munus* = gift, duty, service（礼物、职责、服务）
- 前缀 `re-` = back（回报）；后缀 `-ation` = 名词后缀（表动作、结果）
- 字面含义：**为（他人付出的）服务而回报的钱** → 报酬
- **Memory hook**: To **re**munerate is to give something **back** for a service — the same `munus` root as **com**munity, where services are shared.
  （re-（回报）+ muner（服务）= 对服务给予回报；与 community 同源，本义都关乎“服务”。）

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ✗ `renumeration` —— 高频拼写错误，误与 `numeration`（计数）混淆。正确为 **re-mu-ner-ation**（含 `muner` 词根）。
- ✗ `remuneration for do sth` —— 介词后接名词或动名词：`remuneration for doing sth` / `remuneration for work`。
- ⚠️ 语域偏差：口语中说 “I want a high remuneration” 极不自然，应说 `a good salary` / `well-paid`。

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
Many interns work long hours for little or no __________.
（中文提示：许多实习生长时间工作却几乎没有报酬）
### 2. Speaking scenario
Talk about whether people should choose a job they enjoy or one that pays well, and discuss how employers can reward their staff fairly.
（中文说明：口语 Part 3，讨论“选喜欢的工作还是高薪工作”，以及雇主如何公平回报员工）
> 💡 Use "remuneration" once and "a competitive salary" once.
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 sentence about whether there should be a legal limit on executive remuneration.
（中文说明：大作文写一句话，论述高管薪酬是否应受法律限制）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using "remuneration package", "well‑remunerated" and "skilled workers".
（中文提示：用 remuneration package、well-remunerated、skilled workers 合成一句）

> 🔗 **关联**：`remuneration` 是**账面上的报酬**，`rewarding`（[[rewarding]]）是**心里的回报**——讨论「选喜欢的工作还是高薪工作」时正好成对：*The pay was modest, but the work was rewarding.*（薪水不算高，但这份工作很有意义。）
