# renovation
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /ˌrenəˈveɪʃn/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /ˌrenəˈveɪʃn/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第三音节 **VEI** — re‑no‑**VA**‑tion
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the ending "-tion" is always /ʃn/, never /tɪən/; the first syllable is weak /ˌre/, so the word can sound like "ruh‑nuh‑VAY‑shun"; do not confuse with "renewal /rɪˈnjuːəl/".
  (中文说明：-tion 一律读 /ʃn/，不读 /tɪən/；首音节弱化为 /ˌre/，听感近似 “ruh-nuh-VAY-shun”；勿与 renewal（更新）混淆。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[noun, countable / uncountable]** the act or process of repairing and improving an old building（翻新；整修；改造）
- **Speaking example** – The old library has just reopened after a major renovation.
  （那座老图书馆经过大规模翻新后刚刚重新开放。）
- **Writing example** – Large‑scale renovation of the historic district has attracted both tourists and investors.
  （历史街区的大规模改造吸引了游客与投资者。）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `nov` = new（拉丁语 *novus* = 新的）
- Prefix `re-` = again（再一次）；后缀 `-ation` = 名词后缀（表动作、过程）
- 字面含义：**使…重新变新** → 翻新、整修
- **Memory hook**: **Re** + **nov**ation = to make something **new again**.
  （re-（再次）+ nov（新）= 把旧的重新变新。）

## ⚔️ Confusion Matrix (with Chinese explanations)
Compare with restoration, regeneration
| Dimension | This word | Synonym 1: restoration | Synonym 2: regeneration |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Core nuance (English + 中文) | repairing and **modernising** a building so it becomes usable again（翻新并现代化，使其重新可用） | returning a building or artwork to its **original condition**（把建筑或文物**还原到原貌**，强调保真） | reviving a whole **area** economically and socially（对**整个区域**进行经济社会层面的复兴） |
| Formality (English + 中文) | neutral‑formal（中性偏正式） | formal（正式，多用于文物、古迹） | formal（正式，多用于政策、城市规划） |
| Typical collocates (English + 中文) | undergo renovation；renovation project（经历翻新；改造项目） | restoration of a painting / temple；under restoration（画作、寺庙的修复；修复中） | urban regeneration；economic regeneration（城市更新；经济复兴） |
| IELTS example (English + 中文翻译) | The government should invest more in renovating dilapidated housing in urban areas.（政府应投入更多资金翻新城市地区的破旧住房。） | The restoration of the ancient temple took over a decade.（这座古庙的修复耗时十余年。） | Urban regeneration has transformed the former industrial zone.（城市更新改造了这片昔日的工业区。） |
> ⚠️ 常见误用：不要用 `overturn`（推翻、撤销判决）来表达“翻新”，二者语义完全不同。
