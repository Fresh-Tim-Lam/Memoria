# rewarding
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /rɪˈwɔːdɪŋ/   (based on Oxford / Cambridge)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /rɪˈwɔːrdɪŋ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster / Oxford US)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 三音节，重音落在第二音节 **-WARD-**（re·ˈward·ing）；词尾 ‑ing 不重读
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the stress sits in the middle, so the first syllable is weak /rɪ‑/ and the ending /‑ɪŋ/ is barely heard — *rewarding* can sound like *we're ordering* if you lose the /w‑/.
  (中文说明：重音在中段，前后音节都弱读；/rɪˈwɔːdɪŋ/ 别听成 “we're ordering”。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: 课件「Useful language for different situations · POSITIVE OR IMPROVING」（原图 docs/ref/ppt/6778979f225b0b1c897a7d75fcefc3c2.jpg；OCR 见 docs/ref/ppt/ocr.md:79‑87，课件例句 *The experience was challenging but rewarding.*）＋ Oxford / Cambridge / Merriam‑Webster 词典释义

- **[adjective]** (of an experience or activity) worth doing because it makes you feel happy and satisfied（值得做的；有意义的；令人满足的）
  (中文释义：重在**心理回报**——累、难，但做完觉得值；与钱无关。)
- **Speaking example** – Learning to cook for myself was slow at first, but it's been really rewarding.
  (中文翻译：自己学做饭一开始很慢，但真的很有收获。)
- **Writing example** – Volunteering at the community centre has been a rewarding experience for the students involved.
  (中文翻译：在社区中心做志愿工作，对参与其中的学生来说是一段有意义的经历。)

- **[adjective]** (of a job or payment) giving you money or other benefits（报酬丰厚的；有回报的）
  (中文释义：这一支才与钱有关，如 a financially rewarding career（收入可观的职业）；日常说“工资高”更常用 well‑paid / lucrative。)
- **Writing example** – Teaching is not always a financially rewarding career, yet many graduates find it deeply satisfying.
  (中文翻译：教书这份职业收入未必可观，但许多毕业生觉得它带来深深的满足感。)

- **[adjective]** (of a task) giving you useful information or a result（有收获的；值回票价的）
  (中文释义：做某事“没白做”，如 a rewarding exercise / a rewarding discussion（有收获的练习／讨论）。)
- **Writing example** – The survey proved rewarding: it gave us three clear patterns to work with.
  (中文翻译：这次调查很有收获，给了我们三条清晰的规律可用。)

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① 口语 Part 2「难但值得」的经历（学技能、志愿服务、独自生活） ② Part 3 与写作谈教育、工作、志愿服务的**意义** ③ 描述爱好：a rewarding hobby ④ 结论句：hard work, but rewarding ⑤ 不含金钱的满足感（此点最易与「报酬」混淆）
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「工资高／赚钱多」 → 用 `well‑paid` / `lucrative`，或加限定 `financially rewarding`（rewarding 单用只表示「让人满意」，不说钱多）
  - ✗ 想说「好玩、有意思」 → 用 `fun` / `enjoyable`（rewarding 含**付出与收获**的对比，纯娱乐用它属**用词过头**）
  - ✗ 想说「划算、值得买」 → 用 `good value` / `worth the money`（rewarding 修饰**经历**，不修饰价格）
  - ✗ 直接形容人 → rewarding 多修饰**事情／经历**：❌ My teacher is rewarding.／✓ My teacher's lessons are rewarding.
- **强度标记 Strength**: **neutral‑positive**——不是程度强，而是**评价型**：课件把它与 `worthwhile` 并列在 **POSITIVE OR IMPROVING** 组
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 日常 `fun` / `nice` → 通用 **`rewarding` / `worthwhile`** → 正式 `gratifying` / `fulfilling`

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 构词：`reward`（v./n. 报答、奖赏）＋ 形容词后缀 `‑ing` → `rewarding`＝「能带来回报的」
<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：构词与词源说明按通用词典整理，课件未给词源 -->
- **Memory hook**: 拆成 **re‑ward‑ing**＝「**再**（re）**往回**（ward）给你一次回报」——像爬完一段陡坡回头看全景：累是真的，值也是真的。
- 一句话分两层：**心理满足**（难但值得，最常用）→ **有报酬的**（只在这一支才谈钱）。
### High‑Frequency Collocations
| Type | Collocation (English) | Chinese Meaning | Example Sentence (English) | Translation (Chinese) |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| adj+noun | **a rewarding experience / job / career** | 有意义的经历／工作／职业 | Looking after my grandparents was a rewarding experience. | 照顾祖父母是一段有意义的经历。 |
| adj+noun | **a rewarding hobby / activity** | 有收获的爱好／活动 | Gardening is a rewarding hobby if you have the patience. | 如果你有耐心，园艺是个很有收获的爱好。 |
| be+adj+to do | **it is rewarding to do sth** | 做某事令人满足 | It is rewarding to watch your students improve. | 看到学生进步令人满足。 |
| verb+adj | **find sth rewarding** | 觉得某事有意义 | Many volunteers find the work extremely rewarding. | 很多志愿者觉得这份工作极有意义。 |
| adv+adj | **immensely / hugely / deeply rewarding** | 极其有收获的 | Teaching abroad was hugely rewarding for me. | 对我来说，在国外教书收获极大。 |
| adv+adj | **personally / professionally rewarding** | 个人／职业上很有收获的 | The course was personally rewarding, even if it did not lead to a promotion. | 这门课让我个人收获很大，尽管它没带来升职。 |
| adv+adj | **financially rewarding** | 收入可观的（谈钱时才用） | Medicine is demanding but financially rewarding. | 学医很辛苦，但收入可观。 |

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ⚠️ **别把 rewarding 当「有钱」**：rewarding＝满足感；单纯说钱用 `well‑paid` / `lucrative`，或加限定 `financially rewarding`。
- ⚠️ **主语是「事」，不是「人」**：❌ My job is a rewarding person.／✓ My job is rewarding.
- ⚠️ **不用于价格**：❌ The hotel was rewarding for the price.／✓ The hotel was good value for the price.
- ⚠️ **程度副词搭配**：`highly rewarding` 略生硬，常用 `immensely / extremely / really rewarding`。
- ⚠️ **拼写与重音**：❌ rewardning / rewardering；重音不在第一音节（❌ ˈrewarding）。

## ⚔️ Confusion Matrix (with Chinese explanations)
| Dimension | This word: rewarding | Synonym 1: worthwhile | Synonym 2: lucrative |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Core nuance (English + 中文) | 经历**难但值得**，给的是**心理满足**（a rewarding career） | 强调**值不值得花**时间／力气／钱，回报可虚可实（a worthwhile investment） | 只讲**金钱回报**，无情感色彩（a lucrative contract） |
| Formality (English + 中文) | neutral（口语、写作通用） | neutral‑formal（偏书面） | formal / business（商业、正式） |
| Typical collocates (English + 中文) | a rewarding experience, find sth rewarding, immensely rewarding（有意义的经历、觉得某事有意义） | a worthwhile investment, it is worthwhile doing sth（值得的投资、做某事值得） | a lucrative business / contract / deal（赚钱的生意／合同／交易） |
| IELTS example (English + 中文翻译) | Nursing is a rewarding but stressful career.（护理是有意义但高压的职业。） | It is worthwhile spending a year abroad before university.（上大学前在国外待一年是值得的。） | Many graduates choose a lucrative career in finance.（许多毕业生选择金融业的高薪职业。） |

> 🔗 **关联**：`rewarding` 说的是「**难，但值**」——它天生与「难」的词搭档：走过 `a perilous journey`（[[perilous]]）或熬过 `a troubled period`（[[troubled]]），人常收一句 *It was hard, but rewarding.* 要分清「有回报」与「有报酬」：谈薪酬用 `remuneration`（[[remuneration]]）与 `reward` 的名词义，谈满足感才用 rewarding。同属**正面评价组**，可与 `promising`（[[promising]]）、`favourable`（[[favourable]]）一起用于结论句。

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
Looking after my little brother all summer was exhausting, but it was also __________.
（中文提示：整个夏天照顾弟弟累坏了，但也很有收获）
### 2. Speaking scenario
**Describe a skill you learned that was difficult at first.**（口语 Part 2 经历题，可套用 perilous 词卡的 DANGER → ACTION → RESULT 三槽位）
- what the skill was ｜ how you started ｜ what was hard about it ｜ why it turned out to be rewarding
（中文说明：把「难」与「值」成对说出来：*It was frustrating at first, but learning it was immensely rewarding.*）
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 sentence arguing that universities should fund volunteering programmes.
（中文说明：大作文写一句话，论述大学应资助志愿项目）
> 💡 例：Volunteering is a rewarding part of university life: students gain skills that no lecture can teach, while their communities benefit too.（志愿服务是大学生活中有意义的一部分：学生获得课堂教不会的能力，社区也从中受益。）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using "rewarding", [[perilous]] and [[remuneration]].
（中文提示：用 rewarding、perilous、remuneration 合成一句——写一个人放弃高薪、去经历一场凶险却值得的旅程）
### 5. User Output Sample（你的输出 · 订正版）
- **你的原句**: don't be afraid of trying new stuff, hard though experiencing new things is hard but [rewarding]
- **订正版**: Don't be afraid of trying new things: hard though the experience may be, it is ultimately rewarding.
- **要点**:
  1. `hard though ... is` 是**让步倒装**（句式卡片：[[concessive-inversion]]），本身已含「难」——原句又写了一次 `is hard`，重复了；用 `hard though the experience may be` 直接接结果即可。
  2. `[rewarding]` 是形容词，需要主语：补成 `it is ... rewarding`。
  3. 口语里 `stuff` 没问题；写作中换 `new things` / `new experiences` 更稳。
