# secluded
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /sɪˈkluːdɪd/   (based on Oxford Dictionary)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /sɪˈkluːdɪd/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 重音在第二音节 **CLUD** — se‑**CLUD**‑ed
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the first syllable is weak /sɪ/, and the final "-ded" is often reduced to /dɪd/ with a soft second vowel; do not confuse with "secluded" and "secular /ˈsekjələ(r)/".
  (中文说明：首音节弱读为 /sɪ/，词尾 -ded 常弱化为 /dɪd/；注意勿与 secular（世俗的）混淆。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary

- **[adjective]** (of a place) quiet, private and far away from other people（僻静的；幽静的；远离人烟的）
- **Speaking example** – I know a secluded beach where you can hear nothing but the waves.
  （我知道一处僻静的海滩，在那里除了海浪声什么也听不到。）
- **Writing example** – The village remains secluded because no railway or highway reaches it.
  （由于没有铁路或公路通达，这个村庄至今依然偏僻幽静。）

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- Root `clud` / `clus` = to close, to shut（拉丁语 *claudere* = 关闭）
- Prefix `se-` = apart, aside（分开、隔离）；后缀 `-ed` 构成形容词
- 字面含义：**被隔开关闭起来的** → 与外界隔绝的 → 僻静的
- **Memory hook**: A **se**cluded place is **se**aled off from the crowd.
  （secluded 的地方就像被“封闭隔离”在人群之外。）
