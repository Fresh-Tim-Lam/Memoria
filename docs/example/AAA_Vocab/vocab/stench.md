# stench
## 🔉 Pronunciation & Listening Cue
- **英式音标 (British IPA)**: /stentʃ/   (based on Oxford / Cambridge)
- **美式音标 (American IPA)**: /stentʃ/   (based on Merriam‑Webster)
- **重音位置 (Stress)**: 单音节词，全重音 **STENCH**；无重音位移
- **🗣️ 听力陷阱 (Listening trap)**: the opening `st‑` stays one cluster /st/, and the ending `‑ch` is always /tʃ/ — so *stench* rhymes with **bench** /bentʃ/, not with **stretch** /stretʃ/（伸展）or **sting** /stɪŋ/（刺痛）.
  (中文说明：词首 st‑ 连读不拆开，词尾 ‑ch 一定读 /tʃ/；stench 与 bench 押韵，别与 stretch（伸展）、sting（刺痛）听混。)

## 📖 Core Definitions (Dictionary‑Based)
> Source: 用户要点（气味有好坏之分）＋ Oxford / Cambridge / Merriam‑Webster（库内暂无该词语料）

- **[noun, countable, usually singular]** a very strong and unpleasant smell（恶臭；臭气）
  (中文释义：**强烈 + 难闻**两个条件缺一不可——只是一点点怪味，用 `a smell` 就够了。)
- **Speaking example** – There was a terrible stench coming from the dustbins behind the restaurant.
  (中文翻译：餐馆后面的垃圾桶散发出阵阵恶臭。)
- **Writing example** – Residents have complained about the stench from the landfill site on hot days.
  (中文翻译：居民抱怨高温天里垃圾填埋场飘来的恶臭。)

- **[noun, figurative]** a strong feeling that something is dishonest, corrupt or wrong（腐败、不正之事的「臭味」；丑恶气息）
  (中文释义：书面语用法，`the stench of corruption / hypocrisy` ＝ 腐败／虚伪的「臭味」，常见于评论与新闻报道。)
- **Writing example** – The scandal left the stench of corruption hanging over the whole department.
  (中文翻译：这桩丑闻让整个部门笼罩在腐败的阴影里。)

## 🎯 Best Situation & Register Gate（何时该用 / 何时别用）
- **✅ Best situation**: ① **口语 Part 2 地点题**：`the stench from the river`、`the stench of rotting rubbish` ② **气味对比**：`a sweet scent` 对 `a foul stench` ③ **大作文环境话题**：`the stench of untreated waste`、`families living with the stench of the landfill` ④ **引申**（书面）：`the stench of corruption`
- **∇ Avoid**:
  - ✗ 想说「淡淡的怪味／有点难闻」 → 用 `a smell` / `a bit smelly`（stench 是**强烈**恶臭，轻微气味用它属**用词过头**）
  - ✗ 想说好闻 → 用 `scent` / `aroma` / `fragrance`（❌ 不说 a good stench / a pleasant stench）
  - ✗ 把 stench 当动词 → 动词用 `stink`（❌ It stenches. / ✓ It stinks.）
  - ✗ 一般不用复数 → 说 `a stench` / `the stench of ...`（❌ stenchs）
- **强度标记 Strength**: **strong**——比 `smell` / `odour` 强得多，接近 `reek`；一段里用一次即可
- **语域三档 Register ladder**: 委婉 `a smell` / `an odour` → 口语 `smelly` / `it stinks` → 强／描写性 **`a stench` / `reeking`**

## 🧩 Word Family & Collocation Matrix
### Morphology / Root (memory aid)
- 古英语 `stenc`（气味），与 `stink`（发臭）同源<!-- ⚠ 来源待核实：词源说法按通用词源词典整理，未核原书 -->
- 词形变化：复数 **stenches**（少用）｜动词形态借助 `stink – stank – stunk`
- **Memory hook**: 把 stench 拆成 **st** + **ench**——读起来像 **bench**（长椅）。想象夏日公园长椅旁的垃圾桶，那股味儿就是 stench。
- 一句话分两层：**强烈难闻的臭味**（字面，名词）→ **腐败／不正之事的「臭味」**（引申，书面）。

### ⚠️ 常见误用
- ⚠️ **褒贬不可混用**：stench 天生**贬义**，❌ a good stench / a pleasant stench；好闻要说 `scent` / `aroma` / `fragrance`。
- ⚠️ **强度要匹配**：只是「有点味儿」用 `a smell` / `smelly`；用 stench 说轻微气味是**用词过头**。
- ⚠️ **可数与冠词**：常说 `a stench` / `the stench of ...`，一般不用复数（❌ stenchs）。
- ⚠️ **拼写与派生**：stench（❌ stinch）；形容词形式是 `stinking`，不用 `stenching`。

## 🎯 Active Output Drills
### 1. Gap‑fill
Nobody would sit near the bins because of the __________ rising in the midday heat.
（中文提示：没人愿意坐在垃圾桶附近，因为正午的热气里升起一股恶臭）
### 2. Speaking scenario
**Describe a place you visited that had a bad smell.**（口语 Part 2 地点／经历题，按四槽位组织）
- where it was ｜ what you were doing there ｜ what it smelled like and why ｜ how you reacted to it
（中文说明：别只会 `very smelly`——用 `a stench` 定强度，再补来源 `the stench of ...`，最后加一句反应 `I had to cover my nose`。）
### 3. Writing scenario
Write one Task‑2 sentence about how poor waste treatment affects people living nearby.
（中文说明：大作文写一句话，论述垃圾处理不当对周边居民的影响）
> 💡 例：When waste is left untreated, families live with the stench of rotting rubbish and the health risks that come with it.（垃圾不加处理时，家庭只能与腐烂垃圾的恶臭及其带来的健康风险共处。）
### 4. Word‑weaving (advanced)
Make one complex sentence using 'stench', [[dilapidated]] and [[renovation]].
（中文提示：用 stench、dilapidated、renovation 合成一句，例如写河边一片破败厂房翻新前后的对比）
