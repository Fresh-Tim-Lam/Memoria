# Conclusion
> 2 rules
> never write anything new
> one sentence: repeat, summarise

### technique:
**paraphrase the answer that you gave in your introduction**

## Discussion Type(+ opinion)
**Question**: In many cities the use of video cameras in public places is being increased in order to reduce crime, but some people believe that these measures restrict our individual freedom.
Do the benefits of increased security outweigh the drawbacks?

### **Introduction**
It is true that [[\h:blue:blue|video surveillance]] has become commonplace in many cities in recent years. While I understand that critics may see this as an [[\h:green:green|invasion of privacy]], I believe that there are [[\h:blue:blue|more benefits ]]than drawbacks.
### **Conclusion**
**In conclusion**, I would **argue **that the [[\h:blue:blue|advantages ]]of [[\h:blue:blue|using video security systems ]]in public places do outweigh the
[[\h:green:green|disadvantages]].
> shorter oppinion more likely to be the conclusion part

## Opinion Type

**Question**: Families who send their children to private schools should not be required to pay taxes that support the state education system.
To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?

### **Introduction**
Some people believe that [[\h:blue:blue|parents of children who attend private schools]] should not need to [[\h:blue:blue|contribute to state schools through taxes]]. Personally, I [[\h:green:green|completely disagree with this view]].
### **Conclusion**
**In conclusion**, I [[\h:green:green|do not believe]] that any [[\h:blue:blue|financial concessions]] should be made for [[\h:blue:blue|people who choose private education]].
> the answer is restated, not re-argued: `disagree` in the introduction → `do not believe` + `no financial concessions` here.


## Problem and Solution
**Question**: In the developed world, average life expectancy is increasing.
What problems will this cause for individuals and society? Suggest some measures that could be taken to reduce the impact of ageing populations.

### **Introduction**
It is true that [[\h:blue:blue|people in industrialised nations can expect to live longer than ever before]]. Although there will undoubtedly be some [[\h:orange:orange|negative consequences of this trend]], [[\h:green:green|societies can take steps to mitigate these potential problems]].

### **Conclusion**
**In conclusion**, [[\h:green:green|various measures can be taken to tackle the problems]] that are [[\h:orange:orange|certain to arise]] as the [[\h:blue:blue|populations of countries grow older]].
> two questions in the task ⇒ two answers here: the problems answer (`negative consequences of this trend` → `problems that are certain to arise`) and the solutions answer (`take steps to mitigate` → `measures can be taken to tackle`). Nothing new is added.
