# 3 steps essay plan

## Step 1 — Read and understand the question
- highlight / underline key parts

**Question**
Some people think that it is [[\h:red:red|more effective]] for students [[\h:red:red|to study in groups]], while others believe that it is better for them to [[\h:red:red|study alone]].
**Discuss both views** and [[\h:green:green|give your own opinion]].

## Step 2 — Plan your essay structure (4 paragraphs)
1. Introduction: study in groups or alone, sometimes better alone, usually better in a group
2. Benefits of studying alone
3. Benefits of group study (my view)
4. Conclusion: both have benefits, but I prefer group study

## Step 3 — Planning ideas for main paragraphs
- brainstorm, note down any ideas you have
- develop ideas in detail
- keep asking yourself 'why?'
- think of examples to support your ideas
- finally, try to group related ideas (number them)

### Benefits of studying alone
concentrate better, no distractions, focus on the task, read books or articles, study at own pace, e.g. when revising for an exam, memorise information

### Benefits of group study
more ideas, share knowledge, gather more information e.g. research project, learn from each other, more motivating, responsibility to the group, sense of competition
