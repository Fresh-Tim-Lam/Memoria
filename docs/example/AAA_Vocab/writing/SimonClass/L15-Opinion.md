# Opinion
> my view, use "I" "my"

## Step 1 — Read and understand the question
- highlight / underline key parts

**Question**
The money spent by governments on space programmes would be better spent on vital public services such as schools and hospitals.
To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## Step 2 — Plan your essay structure (4 paragraphs)
1. Introduction: government spending — agree — public services instead of space projects
2. explain why 'space' spending should be stopped
3. explain why public service spending is better
4. Conclusion: spend on services that benefit us all

## Step 3 — Planning ideas for main paragraphs
### Why space spending should be stopped
waste of money when projects fail, expensive (scientists, facilities, equipment), no benefit to normal people, politicians showing power, risk of accidents, deaths e.g. Challenger space shuttle

### Why public service spending is better
cheaper e.g. doctors, teachers, police instead of astronauts, public services impact on everyone, reduce poverty, better quality of life, we all use schools, hospitals, police, roads etc.

## Step 4 — Write the essay

### Introduction
> **Plan** — Topic / Answer
> - Topic — government spending
> - Answer — agree, public services instead of space projects

Governments in some countries spend large amounts of money on **space exploration programmes**. I **completely agree** with the idea that these are a **waste of money**, and that the **funds should be allocated to public services**.
> The answer sentence paraphrases the question piece by piece: `money spent by governments` → `the funds`, `better spent on vital public services` → `should be allocated to public services`, and `completely agree` carries the opinion.

### Why space spending should be stopped
> **Plan** — the points below appear one per animation state
> - expensive (scientists, facilities, equipment)
> - waste of money when projects fail, risk of accidents, deaths

**Main body**

**There are several reasons why** space programmes should be **abandoned**. ***Firstly***, it is **extremely expensive** to train **scientists** and other staff involved with space missions, and **facilities and equipment** also come at a **huge cost** to the government. ***Secondly***, these programmes **do not benefit normal people** in our daily lives; they are simply **vanity projects** for politicians. ***Finally***, many missions to space **fail completely**, and the **smallest technological error** can cost astronauts their lives.
> One sentence per plan point, `Firstly / Secondly / Finally`. No `For example` in this paragraph — each reason is expanded inside its own sentence instead, and the `Challenger` example from the idea list was not used.

### Why public service spending is better
> **Plan** — the points below appear one per animation state
> - cheaper e.g. doctors, teachers police instead of astronauts
> - public services impact on everyone
> - we all use schools, hospitals, police, roads etc.
> - reduce poverty, better quality of life

**Main body**

I **believe** that the money from space programmes should go to **vital public services** instead. It is **much cheaper** to train **doctors, teachers, police** and other public service workers than it is to train **astronauts** or the scientists and engineers who work on space exploration projects. **Furthermore**, public servants do jobs that have a **positive impact on every member of society**. **For example**, we all use **schools, hospitals and roads**, and we all need the **security** that the police provide. If governments **reallocated** the money spent on space travel and research, many thousands of people could be **lifted out of poverty** or given a **better quality of life**.
> Four plan points → four sentences, each adding one layer: cheaper → impact → example → result. That is the Idea / Explain / Example shape, so `Furthermore` and `For example` each bring new information instead of repeating.

### Conclusion
> **Plan** — spend on services that benefit us all

**In conclusion**, my **view** is that governments should spend money on **services that benefit all members of society**, and it is **wrong to waste resources** on projects that do not **improve our everyday lives**.
> No new argument: the introduction's answer returns in paraphrase (`agree` → `benefit all members of society` + `wrong to waste resources`), which is the single rule at the top of this file.

> 第一条是同义换称库：题干 `space programmes`（`:8`）在成文里依次换成 `space exploration programmes`（`:31`）、`space missions`（`:41`）、`space travel and research`（`:53`）——同一个东西三种说法，正好躲开照抄题干
> `funds should be allocated`（`:31`）与 `should be abandoned`（`:41`）是两个被动骨架：主语是被处置的东西（钱 / 项目），施动者不出场，是政府花钱题里最省字的一招
> `facilities and equipment come at a huge cost`（`:41`）记的是槽位 `come at a cost`，比 `be expensive` 多一层「钱是这么花出去的」；`can cost astronauts their lives`（`:41`）同理，`cost sb sth` 把「死亡」压成代价而不是事件
> `space shuttle disaster`（图中备选）成文里没用：`:42` 已说明每个理由都在句内展开、不再补例子——存疑时一句一理由，例子留给第三段
> `public servants`（`:53`）与 `a positive impact on every member of society`（`:53`）配对出现：先给人（谁受益），再给面（受益多广）
> 末三条（`:53`）是一条假设 + 累积链：`if governments reallocated … could be lifted out of poverty or given a better quality of life`——情态 `could` 带两个过去分词并列，把「政策不做，这些人就还在原地」写进虚拟语气里
