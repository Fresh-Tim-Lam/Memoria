# Problem and Solution
> one task, two questions: the causes first, then the solutions — both must be answered

## Step 1 — Read and understand the question
- highlight / underline key parts

**Question**
In many countries [[\h:green:green|schools]] have **severe problems** with [[\h:green:green|student behaviour]].
What do you think are the [[\h:red:red|causes]] of this?
What [[\h:red:red|solutions]] can you suggest?

## Step 2 — Plan your essay structure (4 paragraphs)
1. Introduction: student behaviour in schools — variety of reasons, steps can be taken to tackle
2. Causes of bad student behaviour
3. My suggested solutions
4. Conclusion: summarise the problem and steps

## Step 4 — Write the essay



## Introduction

> Plan — Topic: student behaviour in schools
> Plan — Answer: variety of reasons, steps can be taken to tackle the problem

It is true that the behaviour of school pupils in some parts of the world has been **getting worse** in recent years. There are [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|a variety of]] possible reasons for this, but [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|steps can definitely be taken]] to tackle the problem.

> 两句法：第一句 paraphrase 题目（`many countries` / `severe problems` 换成 `in some parts of the world` / `has been getting worse`，语气降一档但问题照认）；第二句把两个问句各答一次 —— `a variety of reasons` 接 causes，`steps can definitely be taken` 接 solutions。

## Causes of bad student behaviour

> Plan — parents / teachers / celebrities

**Main body**

**State 1 — Causes of bad student behaviour（图上蓝色小标题 ＋ 段落主题句）**

In my opinion, three main factors are to blame for the way young people behave at school nowadays.

> `three main factors` 是明码标价：下文就必须正好三条（Firstly / Secondly / Finally）。写这句之前先数得清。

**State 2 — parents not strict (too lenient) (#1)**

Firstly, modern parents tend to be too **lenient** or **permissive**.

> `lenient` / `permissive` 同义并列，是「一个意思两个词」的加固写法，也顺手换掉了题目里的普通说法。

**State 3 — children don't accept teachers' instructions or school rules (#2)**

Many children become accustomed to getting whatever they want, and they find it difficult to accept the demands of teachers or the limits imposed on them by school rules.

> 这里没有另起 Secondly：脑暴 #2 被写成了 #1 的**后果**（父母纵容 → 要什么有什么 → 受不了 demands 和 limits）。四选三就靠这种串接。

**State 4 — teachers' fault, no control, bad classroom management (#3)**

Secondly, if teachers cannot control their students, there must be an issue with the quality of classroom management training or support within schools.

> `there must be an issue with...` 是很好用的因果推测句；同时把脑暴里刺眼的 `teachers' fault` 降级成「培训或支持不到位」——从指责个人改成指向系统。

**State 5 — influence of celebrities who are a bad example (#4)**

Finally, children are influenced by the behaviour of celebrities, many of whom set the example that success can be achieved without finishing school.

> `many of whom` 这种介词前置的定语从句用一次就够。`set the example that...` 把「坏榜样」说到具体 —— 不是 `are a bad example`，而是交代坏在哪儿。

> 全段 5 句：主题句 + Firstly + 后果句 + Secondly + Finally，覆盖脑暴 4 条（#1 与 #2 合并）。这正是 Problem & Solution 与 Discussion 最大的操作差别：脑暴条数 ≠ 正文条数。

## My suggested solutions

> Plan — parents / schools / celebrities

**Main body**

**State 1 — My suggested solutions（图上蓝色小标题 ＋ 段落主题句）**

Student behaviour can certainly be improved.

> 主题句只有五个词，却把整段方向定了：上一段讲「问题有多严重」，这里立刻转成「能改」——`certainly` 与 Introduction 的 `can definitely be taken` 遥相呼应。

**State 2 — parents set rules for children (#1)**

I believe that [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|the change must start with parents]], who need to be persuaded that it is important to set firm rules for their children.

> `who need to be persuaded that...` 非限定定语从句，把「父母该做什么」接在「从父母开始」后面，省下一整句。`start with` 表明顺序最先，正好对上原因段的 #1 也是父母。

**State 3 — use punishments, actions have consequences (#2)**

When children misbehave or break the rules, parents should use **reasonable** punishments to demonstrate that actions have consequences.

> `reasonable` 是这儿的保险栓 —— 不加就等于主张体罚，加了就把范围限定在「合情合理」以内。`demonstrate` 比 `show` 书面。

**State 4 — schools train teachers and parents, discipline techniques, better communication (#3)**

Also, schools could play an important role in training both teachers and parents to use [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|effective disciplinary techniques]], and in improving the communication between both groups.

> 一句里装了两个 `in doing` 并列（training… / improving…），共用 `play an important role in`。`both … and …` 与 `both groups` 在同一句出现两次，指的都是「老师和家长」。

**State 5 — famous people act as role models (#4)**

At the same time, famous people, such as musicians and football players, need to understand the responsibility that they have to [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|act as role models]] to children.

> `such as musicians and football players` 是同位插入语，给 `famous people` 举例 —— Task 2 里最常见的「具体化」手段。`At the same time` 做第三条的过渡，避免 Thirdly / Finally 堆砌。

> 全段 5 句覆盖脑暴 4 条，顺序与原因段一一对应（父母 → 惩罚 → 学校培训 → 名人）。这种**对脏下药**的对位是 Problem & Solution 拿分的关键，也是收尾时不用再回头数「我到底回应了哪几条原因」的原因。

## Conclusion

> Plan — summarise the problem and steps

In conclusion, schools will continue to face **discipline problems** unless [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|parents, teachers and public figures]] **set clear rules** and **demonstrate the right behaviour** themselves.

> 骨架是 `will continue to face X unless Y`：一个 `unless` 就把「问题」和「唯一出路」压进一句，省掉一整句连接词。`parents, teachers and public figures` 正是两个主体段里的三条责任方，`set clear rules` 对父母，`demonstrate the right behaviour` 对老师与名人。

> 结论段只做两件事：把问题重述一遍，把办法重述一遍。不举新例子、不添新原因 —— 课件这句里没有任何一个前文没出现过的信息，这就是标准写法。


## Vocabulary

- steps can be taken to tackle the problem
- three main factors are to blame
- parents tend to be too lenient or permissive
- children become accustomed to
- limits imposed on them
- quality of classroom management
- celebrities, famous people, public figures
- set an example
- set firm rules, reasonable punishments
- play an important role in
- effective disciplinary techniques
- responsibility to act as role models
- face discipline problems

> 这一串按写作顺序排下来就是整篇的骨架：`steps can be taken to tackle the problem` 出自 Introduction 第二句，`three main factors are to blame` 是原因段主题句，最后 `face discipline problems` 回到 Conclusion。

> `be to blame`：主动形式表被动意思，说「责任在谁」，不写 `are blamed`。

> `become accustomed to` 接名词或 `doing`，不接 `to do`；`limits imposed on them` 里的 `impose ... on ...` 变被动后 `on` 不能丢。

> `celebrities, famous people, public figures` 三个词并排等于一个替换库：`celebrities` 偏娱乐明星，`public figures` 最中性，`famous people` 最口语。

> `play an important role in` 后面接 `doing`；`act as role models` 里的 `as` 不能省。

> `set an example` / `set firm rules` / `set clear rules` —— `set` 在这一篇里反复出现，都取「立起来、定下来」的意思。
