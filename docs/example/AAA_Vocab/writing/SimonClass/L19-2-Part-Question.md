# L19-2 Part 2 — Two-part question: news editors / bad news

> **题型**：two-part question。题干 = 一句背景（编辑怎么选题）＋ 三个问号（factors / used to bad news? / more good news better?）。同型解法见 `L11-Part2Introductions.md` 的 "Two-part question" 一节。截图只有文字 ⇒ 只转写、不插图。

## Step 1 — Read the question

**Question**
> News editors decide what to broadcast on television and what to print in newspapers. What factors do you think influence these decisions? Do we become used to bad news, and would it be better if more good news was reported?

- 第一句是**背景**，不是问句：整句可以直接当 paraphrase 的原料，不必自己重新组织（和 L11 的 job satisfaction 一样）。
- 三个问号 = 三个答案：`factors`（开放，答「有很多」）＋ `used to bad news`（是非，答「是」）＋ `more good news better`（是非，答「是」）。

## Step 2 — Plan

> **Plan**（课上原笔记）
> - Topic — decisions about news stories
> - Answer — a variety of factors; yes, too much bad news

## Step 3 — Write the essay

### Introduction

**两句话：第一句换说背景，第二句答后两问**

> [[\h:red:red|It is true that]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|editors]] [[\h:red:red|have to make difficult decisions about which]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|news stories]] [[\h:red:red|they]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|broadcast or publish]], [[\h:red:red|and]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|their choices]] [[\h:red:red|are no doubt influenced by]] [[\h:blue:blue|a variety of factors]]. [[\h:red:red|In my opinion, we are exposed to]] [[\h:blue:blue|too much]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|bad news]], [[\h:red:red|and I would welcome]] [[\h:blue:blue|a greater emphasis on]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|good news]].

**Line-by-line（红 = 框架，黄 = 题干内容，蓝 = 自答）**
- [[\h:red:red|It is true that]] —— 原题没有，加进来的让步框架：先把背景句当公认事实收下，第二句才转去表态。
- [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|News editors decide]] → [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|editors]] [[\h:red:red|have to make difficult decisions about which]] —— `decide` 变名词 `decisions`；`News editors` 只留 `editors`，`news` 挪去修饰宾语。
- [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|what to broadcast on television and what to print in newspapers]] → [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|news stories]] [[\h:red:red|they]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|broadcast or publish]] —— 电视与报纸折成一个动词对，`on television` / `in newspapers` 全省：introduction 不必交代媒介。
- [[\h:red:red|What factors do you think influence]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|these decisions]]? → [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|their choices]] [[\h:red:red|are no doubt influenced by]] [[\h:blue:blue|a variety of factors]] —— 问「哪些」，答「许多」：只说**有 factors**，具体因素留给正文。
- [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|Do we become used to]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|bad news]]? → [[\h:red:red|we are exposed to]] [[\h:blue:blue|too much]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|bad news]] —— `become used to`（已经适应）换成 `be exposed to too much`（被喂得太多），是非问句的「是」折进 `too much`。
- [[\h:red:red|would it be better if]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|more good news was reported]]? → [[\h:red:red|I would welcome]] [[\h:blue:blue|a greater emphasis on]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|good news]] —— 判断题的答案写成 `I would welcome`（我乐见）而非 `it would be better`（更好）：立场一样，但换成个人态度，就不必为「更好」举证。

**Reading the pairs**

> **三问两句**：`factors` 与背景句并进第一句，后两问用 `and` 并进第二句 —— 它们本来就是一根轴（坏新闻过量 → 更看重好新闻）。所以正文也得三个问号各落一处（本课后续段落待补）。

> **两处限定词**：`no doubt`（影响无可置疑）与 `would welcome`（个人偏好）都不把话写死 —— 前者认下背景句，后者只表态、不举证。答案因此是 general 的：`a variety of factors` 不列因素，`greater emphasis` 不写具体做法。

> **最易失手的一处**：`Do we become used to bad news?` 问的不是「坏消息多不多」，而是「人会不会习惯」。成文只用量（`too much`）回答，把「习惯」的机制（`desensitise` 那一层）留给正文 —— 在 introduction 里直接写 `we are desensitised`，正文第二段就无话可说。

> ⚠ 三个问号 ⇒ 正文三处落地（factors / used to bad news / better with more good news）。只答前两个、把第三问漏掉，Task Response 封顶。

> ⚠ 别把 `become used to` 原样搬进成文：它本身是题目的一半，照抄等于没答；换成 `be exposed to too much` 才带上立场。

> 💡 `influence` 的被动式在这里更好用：`their choices are no doubt influenced by a variety of factors` —— 宾语在前，后文每一段刚好展开其中一个 factor。

### Main body 1 — the factors

> 本段只答第一问（`What factors do you think influence these decisions?`）：主题句先收口，再用 `Firstly / Secondly / Finally` 让三个 factor 各占一帧。

**State 1 — variety of factors that influence news editors（图上蓝色小标题 ＋ 段落主题句）**

Editors face [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|a range of considerations]] when deciding what news stories to focus on.

> Introduction 说的是 `a variety of factors`，这里换成 `a range of considerations` —— 同一个意思第二次出现必须换词；`considerations` 还比 `factors` 多一层「他心里要权衡的顾虑」。`when deciding` 又把 intro 的 `decisions` 变回动词。

**State 2 — interest or attract viewers / readers（图上红色小标题 · #1）**

Firstly, I imagine that they have to consider whether viewers or readers will be **interested enough** to choose their television channel or their newspaper [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|over competing providers]].

> `I imagine that` 是给自己留余地的口吻 —— 谈的是编辑脑子里怎么想，谁也拿不出证据，所以整段用推测句。`interested enough` 的 `enough` 才是关键词：光「感兴趣」不够，得「够」到肯换台。`over competing providers` 一句把商业逻辑摆明，全段只这一处提到竞争。

**State 3 — inform the public, important issues and events, in the public interest（图上蓝色小标题 · #2）**

Secondly, news editors have a responsibility to inform the public about important events and issues, and they should therefore **prioritise** stories that are [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|in the public interest]].

> 这一条与上一条正好分站两端：`viewers or readers` 是市场，`responsibility` / `in the public interest` 是职责。`should therefore` 把前因后果压进同一句，不必另起一句解释。`prioritise` 及物，后面直接接宾语、不接介词。

**State 4 — pressure from owners, promote political views（图上红色小标题 · #3）**

Finally, editors are **probably** under [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|some pressure from]] the owners who employ them. For example, a newspaper owner might have particular political views that he or she wants to promote.

> 第三条落到资本。`the owners who employ them` 用定语从句一笔交代「谁给饭吃」，不必解释编辑受雇于人。`For example` 是这一段唯一的举例，且只占一句：三条 factor 里只有最后一条需要落到实处。

**Reading the paragraph**

> **一帧一条，三条就收**：`Firstly / Secondly / Finally` 是固定标号法 —— 第三条不写 `Thirdly` 而写 `Finally`，等于顺手宣告「到此为止」，下一句就进新段落。

> **主题句不报数**：只说 `a range of considerations`，正文写三条、四条都不算失信（对比 L18 的 `three main factors` —— 那种报数写法就必须正好三条）。

> **两条对立轴 ＋ 一条外压**：市场（`viewers or readers`）↔ 职责（`inform the public`），再补资本（`owners`）—— 三条已覆盖一份媒体的全部压力来源，第四条必然与前三条重复。

> 全段 5 句、约 100 词：主题句 1 句 ＋ 三个 factor 各 1–2 句。第一问只值一段，别写长。

### Main body 2 — accustomed to bad news

> 本段一次承接第二、第三问（`Do we become used to bad news` ＋ `would it be better if more good news was reported`）：前三帧答「会习惯」，后两帧顺势转到「所以更该多报好消息」——两问是「既然…所以」的关系，合一段比硬拆两段省一个主题句。

**State 1 — accustomed to bad news（图上蓝色小标题 ＋ 段落主题句）**

It seems to me that people do become accustomed to negative news.

> `It seems to me that` 比 `I think` 留余地；`do` 是强调助动词——题目问 `Do we become used to bad news?`，这个 `do` 一个字就把「是，会」顶回去。`accustomed to` 是 `used to` 的同义替换，避开题干原词。

**State 2 — war, crime, natural disasters, human suffering（图上红色小标题）**

We are [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|exposed on a daily basis to]] stories about war, crime, natural disasters and tragic human suffering around the world.

> 这一句是上句的证据：先用 `on a daily basis` 交代频率，再摆四个名词交代内容。末尾 `tragic` 修饰 `human suffering`，最重的一项放最后。

**State 3 — desensitises us, cynical about the world（图上蓝色小标题）**

I believe that such repeated exposure [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|gradually desensitises]] people, and we become more cynical about the world and more sceptical that we can do anything to change it.

> 全段的机制句。`such repeated exposure` 用 `such` 回指上一句，不必重列 `war, crime...`；`gradually` 交代习惯是慢慢养成的，正好答 `become`。三个词层层递进：`desensitises`（感受变钝）→ `cynical`（对世界失去信任）→ `sceptical that...`（连「能改变」都不信）。注意 `sceptical` 后接 `that` 从句，不接 `about`。

**State 4 — prefer positive news, e.g. medical workers, volunteers, kindness（图上红色小标题）**

I would prefer to see more positive news stories, such as reports of the work of medical staff after a natural disaster, or the kindness of volunteers who help in their communities.

> `I would prefer` 是虚拟式表态，比 `I prefer` 软，正好对上题目的 `would it be better`。`such as` 只带两例：一个「专业救援」（`medical staff`）、一个「普通人善意」（`volunteers`），覆盖好消息的两类来源。

**State 5 — news to inspire us（图上蓝色小标题）**

This kind of news might inspire us all to lead better lives.

> 收段只值一句。`might` 不说满——「可能激励」而非「一定激励」；`inspire sb to do sth` 是固定搭配；`lead better lives` 是 `live` 的书面变体，Simon 在这里选了 `lead`。

**Reading the paragraph**

> **先结论，后机制**：第 1 帧就把第二问答成「会」，第 2–3 帧才解释为什么。别让读者等到段尾才知道你的立场。

> **两个问句可以同段**：第 4–5 帧已经进入第三问。既然麻木了，就更需要好消息——中间只有一个「所以」，硬拆两段要多一个主题句、多耗 20 词。

> **靠两个生词撑分量**：`desensitise` 与 `cynical` 正是本课词表里的两个词（词条已另建）。整段的句式其实很平，读起来有分量全靠这两个词用得准。

## 🔧 可复用骨架

| 槽位 | 本课原句 |
|:---|:---|
| 让步开头（题干是陈述句时） | `It is true that ...` |
| 「因素很多」但不列 | `... are no doubt influenced by a variety of factors` |
| 「太多坏东西」 | `we are exposed to too much ...` |
| 判断题的软表态 | `I would welcome a greater emphasis on ...` |

## Conclusion

> Plan — hard news choices, more positive approach

In conclusion, it must be extremely difficult for editors to choose which news stories to present, but I would like to see [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|a more positive approach]] to this vital public service.

> 骨架是 `it must be extremely difficult for sb to do X, but I would like to see Y`：前半用 `must` 推测，替对方认下难处；后半才表态。`extremely` 加重让步，让后面的 `but` 有力。

> 一句收全篇：`which news stories to present` 回到第一问，`a more positive approach` 回到第二、第三问。不举新例子、不添新理由 —— 这正是结论段不丢分的地方。
