# Agree or Disagree (to what extend, Extra Help)
> don't write the other side of arguement, that will be a discussion essay

> ⚠ 标题的 `extend` 照课件原样保留，题目原文作 `to what extent`。

## 一条指令，四种答法

| # | 题目 | 立场 | 两段正文的分工 |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | space programmes ↔ public services | completely agree | 2 停掉航天开支 → 3 改投公共服务 |
| 2 | hobbies need to be difficult to be enjoyable | balanced（两边都认） | 2 简单的也有意思 → 3 难的也有意思 |
| 3 | consumer societies | partly agree | 2 确实太重钱 → 3 但不是人人如此 |
| 4 | the moon landing | partly agree（换层面） | 2 实用层面没好处 → 3 精神层面有价值 |

> 四种答法的分水岭都在 Introduction 第二句：**completely** 只表一个态；**balanced** 说两边都对；**partly** 先退半步再划线。题目给的立场越绝对，越适合用 `partly` 那一档接住一半、挡掉一半。

## Example 1 — space programmes（completely agree）

> 与 `L15-Opinion.md` 完全同题，那一篇已有 Step 1–4 的完整成文，这里只看开头怎么立。

**Question**
> The money spent by governments on space programmes would be better spent on vital public services such as schools and hospitals.
> To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Introduction**

[[\h:yellow:#b37b02|Governments in some countries spend large amounts of money on space exploration programmes.]] [[\h:red:red|I completely agree with the idea that]] [[\h:blue:blue|these are a waste of money]], [[\h:red:red|and that]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|the funds should be allocated to public services]].

**Plan**

- 2. explain why 'space' spending should be stopped
- 3. explain why public service spending is better
- 4. Conclusion: spend on services that benefit us all

> 题目只说 `would be better spent`（**比较**），作者把它推成 `a waste of money`（**否定**）—— 第 2 段因此不必证明「更好」，只要证明「更差」。completely agree 的常用升级：把比较题答成是非题。

## Example 2 — hobbies（balanced opinion）

**Question**
> Some people believe that hobbies need to be difficult to be enjoyable.
> To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Introduction**

[[\h:yellow:#b37b02|Some hobbies are relatively easy, while others present more of a challenge.]] [[\h:red:red|Personally, I believe that]] [[\h:blue:blue|both types of hobby can be fun]], [[\h:red:red|and I therefore disagree with the statement that]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|hobbies need to be difficult in order to be enjoyable]].

**Plan**

- 2. explain why easy hobbies can be enjoyable
- 3. explain why difficult hobbies can be fun
- 4. Conclusion: disagree that difficult hobbies are better

> `both types of hobby can be fun` 是四篇里唯一的**两边都认**。它否掉的不是「难的没意思」，而是「**非得**难」—— 争点收窄到一个必要条件上，比全面开战好守得多。

> ⚠ 这一版结尾把题目原句抄了回去（`hobbies need to be difficult in order to be enjoyable`，只补了个 `in order`）。句 1 刚换过角度的 `present more of a challenge`，到这里又退回 `difficult`，读起来像题面复制件 —— 下面的四步版把这一步补掉。

### balanced opinion 的四步行文（改进版）

> 按这一题推的排法，与课件 plan 的 2 / 3 顺序相反。

1. **先复述某些人的观点，保住归属**：`Some people believe that hobbies need to be difficult to be enjoyable.`
2. **承认难的那一边**：`I accept that a challenging hobby can be genuinely satisfying.`
3. **否掉「必须」，顺带把简单的那边接住**：`However, easy hobbies can be just as enjoyable.`
4. **正文先解释对方**：Body 1 讲「为什么有人觉得难的才有意思」（挑战感、成就感、进步看得见）；Body 2 开头一句拆掉必要条件 —— `Enjoyment does not depend on difficulty.`

> **为什么这样排更顺**：
> - 句 1 保留 `some people believe`，读者知道这是**别人的**看法，作者到句 2、3 才亮相，落差才成立。课件把归属去掉，句 1 读起来像作者自己在陈述事实。
> - 句 2 先认对方最硬的那条理由，句 3 只拆「必要条件」这一环 —— 不是树稻草人，也只在最小的战场上交锋。
> - Body 1 顺着句 2 的让步讲「为什么大家会这么想」，Body 2 才动刀，正文与开头是**同一根线**；课件先讲简单的乐趣，开头刚承认过的「难也有意思」反而没在正文里落地。

> ⚠ Body 2 开头那句 `Enjoyment does not depend on difficulty` 是**回扣句**，不是新论点 —— 写完它必须立刻举一个「简单却好玩」的具体例子，否则就成了空喊。

## Example 3 — consumer societies（partly agree）

**Question**
> Many people say that we now live in 'consumer societies' where money and possessions are given too much importance.
> To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Introduction**

[[\h:red:red|It is true that]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|many people criticise modern society because it seems to be too materialistic]]. [[\h:red:red|I agree with this to some extent, but]] [[\h:blue:blue|I do not think it is the case that everyone is a victim of consumer culture]].

**Plan**

- 2. I believe many people do focus too much on money
- 3. However, many others are not money oriented
- 4. Conclusion: partly agree

> `It is true that` ＋ `to some extent` ＋ `I do not think it is the case that` 是 partly agree 的三件套：先认题目的现象，再用 `to some extent` **量出**同意的边界，最后把要否掉的那一半原样抬出来再否。

> ⚠ `everyone` 是题目里**没有**的靶子 —— 题目只说 `many people say`，作者自己立了「人人受害」这个极端版。这招能用，但正文必须真把「并非人人如此」论证掉，否则会被看作答非所问。

## Example 4 — the moon landing（partly agree，分层面）

**Question**
> In the last century, the first man to walk on the moon said it was "a giant leap for mankind". However, some people think it has made little difference to our daily lives.
> To what extent do you agree or disagree?

**Introduction**

[[\h:red:red|It is often argued that]] [[\h:yellow:#b37b02|the act of sending a man to the moon has been of no benefit to normal people]]. [[\h:red:red|While I agree that this is true in practical terms,]] [[\h:blue:blue|I believe that the psychological impact of this great achievement should not be underestimated]].

**Plan**

- 2. no benefit in practical terms (standard of living, health)
- 3. but it was an inspiring achievement
- 4. Conclusion: partly agree

> 胜负手是 `in practical terms`：把「没好处」**限定在实用层面**承认下来，于是第 3 段的「有好处」只要换个层面就成立，两段不打架。承认一半不等于输，是给自己圈一块能赢的地。

## 🔧 四种立场的开口句

| 立场 | 骨架 | 出处 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| completely agree | `I completely agree with the idea that ...` | 例 1 |
| balanced | `Personally, I believe that both types of X can be Y, and I therefore disagree with the statement that ...` | 例 2 |
| partly agree | `I agree with this to some extent, but I do not think it is the case that ...` | 例 3 |
| partly agree（分层面） | `While I agree that this is true in practical terms, I believe that ... should not be underestimated` | 例 4 |
| 让步子句（题面是陈述句） | `It is true that ...` / `It is often argued that ...` | 例 3 / 例 4 |

> 最后一行是前三行的地基：题面写成「有些人认为…」的陈述句时，第一句几乎总能收进 `It is true that` 或 `It is often argued that`，把陈述句交还给别人，自己的立场留到第二句。

## 🔁 改写：换主题词，不换近义词

> 四篇 intro 的第一句都在做同一件事，但没有一处走同义词路线。`difficult` → `hard`、`money` → `funds` 这种换皮，考官一眼就看穿；课件走的是另一条 —— **换同一主题场里的另一个词，把观察角度挪一下**。

| 题目原词 | 课件换成 | 换法 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `space programmes` | `space exploration programmes` | 加限定词，从泛指缩到具体 |
| `The money spent by governments on X` | `Governments in some countries spend large amounts of money on X` | 名词化 → 主谓，被动 → 主动，顺带补 `in some countries` |
| `difficult` | `present more of a challenge` | 形容词 → 动词短语；`difficult` 是评价，`challenge` 是可被接受的对象 |
| （题面只有 `difficult`） | `relatively easy` | 反义侧补出来 —— 「必须难」隐含了「简单的不行」，把隐含的那边明说 |
| `money and possessions` / `consumer societies` | `too materialistic` | 具体名词 → 形容词，两句压成一词 |
| `made little difference to our daily lives` | `has been of no benefit to normal people` | 换主体：`daily lives`（生活）→ `normal people`（人） |
| `the first man to walk on the moon said ...` | `the act of sending a man to the moon` | 人 → 行为，事件抽象化，顺手把引语甩掉 |
| `encourage` / `inspiring` | `the psychological impact` | 具体动作 → 抽象范畴（见下节） |

> **规律**：四个方向反复出现 —— ①具体 ↔ 抽象（`possessions` ↔ `materialistic`）②人 ↔ 事（`the first man` ↔ `the act of sending`）③泛指 ↔ 具体（`space` ↔ `space exploration`）④隐含 ↔ 明说（`easy` 从 `difficult` 反推）。挑一个方向挪一次，句子既新、又和题面对得上。

> **为什么比同义词稳**：同义词只有一个正确答案，人人都会；换角度改变的是句子的**信息结构**，能顺带把 `in some countries`、`in practical terms` 这类自己的限定塞进去，改写与立意一步完成。

## 🎯 最难想到的一处：`encourage` → `psychological impact`

> 题目说登月对日常生活没影响，要反驳，最直接的说法是 `it encouraged people` / `it inspired people`。课件没这么写。

- 直接写：`the moon landing encouraged people` —— `encourage` 太轻，而且与 plan 第 3 条的 `an inspiring achievement` 撞车。
- 课件写：`the psychological impact of this great achievement should not be underestimated`。

> 这一步为什么走得通：
> - **换了词性范畴**：`encourage` 是动词（对某人施加的动作），`psychological impact` 是名词（一个可被衡量的量）。名词进了 `should not be underestimated` 这个槽，句子立刻有分量 —— 只有「量」才谈得上被低估。
> - **把结论挂到了层面和方法上**：`psychological` 交代是哪一层，`impact` 交代有多大，两者一起先替第 3 段划好论证范围，正文只剩「`standard of living` / `health` 无收益」与「精神收益」的对照。
> - **留出让步的位置**：正因为前面写了 `in practical terms`，这里才能说「不在那一层，在这一层」。`encourage` 做不到这种分层，它是单层的。

> 同族的 [[cannot-be-overestimated|cannot be overestimated]] 记的正是这个套路（否定翻转：怎么强调都不为过），可以并读。

> ⚠ 这只是**该用的时候**的手法：只有当你要论证的那一层需要一个名字时，才把动词换成范畴。随手把具体词换成抽象词，会让 CC 掉分（读者不知道你在说什么）。
