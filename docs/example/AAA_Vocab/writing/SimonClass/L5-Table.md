## A key skill is 'selecting' which information to mention
## Job: select, describe and compare the key numbers
- 1. look for main or general features (summary / overview paragraph)
- 2. describe specific numbers ('details' paragraphs)
> You can't mention every number: the report used around 10 of the 20 numbers in the table

### Introduction - paraphrase the question
[[\h:#7f1d1d:#fecaca|The table below shows]] [[\h:#fff3cd:#5c4a00|statistics about the top five countries]] [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|for international tourism]] [[\h:#14532d:#a7f3d0|in 2012 and 2013]].

[[\h:#7f1d1d:#fecaca|The table compares]] [[\h:#fff3cd:#5c4a00|the five highest ranking countries]] [[\h:#fff3cd:#5c4a00|in terms of]] [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|the numbers of international visits and the money spent by tourists]] [[\h:#14532d:#a7f3d0|over a period of two years]].
> top five = five highest ranking（同义换壳）
> statistics about → in terms of（同色相接；statistics 整个消失，功能交给后面两个指标），shows → compares 另算
> for international tourism → 拆成两个能数的指标；international 不能丢，丢了把本国游客也算进来
> in 2012 and 2013 = over a period of two years（年份抽象成时段）

### Overview - 2 sentences, 2 main points

It is clear that France was the world's most popular tourist destination in the years 2012 and 2013. However, the USA [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|earned by far]] the most revenue from tourism over the same period.

> 每列谁最高：France 的 visitors 两列第一，USA 的 spending 两列第一

### Details 1 - visitors

In **2012**, **83 million** tourists visited **France**, and the **USA** was the [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|second most visited]] country, with **66.7 million** tourists. 
**Spain and China** each received [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|just under]] **58 million** visitors, while **Italy** was [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|ranked]] **fifth** with **46.4 million** tourists. 
**2013** saw [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|a rise of]] between **1 and 4 million** tourist visits to each country, [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|with the exception of]] **China**, which received **2 million fewer** visitors than in the previous year.

> 趋势在这张表里只有一句的量：五国同向全涨 1-4 million；有升有降、有交叉的趋势才撑得起段落，那是 line graph 的活
> 所以主体是排序（一 France、二 USA、Spain 与 China 并列、五 Italy），排序打完，趋势才用末句打包
> 四国同向就并成一句，只有 China 反向才单独拎出：each / just under / ranked fifth / with the exception of 都是省字的地方

### Details 2 - spending

**Spending by tourists visiting the USA** increased from **$126.2 billion** in **2012** to **$139.6 billion** in **2013**, and these figures were [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|well over twice as high as]] those for any other country. **Spain** received the [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|second highest amounts]] of tourist revenue, rising from **$56.3 billion** to **$60.4 billion**, [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|followed by]] **France, China and Italy**. ***Interestingly***, [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|despite falling numbers of tourists]], Chinese revenue from tourism [[\h:#1e3a8a:#bfdbfe|rose by]] **$1.7 billion** in **2013**.

> 主语从年份换成 spending：前面已经两次用 In 2012 / 2013 起句，再以年份起句就同构；而且这里焦点是钱，不是年
> well over twice as high as those for any other country 一句话比完全表：139.6 是第二名 60.4 的两倍多，差距大到不必报第二名；those for 就是前面的 the figure for，免得 figures 重复
> 末句 Interestingly + despite falling numbers 把两列接起来：visitors 跌而 revenue 涨，全表只有这一处背离，值得单独一句；despite 后直接跟名词短语，是让步的压缩写法

### Tips

**Key skill: selecting information**
- **Let's check to see which numbers were mentioned in the report.**
> 逐个点数：20 个格子，报告实际用到约 10 个。
> - **visitors 2012 五国全报**：83 / 66.7 / 58（Spain 与 China 同值，并成一句）/ 46.4
> - **spending 只报前两名**：126.2 → 139.6（USA）、56.3 → 60.4（Spain）
> - **2013 visitors 一个都不报**：五国压成 `a rise of between 1 and 4 million`；只有 China 反向，单拎 `2 million fewer`
> - **整列被扔掉**：France / China / Italy 的 spending，只留点名 `followed by France, China and Italy`，不报数
> 判断口径：**这个数写出来会改变排序或结论吗？** 不会就删。139.6 已是第二名的两倍多，再报第三名的具体金额零增量。
- Don't use all the numbers
