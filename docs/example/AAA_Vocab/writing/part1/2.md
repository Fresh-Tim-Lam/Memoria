## 🎯 差距诊断：5.5 → 7 只差 4 件事

| # | 差什么 | 对应官方哪条 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **没有独立、清晰的 overview** | 官方 7 档：**"It presents a clear overview"**；硬门限「无 overview → TA ≤ 6」 |
| 2 | **全文零数字** | 官方 6 档："supported using figures/data"；官方 5 档："There may be no data to support the description"。样卷 **1A-A（Band 5）** 就是"有趋势、无数字"的典型 |
| 3 | **写了图表以外的推测与观点** | 官方 Key Assessment Criteria：Task 1 只处理图表的事实内容，**"not to speculative explanations that lie outside the given data"** |
| 4 | **跨国比较不成体系**（只在个别句里点了一下） | 官方 7 档：**"main trends or differences are identified"**；题干也明确要求 "make comparisons where relevant" |

> 语法与词汇的错（时态、`datas`、`indice`、`others goods`）当然也要改，但它们**不是 5.5→7 的瓶颈**——1、2、3、4 才是。这四条改完，TA 从 5 到 7，整体才真正上行。

## 🧱 段落结构：原稿 vs 7 分版

| 段 | 原稿功能 | 7 分版功能 | 为什么这样调整 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 引言（含**推测**：人们"变得更慷慨"） | **改写题干**（是谁/何时/什么指标） | 删掉推测——Task 1 不允许；改写题干是评分第一眼的印象分 |
| — | *（缺）* | **新增：Overview 段**（`Overall, …`） | 这是 5→7 最关键的一步。官方 7 档硬性要求"独立、清晰的 overview" |
| 2 | 法国（**无数字**） | 法国（**补齐 1998/2008 两组数据** + 与他国对照） | 保留你的走位（按国家），只把"没有数"补成"有数" |
| 3 | 德国 + 英国（挤在一段） | **拆成两段**：德国 / 英国 | 原段同时讲两国，导致每国都写不透；拆开后每段能给出完整的数据对照 |
| 4 | `In summary`（复述） | **删除** | Task 1 不需要结论段；overview 已前置，这一段纯属浪费字数 |

> 你也可以选**按图分组**（food 一段 / other goods 一段）。两种都能上 7；本示范**保留你原稿的"按国家"走位**，改动更小、更容易迁移。

## ✍️ 逐段改写

### 第 1 段：引言（改写题干）

- **原稿**：
  > As the graph shows, people in all three countries are spending more money out from there income on both food and other goods after a decade. We could tell that people are changing their opinion and they are more generous about spending money.
- **问题**：
  1. **写了推测与动机**（"changing their opinion"、"more generous"）——官方明令 Task 1 不做数据以外的推测性解释，属**不相关信息**；
  2. 时态错（用现在时写 1998–2008 的过去数据）；
  3. `out from there income` 搭配与拼写错；
  4. 题干是**两张图**，用 `the graph` 不准确；
  5. 关键概念错位：题目给的是**收入占比**，你写成了"花更多钱"（金额）。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > The two charts compare the proportion of income spent on food and on other goods by households in France, Germany and England in 1998 and 2008.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 一句话交代**四要素**：谁（三个国家的家庭）、何时（1998 与 2008）、什么指标（food 与 other goods 的**收入占比**）、何种图（two charts）；
  - 用 `the proportion of income` 精确对应题干，直接堵住"金额 vs 占比"的概念错误；
  - **一个字的观点都不写**。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| As the graph shows | The two charts compare… / The charts illustrate… | 去掉套话开场；两幅图用复数 |
| spending more money out from there income | **the proportion of income spent on** / **the share of income allocated to** | 图表题的高分标配词块，精确度高 |
| We could tell that | It is clear that… / Overall, … | 前者口语且弱，二者是 overview 的标准引导语 |
| they are more generous about spending money | *（整句删除）* | Task 1 不得写观点、态度、动机 |

### 第 2 段：新增 Overview

- **原稿**：*（无此段）*
- **问题**：缺 overview 是最致命的一项——官方 7 档明确要求，且硬门限规定「无 overview → TA ≤ 6」。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > Overall, both categories rose in all three countries over the decade, but the most striking change was in France, which moved from a mid-range position in 1998 to the highest figure in both categories by 2008.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 以 `Overall,` 起句，**一眼可辨**是 overview（阅卷人找的就是这一句）；
  - 包含**两层信息**：① 三国两类的**共同趋势**（都上升）② **最突出的差异**（法国跃居两项第一）。这正对应官方 7 档 "main trends or **differences** are identified"；
  - 不写具体数字（数字留给主体段），保持 overview 凝练——官方样卷 1C-C（8.5）被要求"a **fuller** overview"正是因为 overview 仅列了步数、没有归纳关键阶段。
- **更好的表达**：

| 功能 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| 引出 overview | **Overall,** / **It is clear that** / **The most notable feature is that** |
| 表达"共同趋势" | both categories rose in all three countries / a similar upward pattern is evident across all three |
| 表达"最突出" | the most striking change was… / by far the most dramatic shift occurred in… |

### 第 3 段：法国（补数据 + 对照）

- **原稿**：
  > In different countries perspective, the phnomenon varies. Back in 1998, statics that France people paid on food and goods didn't stand out. When it turned to 2008, datas show that the France won in both indice, what's more, they even kept a balance between food and other goods.
- **问题**：
  1. **一个数字都没有**——这是 TA 卡在 5 的直接原因（官方 5 档原话："There may be no data to support the description"）；
  2. 首句 `the phenomenon varies` 是**空转句**，不承载任何信息，纯占字数；
  3. `statics / datas / indice`（statistics / data / indices）词形错；`France people / the France` 表达错；
  4. 没有与德、英对照——题干要求 "make comparisons"。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > France, by contrast, began the period in the middle of the group, spending around 15% of income on food and only 9% on other goods in 1998. Over the following decade both figures climbed to 40%, so that by 2008 France recorded the highest proportion in either category. It was also the only country where the two categories accounted for an equal share of income.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 你原本观察到的 "didn't stand out" 和 "kept a balance" **两个判断都对**，只是没兑现成数据与正式表达——`didn't stand out` → `in the middle of the group`（并给出 15%/9%）；`kept a balance` → `the only country where the two categories accounted for an equal share of income`（并给出 40%/40%）；
  - 数字**落在句子里**而非单独罗列，满足官方 6 档 "supported using **figures/data**"；
  - `by contrast` 承接 overview，形成段间推进。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| In different countries perspective, the phenomenon varies. | *France, by contrast, began the period in the middle of the group…* | **删空转句**，直接用国家 + 数据开场 |
| didn't stand out | was in the middle of the group / occupied a mid-range position | 口语 → 正式；并强制带出数据 |
| the France won in both indice | recorded the highest proportion in either category | `indice` 词形错；`won` 过于口语 |
| kept a balance between food and other goods | the two categories **accounted for an equal share of** income | "平衡"要用占比语言说清，且给出 40%/40% |

### 第 4 段：德国（从原稿第 3 段拆出）

- **原稿（原第 3 段前半）**：
  > As for the german, they used to spend more on both area, but after slow growth in decade, they became the ones who spend the least on others goods.
- **问题**：
  1. 无数字；
  2. `the german`（应为 Germany / German people）、`both area`、`others goods`、`in decade` 均有误；
  3. 你说德国"成为 other goods 花费最少的"——**方向正确**，但没有数据支撑（17% → 24%，被英国 35% 反超）。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > Germany started as the biggest spender in both categories, allocating roughly 25% of income to food and 17% to other goods in 1998. Its growth was then comparatively modest: food edged up to 30% and other goods to about 24%, so that by 2008 Germany had fallen to the lowest position of the three for spending on other goods.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 用 `started as the biggest spender in both categories` 明确 1998 的地位，再用两个数字落实；
  - `comparatively modest` / `edged up` 表达"缓慢增长"——比 `slow growth` 更准确、更像图表语言；
  - `had fallen to the lowest position of the three` 保留你原稿的正确判断，并补上"被谁反超"所需的对照。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| As for the german, they used to spend more on both area | Germany **started as the biggest spender in both categories** / Germany **led the group on both measures in 1998** |
| after slow growth in decade | its growth was **comparatively modest** / the increase was **far less pronounced** |
| became the ones who spend the least on others goods | **had fallen to the lowest position of the three** for spending on other goods |

### 第 5 段：英国（从原稿第 3 段拆出）

- **原稿（原第 3 段后半）**：
  > Finally, Let's focus on England. People there didn't show any interest in food because they kept spend the least in food for ten years. However, there was a tremendous trend in other goods that the English were getting more love with other goods, as the France do.
- **问题**：
  1. 无数字；
  2. **`didn't show any interest in food` 是推测动机**——Task 1 只能写数据说了什么（"占比最低"），不能写"没兴趣"；
  3. `Let's focus on` 过于口语，属演讲腔而非学术报告；
  4. `kept spend`、`getting more love with`、`as the France do` 均为错误表达。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > England, meanwhile, consistently devoted the smallest share of income to food, at 10% in 1998 and 15% in 2008. Its spending on other goods, however, showed the steepest proportional increase of any country in the charts, climbing from around 8% to 35% over the same period.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - `consistently devoted the smallest share … to food` = 你原意（十年都最低）的**数据化表达**，同时删掉"没兴趣"这一动机推测；
  - `the steepest proportional increase of any country` 是**比较级 + 最高级**叠加的高分写法，直接对应题干 "make comparisons"；
  - 8% → 35% 这个涨幅是全场最戏剧化的单项变化，值得用一句单独呈现（官方 8 档："key features are **skilfully** selected"）。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| Finally, Let's focus on England | **England, meanwhile,** … / **Turning finally to England,** … |
| didn't show any interest in food | **consistently devoted the smallest share of income to food** |
| kept spend the least in food for ten years | **remained the lowest food spender throughout the period** |
| getting more love with other goods / as the France do | **showed the steepest rise of any country** / **as was also the case in France** |

## 📖 全文改写版（连读）

> The two charts compare the proportion of income spent on food and on other goods by households in France, Germany and England in 1998 and 2008.
>
> Overall, both categories rose in all three countries over the decade, but the most striking change was in France, which moved from a mid-range position in 1998 to the highest figure in both categories by 2008.
>
> France, by contrast, began the period in the middle of the group, spending around 15% of income on food and only 9% on other goods in 1998. Over the following decade both figures climbed to 40%, so that by 2008 France recorded the highest proportion in either category. It was also the only country where the two categories accounted for an equal share of income.
>
> Germany started as the biggest spender in both categories, allocating roughly 25% of income to food and 17% to other goods in 1998. Its growth was then comparatively modest: food edged up to 30% and other goods to about 24%, so that by 2008 Germany had fallen to the lowest position of the three for spending on other goods.
>
> England, meanwhile, consistently devoted the smallest share of income to food, at 10% in 1998 and 15% in 2008. Its spending on other goods, however, showed the steepest proportional increase of any country in the charts, climbing from around 8% to 35% over the same period.
