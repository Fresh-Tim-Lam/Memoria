### ✨ 亮点

1. [[\h|it's hard to deny]] —— `it is hard to deny (that) ...` 是高分让步句式，用来承认对方合理之处非常自然；可升级为 `it would be hard to deny that ...`。
2. [[\h|would you feel the same happy as you first experience the things that have left you great memory]] —— 你敢用**修辞性反问**来推进论证，这个策略本身就是 7 分思路；语言虽出错，方向值得保留（改法见错误表）。
3. [[\h|it highly depends on the attitude toward life]] —— 用抽象概括收束一段，观点有哲思；`depend on + 抽象名词` 搭配正确。

### 🔗 连接词分析

1. **`First of all`** —— 可用但偏机械。替换：`To begin with` / `Admittedly` / `On the one hand`。
2. **`However`** —— 使用正确，转折清晰。替换：`That said` / `Even so` / `By contrast`。
3. **`So`（句首）** —— 口语化。替换：`It follows that` / `Hence` / `Consequently`。
4. **`And`（句首）** —— 口语化。替换：`Moreover` / `What is more` / `In addition`。
5. **`In a word`** —— 略显中式/随意。替换：`On balance` / `All things considered` / `Taken as a whole`。
6. **`No matter how much ...`** —— 让步结构使用正确，**保留**；可升级为 `However much people earn`。
