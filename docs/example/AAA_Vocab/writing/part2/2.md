## 🎯 差距诊断：5.5 → 7 只差 4 件事

| # | 差什么 | 对应官方哪条 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **立场不明确、且没有给"程度"**。你写的是 `not quiet supportive … but it's hard to deny as well`，结尾又落在 `helpful but not absolute` —— 等于既不同意也不反对 | 硬门限「`To what extent` 未给程度 → TR ≤ 6」「立场中立 → TR ≤ 6」；官方 5 档："The writer **expresses a position, but the development is not always clear**" |
| 2 | **全篇零具体例证** | 硬门限「主体段无任何例证 → TR ≤ 6」；官方 7 档："Main ideas are extended and **supported**" |
| 3 | **段落功能重叠**：第 4 段基本是第 3 段的复述（都在说"幸福取决于态度"） | 官方 6 档："some main ideas … may be **insufficiently developed or lack clarity**"；第 4 段属**低效篇幅** |
| 4 | **结论没有收束立场**：`I think the idea is helpful but not absolute` 是最典型的"没有结论的结论" | 官方 6 档："the **conclusions drawn may be unclear, unjustified** or repetitive"；硬门限「结论仅机械复述 → CC 降一档」 |

> 你**最值钱的东西已经在了**：用"边际效用递减"（第一次体验很幸福、重复多次就淡了）来质疑"收入均等＝幸福"——这是真正的论证，且**与官方 8.5 档样卷 2A-C 的论证质量同一量级**。你缺的不是想法，是**把想法钉住（立场）＋ 把想法落实（例证）**。

## 🧱 段落结构：原稿 vs 7 分版

| 段 | 原稿功能 | 7 分版功能 | 为什么这样调整 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 表态（**但立场模糊**：`not quiet supportive … but hard to deny`） | **改写题干 + 明确立场与程度**（`I largely disagree … although I accept that extreme inequality is harmful`） | `To what extent` 必须给出**程度**；"承认一面 + 明确反对另一面"是 7 分最稳的开局 |

| 3 | 核心反驳（边际效用递减 + 反问） | **保留为核心反驳段**，补**假设性例证**（护士 vs 高管），并把"attitude"的论点**并入本段** | 反问是好技巧，但官方要的是"ideas **extended and supported**"——反问之后必须有支撑 |

## ✍️ 逐段改写

### 第 1 段：表态（改写题干 + 明确立场与程度）

- **原稿**：
  > I'm not quiet supportive about this condentation, but I think it's hard to deny as well. From my perspective, it's possiblly that normal people would feel in better wealthfare when they are able to reach the most things that the rich have or enjoying. But human demands would never be fulfill and financially equal doesn't doom to bring every one happiness.
- **问题**：
  1. **立场自相矛盾**：`not quiet supportive`（不太支持）＋ `hard to deny`（难以否认）＋ `helpful but not absolute`（结尾）→ 阅卷人找不到你的倾向，直接触发硬门限；
  2. 没有回应 `To what extent` 的**程度**问题；
  3. 自造/拼错词密集：`condentation`（contention?）、`wealthfare`（welfare）、`possiblly`、`fulfill`、`doom to bring`、`huaman`；
  4. `human demands would never be fulfill` 是正确的想法（需求无止境），但放错位置——它是**反驳段的弹药**，不该塞在表态段里。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > It is often claimed that the surest way to make a society happier is to keep the earnings of the richest and the poorest as close as possible. While I accept that extreme inequality does harm the people at the bottom, I **largely disagree** that narrowing the earnings gap is the **best** route to a happier society.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 第 1 句**改写题干**（换词不换义：`ensure the smallest differences` → `keep … as close as possible`；`richest and poorest` 保留）；
  - 第 2 句一次性完成两件事：**让步**（`While I accept that…`）＋ **明确立场与程度**（`I largely disagree`）；
  - 特意点出题目里的 **`best`** 并加以否认——这直接回应"是否是最好的方式"，而不是笼统地"同不同意"，是 `To what extent` 题的高分动作。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm not quiet supportive about this condentation | **I largely disagree with the claim that…** / **I would argue that this is only partly true** | 模糊表态 → 明确程度 |
| but I think it's hard to deny as well | **While I accept that…, I would argue that…** / **Admittedly …, yet …** | 让步与反驳放进同一个句子，立场不打架 |
| it's possiblly that normal people would feel in better wealthfare | ordinary people **are likely to feel better off** / people **may enjoy a greater sense of well-being** | 拼写 + 搭配双修；`feel better off` 比 `feel in better welfare` 地道得多 |
| doesn't doom to bring every one happiness | **does not necessarily make everyone happier** | `doom to` 是错误搭配（doom 表"注定毁灭"） |

### 第 2 段：让步段（保留走位 + 补例证）

- **原稿**：
  > First of all, it is common that people images the promising future and expect to get so, and it's normal to see people complaining about their life and envying the rich. For that reason, minimizing the life quality gap between the poor and rich sounds great, then it's reasonable to make the poorest and richest having a smaller earnings differences.
- **问题**：
  1. **通篇抽象**，没有一个具体场景，因此算不上 7 分要求的 "supported"；
  2. `people images`、`expect to get so`、`having a smaller earnings differences` 语法错；
  3. `First of all` 是**机械模板信号词**（`writing_evaluator.md` 内部校准：`Firstly/Secondly/In conclusion` 全套出现 → 6 分封顶）；
  4. 结论句用 `sounds great`（口语）。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > There is undoubtedly some truth in the argument. Where the difference in earnings is very large, those on lower incomes are constantly reminded of what they lack, and this awareness can breed resentment and a sense of exclusion. Consider two neighbours living on the same street: one can afford holidays abroad and private tuition for her children, while the other works full time yet struggles to pay the rent. In such a case it is not poverty itself but the **visible contrast** that damages well-being, and reducing that contrast is therefore worthwhile in its own right.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 用 `There is undoubtedly some truth in the argument` 代替 `First of all`——**同样起让步作用，但不像模板**；
  - 补进**具体场景**（同街两户邻居），把"people envy the rich"从空泛判断变成**可感的画面**。这正是官方 7 档 "Main ideas are extended and **supported**"；
  - 给出机制句 `it is not poverty itself but the visible contrast that damages well-being`——**it is … that** 强调句，语法分与逻辑分同时加分；
  - `in its own right` 表达"这件事本身就有价值"，比 `sounds great` 精确。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| First of all, it is common that people… | **There is undoubtedly some truth in the argument.** / **It would be naive to deny that…** |
| people complaining about their life and envying the rich | those on lower incomes are **constantly reminded of what they lack** / **a sense of relative deprivation** |
| minimizing the life quality gap … sounds great | reducing that contrast is **worthwhile in its own right** |
| *(无例证)* | **Consider two neighbours …** / **Take the case of someone who …** |

### 第 3 段：核心反驳段（并入原第 4 段 + 补例证）

- **原稿（原第 3 段 + 第 4 段）**：
  > However, thing dosen't go that easy. Think about it, would you feel the same happy as you first experience the things that have left you great memory after it repeat for so many times? The answer is obvious. So it can be conducted that people's happiness mostly don't come from what they have, but what they didn't. And there is no saying that getting richer meand happier. Different people in diferent financial level have thier own troubles in their life, and the rich may be more frustrated than the normal.
  >
  > No matter how much people are earning, they do have their own way and chance to make or enjoying a better life, sometimes, it highly depends on the attitude toward life. Even when faced with a little, some people don't feel happy the way others do.
- **问题**：
  1. 反问句（`would you feel the same happy …`）是**好技巧**，但反问之后只有 `The answer is obvious`——**没有支撑**，7 分要求"extended and supported"；
  2. `it can be conducted`（应为 concluded）、`there is no saying that`（非英语表达）、`dosen't / meand / diferent / thier` 拼写错；
  3. `the rich may be more frustrated than the normal` 是**断言**，没有依据 → 属官方 7 档点名的 "tendency to over-generalise"；
  4. 原第 4 段与第 3 段同义反复，**删段后意见并入**。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > Nevertheless, equalising earnings is unlikely to be the **best** way to achieve happiness, because contentment depends far more on how people live than on how their income compares with other people's. A nurse on a modest salary who has secure housing, close family ties and time to spend with her children may well be happier than a well-paid executive working eighty-hour weeks, even though the gap between their earnings is enormous. What shapes her experience is not the size of that gap but health, relationships, autonomy and a sense of purpose. Moreover, the satisfaction that extra income brings tends to fade: a new car or a larger house is thrilling at first, but soon becomes ordinary, after which people begin to compare themselves with those who have even more. This suggests that closing the earnings gap, on its own, cannot produce lasting happiness.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 开头 `Nevertheless` 承接让步段，同时**再次钉住立场**（`is unlikely to be the best way`）；
  - 用**假设性例证**（护士 vs 高管）把"态度决定幸福"这个抽象主张落地——这是全篇最关键的一步，你的论点从"我这么觉得"变成"可以这样看"；
  - 把**你自己的边际效用洞察**（`the satisfaction that extra income brings tends to fade`）保留为核心论证，并给出具体画面（`a new car or a larger house is thrilling at first, but soon becomes ordinary`）——**这句是你的原创亮点，我只做了语言升级**；
  - 结尾用 `This suggests that …` 收束论证（并非情绪判断），符合 7 分对"extended"的要求。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| However, thing dosen't go that easy. | **Nevertheless, this argument has a serious limitation.** / **Yet the reasoning does not hold in every case.** |
| The answer is obvious. | **This is a familiar pattern, and it is easily observed.** / **The reason is not hard to find.** |
| there is no saying that getting richer meand happier | **there is no guarantee that greater wealth brings greater happiness** |
| No matter how much people are earning, they do have their own way and chance to make or enjoying a better life | **Whatever their income, people have their own ways of building a satisfying life** |
| it highly depends on the attitude toward life | **it often comes down to how they approach life** |
| *(断言 the rich may be more frustrated)* | **may well be happier than …, even though …**（用比较句式代替断言） |

### 第 4 段：结论段（明确收束 + 替代方案）

- **原稿**：
  > It can't be denied that there are still many people are in poverty and scarly able to get enough food in the world, so it's worthy for huaman to help and support the poor in the world. In a word, I think the idea is helpful but not absolute.
- **问题**：
  1. **结论不成立场**：`helpful but not absolute` 等于没有回答 `To what extent`，前文的"largely disagree"到这里被自己抹掉了；
  2. `there are still many people are in poverty` 双谓语；`scarly`（scarcely）、`huaman`（human）、`worthy for` 搭配错；
  3. `In a word` 是模板收尾词；
  4. 突然引入 poverty 是全球议题（前文未铺垫）→ 新论点不得出现在结尾段。
- **改写（目标分版）**：
  > In conclusion, although extreme inequality should certainly be reduced, equalising earnings is not the best means of making society happier. A more effective approach would be to guarantee decent housing, healthcare and education for everyone, since these create the conditions in which people can be content regardless of how much their neighbours earn.
- **为什么这样改**：
  - 用 `although … , … is not the best means` 的句式**把让步与立场一次收干净**，与第 1 段的 "largely disagree" 首尾呼应；
  - **把 poverty 的关切转化成建设性方案**（住房、医疗、教育），而不是删除它——你原稿对弱势者的关怀是真实的，但"该被帮助"不能替代"回答题目"；
  - 结尾 `regardless of how much their neighbours earn` 回扣题目核心（相对收入），形成闭环。
- **更好的表达**：

| 原表达 | 升级表达 |
|---|---|
| In a word, I think the idea is helpful but not absolute. | **On balance, I therefore disagree with the claim as it stands.** / **In conclusion, although …, … is not the best way to …** |
| it's worthy for huaman to help and support the poor | **there is a strong case for supporting those on the lowest incomes** |
| *(结论只否定，不给方案)* | **A more effective approach would be to …** |

## 📖 全文改写版（连读）

> It is often claimed that the surest way to make a society happier is to keep the earnings of the richest and the poorest as close as possible. While I accept that extreme inequality does harm the people at the bottom, I largely disagree that narrowing the earnings gap is the best route to a happier society.
>
> There is undoubtedly some truth in the argument. Where the difference in earnings is very large, those on lower incomes are constantly reminded of what they lack, and this awareness can breed resentment and a sense of exclusion. Consider two neighbours living on the same street: one can afford holidays abroad and private tuition for her children, while the other works full time yet struggles to pay the rent. In such a case it is not poverty itself but the visible contrast that damages well-being, and reducing that contrast is therefore worthwhile in its own right.
>
> Nevertheless, equalising earnings is unlikely to be the best way to achieve happiness, because contentment depends far more on how people live than on how their income compares with other people's. A nurse on a modest salary who has secure housing, close family ties and time to spend with her children may well be happier than a well-paid executive working eighty-hour weeks, even though the gap between their earnings is enormous. What shapes her experience is not the size of that gap but health, relationships, autonomy and a sense of purpose. Moreover, the satisfaction that extra income brings tends to fade: a new car or a larger house is thrilling at first, but soon becomes ordinary, after which people begin to compare themselves with those who have even more. This suggests that closing the earnings gap, on its own, cannot produce lasting happiness.
>
> In conclusion, although extreme inequality should certainly be reduced, equalising earnings is not the best means of making society happier. A more effective approach would be to guarantee decent housing, healthcare and education for everyone, since these create the conditions in which people can be content regardless of how much their neighbours earn.
