### 🔗 连接词分析

1. **`Firstly`** —— 可用但偏机械。这篇没有 `Secondly` / `Thirdly` 全套，所以不触发"6分封顶"硬门限，但依然可以升级。替换：`To begin with` / `On the one hand` / `The most obvious benefit is that …`。
2. **`In addition`** —— 表递进，使用正确。但在经济段只有两句话时，用句内衔接（`which means that …`、`thereby creating …`）比分段用 `In addition` 更紧凑。替换：`Furthermore` / `What is more` / 句内 `thereby + doing`。
3. **`Admittedly`** —— **亮点连接词**！用在让步段开头非常地道，比 `It is true that` 还简洁，且不像模板。可保留；替换：`There is no denying that …` / `It cannot be denied that …`。
4. **`What's more`** —— 口语化缩写（`What's`），写作中用完整形式或正式词。替换：`Moreover` / `Furthermore` / `Beyond this`。
5. **`However`** —— 转折清晰，使用正确。替换：`Nevertheless` / `That said` / `Even so`。
6. **`Moreover`** —— 递进正确。替换：`Furthermore` / `What is more` / `In addition to this`。
7. **`In a word`** —— 偏中式英语/口语化，正式写作中少见。替换：`On balance` / `All things considered` / `Taken as a whole` / `In conclusion`。

### 📖 7 分范文 / Full Rewrite

> It is often argued that the growth of international tourism causes more problems than benefits, particularly for local people and the environment. While I accept that there are valid concerns, I believe the advantages of global travel outweigh the drawbacks.
>
> The most obvious advantage of international tourism is the economic boost it provides to destinations. Visitors spend money on accommodation, food, transport and souvenirs, which directly supports local businesses and creates jobs in hotels, restaurants and tour companies. The income generated can then be invested in schools, hospitals and infrastructure, thereby raising the overall standard of living for residents.
>
> Admittedly, international tourism does have negative impacts. On the environmental side, popular destinations often suffer from overcrowding, pollution and damage to natural habitats. Consider a fragile coastal resort: large numbers of visitors can harm coral reefs through careless boating and diving, and produce tons of plastic waste that ends up in the ocean. Culturally, too, the influx of tourists can lead to tensions. Visitors who are unfamiliar with local norms may inadvertently cause offence, for instance by dressing inappropriately at religious sites or failing to respect local etiquette such as queueing conventions.
>
> That said, these problems are not unavoidable. On the cultural front, far from creating conflict, contact between visitors and hosts actually promotes mutual understanding. Take the case of tourists staying in a rural village in Southeast Asia: by sharing meals, participating in craft workshops and observing local ceremonies, they gain a genuine appreciation of traditions that they could never learn from a guidebook, and local people in turn learn about the perspectives of visitors from other countries. Environmentally, too, careful management can reduce the harm: limiting visitor numbers in fragile areas, enforcing waste-disposal rules and educating tourists about responsible travel can ensure that tourism does not destroy the very places people come to see.
>
> In conclusion, although international tourism brings real challenges for the environment and for local communities, the economic opportunities and cross-cultural understanding it creates are more significant. When managed responsibly, the advantages clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
