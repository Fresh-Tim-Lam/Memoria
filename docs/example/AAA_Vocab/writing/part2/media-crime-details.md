## 📋 题目 / Task

**You should spend about 40 minutes on this task. Write at least 250 words.**

Write about the following topic:

Detailed descriptions of crimes in newspapers and TV can have bad consequences on society, so this kind of information should be restricted in the media.

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

## ✍️ 我的作答 / My Answer
There are worries about the negetive social effect of overly detailed crimes report, While it is reasonable to avoid posting criminal incident too specifically, I think the public have the right to know how is the thing going.
On the one hand, I have to admit that there are some bad effects, if the press depicts the crime in too much detail. Firstly, children watches TV as the grown up as well. When there is no guidence, and they know too much detail about commiting a crime, they might mimic as they don't know how to judge. Morever, some part of the incident might scare children and leave them a shade, because they are not psychologically 
 matured. Finally, someone who related to the crime, such as a man who witnessed the crime, the family and friends around the victim, when they are reported, they probablly get some discussion or judgment. That's why, I disagree with overly picturing the crime.
On the other hand, it will be unrealistic to hide the process of the crime. The public needs to know the truth. For example, the justice of the sentence will be doubt if there is no information about the incident, it might spark concern about the clarity. In addition, there is an alarm effect on the society, detering some people from committing a crime. As a result, to reassure the citizens, improve the order of the coummunity, and prove the authority of the countrie's legal system, part of the crime should be known by the residences
In a word, approches should be taken only to  partly limit the release of information about the crime.

### ✨ 亮点

1. **让步 + 立场的骨架是对的**：`While it is reasonable to avoid ... too specifically, I think ...` —— 先承认对方合理，再给自己的立场，这正是 To what extent 题最稳的开局走位，比一上来就说 "I agree" 高一个层次。
2. **`The public needs to know the truth.`** —— 敢用一个短句独立承担论点，句子干净、有力。很多考生不敢用短句，结果堆出一串自己控制不住的长从句。
3. **你抓到了这篇最值钱的一层**：`there is an alarm effect on the society, detering some people from committing a crime` —— 「详细报道有威慑价值」是大多数考生会漏掉的反面论证，思路本身是 7 分级的。词用错了（`alarm` 应为 `deterrent`），但论证方向正确。
4. **三个目的并列的句型**：`to reassure the citizens, improve the order of the coummunity, and prove the authority of the countrie's legal system` —— 有意做排比，比只写 "because it is good for society" 具体得多。
5. **意象意识**：`leave them a shade` —— 你想用「留下阴影」这个画面，方向是对的（英语是 `leave them with a lasting shadow`）。

### 🔗 连接词分析

1. **`On the one hand / On the other hand`** —— 用得对，但「先让步后主张」时这两个词会让读者以为你在做两面并列。更清楚的做法是用 `Admittedly / There is some truth in ...` 起让步段，用 `Nevertheless / Yet` 起主张段。
2. **`Firstly / Morever / Finally`** —— 一套初级序列词，官方 5.5 档考官原话点名的正是 `some mechanical use of linkers`。替换：`To begin with` / `A further concern is` / `Most seriously`（最后一个尤其适合本段收尾，因为你最后一条确实是危害最重的一条）。—— 这三个不是随手挑的替换词，而是一条**强度阶梯**（由轻到重排列，让连接词自己标注严重性）：用法、边界与对照面已单独收录，见 [[enumeration-linkers]] 的「🪜 强度阶梯」一节。
3. **`That's why,`** —— 口语缩略 + 标点错（应为 `That is why`）。替换：`This is precisely why ...` / `It follows that ...`。
4. **`As a result, to ..., ..., ..., part of the crime should be known`** —— `As a result` 后塞了三个目的状语，主语被推到句尾，读起来绕。替换：`For all these reasons, ...`，或先用分号列三项再用 `which is why ...`。
5. **`In a word`** —— 中式直译（总之），英语不用它收尾。替换：`On balance` / `All things considered` / `In conclusion`。
6. **`In addition`** —— 正确但基础。替换：`Beyond this` / `What is more`。
