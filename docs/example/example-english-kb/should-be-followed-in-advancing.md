## should be followed in advancing sth
完整句式结构：[A principle] should be followed in advancing B
核心含义：在推进B这件事的过程中，应当遵循A原则。
1. 结构拆解
   - should be followed：被动语态，意为应当被遵循、应予恪守，主语通常为principle、rule等原则类名词，是外交书面高频表达
   - in advancing sth：in + 动名词结构，意为在做某事的过程中，advance为及物动词，表示推进、推动
2. 原文例句
   - The principle of Palestinians governing Palestine should be followed in advancing the governance of the West Bank and postwar reconstruction of Gaza.
   - 翻译：在推进约旦河西岸治理以及加沙战后重建工作时，应当恪守巴勒斯坦人治理巴勒斯坦这一原则。
3. 同义改写
   - The principle shall be observed in advancing sth.
   - In the course of advancing sth, this principle should be upheld.
